**连锁故事**

Late at night, a researcher named Leo had to **confront** (使面临) a difficult choice: whether to trust his own **assumption** (假定) about the data. He believed in **equality** (同等) among all test groups, but his **glass** (玻璃杯) of coffee was empty, and the lab felt cold. He decided to **entitle** (给……题名) his new paper "The Silent Shift," a **prompt** (敏捷的) decision that surprised even him. Yet he began to **suspect** (怀疑) that his colleague had tampered with the results, and his **spirit** (精神) wavered. Leo was no **hero** (英雄), but he refused to let **wild** (野生的) speculation ruin his work. He flexed his **muscle** (肌肉) of will and tried to **beat** (击败) his own doubt. At the **peak** (最高点) of his frustration, he raised his **arm** (手臂) and nearly threw the laptop. But he stopped himself, letting his anger **shrink** (减少) like a deflating balloon. He realized this was a **transition** (过渡) point in his career. With **excellent** (优秀的) discipline, he decided to **delete** (删除) all false records, and he confronted the **racial** (种族的) bias hidden in the old methodology. The whole mess continued to **puzzle** (使……困惑) him, but he knew the answer lay in clarity, not chaos.
