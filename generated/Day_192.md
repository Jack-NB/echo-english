**连锁故事**

In a remote village, the villagers tried to **erase** (抹去) every trace of a **fearful** (可怕的) memory: the **plague** (瘟疫) that had once swept through their homes. One night, a mysterious **glow** (光辉) rose from the ancient **holy** (神圣的) well, promising an **inclusive** (在内的、包罗广泛的) cure for all. An old woman held a **jar** (罐子) filled with herbs, her **outward** (向外的) calm hiding her inner dread. She knew the disease could **refute** (反驳、驳斥) all medical logic, and no **spontaneous** (自然产生的) recovery was possible. She decided to **adhere** (坚持) to the ancient ritual, mixing **aluminum** (铝) powder with the herbs to stop the wounds from **bleeding** (流血). Meanwhile, her grandson flipped through a dusty **catalog** (目录) of old remedies, hoping to find a formula that could make the volcano **erupt** (爆发) with healing steam instead of **explosive** (炸药) ash. The village’s fortunes began to **fluctuate** (波动) wildly as the moon changed. They wrapped the sick in warm **fur** (毛皮) and cleaned their **gut** (内脏、肠子) with bitter tea, praying the ritual would **heal** (治愈) them all. By dawn, the glow faded, but the **glowing** (容光焕发的) faces of the cured proved that hope had returned.

**中文翻译**

在一个偏远的村庄里，村民们试图**抹去**一段**可怕的**记忆：曾经席卷他们家园的**瘟疫**。一天夜里，古老的**神圣**水井升起一道神秘的**光辉**，许诺一种**包罗广泛的**疗法。一位老妇人捧着一个**罐子**，里面装满了草药，她**向外的**平静掩盖了内心的恐惧。她知道这种疾病能**反驳**一切医学逻辑，没有**自然产生的**痊愈可能。她决定**坚持**古老的仪式，将**铝**粉混入草药中，以止住伤口的**流血**。与此同时，她的孙子翻着一本满是灰尘的**目录**，希望能找到一种配方，让火山**爆发**出治愈的蒸汽，而不是**炸药**般的灰烬。村庄的命运随着月相剧烈**波动**。他们用温暖的**毛皮**包裹病人，用苦茶清洗他们的**内脏**，祈祷仪式能**治愈**所有人。黎明时分，光辉褪去，但痊愈者**容光焕发的**面容证明，希望已经归来。
