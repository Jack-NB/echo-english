**连锁故事**

On a **rainy** Friday, Tom decided to **reclaim** (回收、利用) his old bicycle from the shed. The metal frame was covered in **rust** (生锈), and the **ridge** (脊) of the seat had worn thin. He wiped off the **sauce** (酱汁) stains from last week's picnic, then used a **strap** (皮带) to tie the basket. His younger **sibling** (兄弟姐妹) came out and gave him a playful **slap** (掴、侮辱) on the back, laughing at his **stale** (陈腐的、不新鲜的) jokes about the weather.

Tom ignored the **repression** (镇压) of his childhood memories—their father had been too strict. Now he wanted to **submerge** (潜入水中、浸没) himself in a new project. After a quick **supper** (晚餐), he adjusted the handlebars with a slight **tilt** (倾斜), then carefully **trim** (修剪、装饰) the old wires. He checked the **trademark** (商标) on the hub—still original. As a **typist** (打字员) in a law firm, he knew the **venue** (审判地、犯罪地点) for tomorrow's hearing was across town. He was **versatile** (多才多艺的、通用的) enough to fix bikes, cook, and draft legal documents.

His **yearly** (每年的) resolution was to practice **thrift** (节俭). He wore a **necklace** (项链) made of old keys, a gift from his late mother. The bike, though old, was his pride. He tightened every **strap** again, and the wheels spun freely. Tomorrow, he would ride it to work, saving money and feeling alive.

**中文翻译**

在一个多雨的星期五，汤姆决定回收利用（reclaim）棚里的旧自行车。金属车架生锈了（rust），座垫的脊（ridge）已经磨薄。他擦掉上周野餐留下的酱汁（sauce）污渍，然后用一条皮带（strap）绑好车篮。他的弟弟/妹妹（sibling）走出来，在他背上玩笑地掴了一下（slap），嘲笑他那些关于天气的陈腐（stale）笑话。

汤姆压抑着（repression）童年的记忆——父亲当年太严厉了。现在他想让自己沉浸（submerge）在一个新项目里。快速吃完晚餐（supper）后，他调整了车把，微微倾斜（tilt）角度，然后小心地修剪（trim）旧电线。他检查了轮毂上的商标（trademark）——还是原装的。作为一名律师事务所的打字员（typist），他知道明天听证会的审判地（venue）在城那头。他足够多才多艺（versatile），会修车、做饭，还能起草法律文件。

他每年（yearly）的决议就是践行节俭（thrift）。他戴着一条用旧钥匙做的项链（necklace），那是已故母亲送的礼物。自行车虽然旧，却是他的骄傲。他把每根皮带（strap）又紧了紧，车轮自由旋转。明天，他要骑车上班，省钱，也感受活着的气息。
