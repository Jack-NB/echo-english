**连锁故事 (English):**

The **instrumental** (仪器的) discovery of a hidden **keyboard** (键盘) inside an old **latitude** (纬度) measurement device changed everything. The device, used by sailors, had a **liquor** (酒) compartment that spilled **manuscript** (手稿) pages—ancient notes written by a mad cartographer. He had designed a **plough** (犁) that could dig through ice, and his **suite** (套房) of drawings was **superb** (极好的). But the notes described a **torture** (拷问) machine meant to extract secrets from prisoners, and a daily **workout** (锻炼) regimen for guards. With the **advent** (出现) of this discovery, a government **audit** (审计) was launched. The **blaze** (火焰、燃烧) of public anger was **brutal** (野蛮的), and reporters found a **bug** (窃听器) planted in the archive. They also found a **bull** (公牛) statue with a hidden chamber—proof of a **conspiracy** (阴谋) to fake the documents. The minister refused to **endorse** (支持) the findings, but he had to **expend** (花费) millions to cover up the scandal. In the end, the only honest thing left was a bag of **flour** (面粉) on the table, used to dust the fingerprints of the real culprit.

**中文翻译：**

一台古老的纬度测量仪器中，隐藏键盘的**仪器性的**（有帮助的）发现改变了一切。这台曾被水手使用的仪器里，有一个**酒**（liquor）隔层，洒出了一叠**手稿**（manuscript）——一位疯狂制图师写下的古代笔记。他设计了一种能破冰的**犁**（plough），他的一**套房**（suite）图纸堪称**极好**（superb）。但笔记描述了一台用于从囚犯口中逼供的**拷问**（torture）机器，以及守卫们的每日**锻炼**（workout）计划。随着这一发现的**出现**（advent），政府启动了一项**审计**（audit）。公众愤怒的**火焰**（blaze）异常**野蛮**（brutal），记者们在档案馆里发现了一个**窃听器**（bug）。他们还发现了一尊**公牛**（bull）雕像，内部藏有暗格——这正是伪造文件的**阴谋**（conspiracy）证据。部长拒绝**支持**（endorse）调查结果，但他不得不**花费**（expend）数百万来掩盖丑闻。最后，桌上唯一诚实的东西只剩下一袋**面粉**（flour），它被用来提取真正罪犯的指纹。
