**The Chain Story**

After a careful **analysis** (分析) of the village’s food security, the young **citizen** (公民) named Leo drew a rough **outline** (轮廓) of a plan. He wanted to use an ancient **code** (代码) from his grandfather’s notebook, which described a way to boost the **strength** (力量) of local **crops** (作物). He decided to **display** (陈列) his idea at the town meeting, but first he needed a **budget** (预算). Leo was **smart** (聪明的) enough to know that every **whom** (谁) he asked for help had a different **manner** (方式) of thinking. He began to **explore** (探索) the old library, where he found a dusty map showing a **launch** (发射) site for a weather balloon. That balloon could carry sensors to **estimate** (估计) rainfall, which was vital for **agriculture** (农业). His **advanced** (高级的) plan impressed the mayor, who gave him the funds. Leo’s father, a farmer, taught him that the **sun** (太阳) and his **son** (儿子) were the two most important forces in life. So Leo worked day and night, **whatever** (无论什么) the difficulty. He even created a new **brand** (品牌) of organic fertilizer that doubled the yield. In the end, the village’s harvest was the richest in decades, and Leo was praised for turning a simple **outline** (提纲) into a living success. His **analysis** (分析) of the data proved that even a small community could achieve great things with **strength** (力量) and unity.

**中文翻译**

经过对村庄粮食安全的仔细分析，年轻公民利奥画出了一个计划的粗略轮廓。他想用祖父笔记本里的一段古老代码，其中描述了一种增强当地作物力量的方法。他决定在镇会上展示他的想法，但首先他需要一份预算。利奥很聪明，他知道他求助的每一个人都有不同的思考方式。他开始探索旧图书馆，在那里他发现了一张布满灰尘的地图，上面标有一个气象气球的发射点。那个气球可以携带传感器来估计降雨量，这对农业至关重要。他高级的计划给市长留下了深刻印象，市长给了他资金。利奥的父亲是个农民，他教导利奥，太阳和他的儿子是生命中最重要的两股力量。所以利奥日夜工作，无论遇到什么困难。他甚至创造了一个新的有机肥料品牌，使产量翻倍。最后，村庄的收成是几十年来最丰收的，利奥因将一个简单的提纲变成了活生生的成功而受到赞扬。他对数据的分析证明，即使是一个小社区也能通过力量和团结取得伟大的成就。
