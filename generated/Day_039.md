In a tiny village, an old **farmer** (农民) named Tom had a **hundred** (百) sheep. He lived a **simple** (简单的) life, but he was **successful** (成功的) because his wool sold for a **thousand** (一千) coins each season. Tom believed in a **theory** (理论): hard work is the **key** (钥匙) to happiness. Every morning, he looked at his own **image** (形象) in the river and smiled. His wife and he were a perfect **couple** (情侣), and **whose** (谁的) advice he followed? Hers. One day, a wolf tried to **catch** (抓住) a lamb. Tom had to **select** (选择) a plan. He grabbed a **card** (卡片) from his pocket—it was a map. He used a cloth to **cover** (覆盖) the trap, hoping to **earn** (挣得) the wolf’s trust. The wolf stepped on it and fell. Tom’s **earnings** (**收入**) grew, and he became a **trendsetter** (**潮流引领者**) in the village, teaching others his **methodical** (**有条理的**) farming **method** (方法). His **promising** (**有前途的**) future **attracted** (**吸引**) many visitors. They saw his **clean** (**干净的**) fields and **uncovered** (**揭露**) the secret: discipline. Tom made a **promise** (承诺) to share his knowledge, and his **imagination** (**想象力**) sparked a **theoretical** (**理论的**) revolution in local agriculture. Soon, **thousands** (**数以千计**) of farmers followed his **trend** (趋势), and his name became **catchy** (**易记的**) everywhere.

**中文翻译**

在一个小村庄里，一位老**农民**（farmer）名叫汤姆，他有一**百**（hundred）只羊。他过着**简单**（simple）的生活，但他很**成功**（successful），因为他的羊毛每季能卖出一**千**（thousand）枚硬币。汤姆相信一个**理论**（theory）：努力工作是幸福的**关键**（key）。每天早晨，他看着自己在河中的**形象**（image）微笑。他和妻子是一对完美的**情侣**（couple），而**谁的**（whose）建议他会听从？她的。有一天，一只狼试图**抓住**（catch）一只羊羔。汤姆必须**选择**（select）一个计划。他从口袋里掏出一张**卡片**（card）——那是一张地图。他用布**覆盖**（cover）了陷阱，希望能**挣得**（earn）狼的信任。狼踩了上去，摔了进去。汤姆的**收入**（earnings）增长了，他成了村里的**潮流引领者**（trendsetter），教大家他**有条理的**（methodical）农耕**方法**（method）。他**有前途的**（promising）未来**吸引**（attract）了许多访客。他们看到他**干净的**（clean）田野，并**揭露**（uncover）了秘密：纪律。汤姆做出**承诺**（promise）分享他的知识，他的**想象力**（imagination）激发了当地农业的**理论**（theoretical）革命。很快，**数以千计**（thousands）的农民跟随他的**趋势**（trend），他的名字变得**易记**（catchy）起来。
