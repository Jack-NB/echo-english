**The Lamp, the Map, and the Arrogant Engineer**

In a dusty study, Tom stared at an **illustration** (插图、说明) of a strange machine in an old book. It looked almost **imaginary** (虚构的), but the **marginal** (微小的、边际的) notes beside it were precise. His **obsession** (困扰) with the design had grown over years, though his colleagues called it an **offensive** (冒犯的、进攻性的) waste of time.

Tom's only clue was a bottle of **petroleum** (石油) found near a forgotten **port** (港口). He would **ponder** (考虑) for hours, tracing the lines on the map. He knew the machine could **safeguard** (保护) the city's failing power grid. The symbols on the page seemed to **signify** (表示、意味着) a hidden energy source. Could he **simulate** (模拟、冒充) its operation without the real parts? He imagined a perfect metal **sphere** (球) rotating inside a **transparent** (透明的) casing, powered by a tiny **thumb** (拇指)-sized reactor.

He opened his **album** (相册) of old photographs, hoping to **analyse** (分析) every detail of the prototype. But the choice of materials seemed **arbitrary** (任意的) — no logic, no pattern. A former mentor, an **arrogant** (傲慢的) genius, had once laughed at his ideas and thrown a **candle** (蜡烛) into the **closet** (壁橱), locking away the only full schematic.

That night, Tom lit the same candle, pulled the map from the closet, and finally saw the truth: the **illustration** was a mirror of his own mind. The **imaginary** machine was real — it was the act of seeking itself. And in that moment, the **petroleum** in the bottle glowed, as if the earth itself had been waiting for his **ponder** to end.

**中文翻译**

在积满灰尘的书房里，汤姆盯着一本旧书中一台奇怪机器的插图。它看起来几乎是虚构的，但旁边的页边注释却十分精确。多年来，他对这个设计的痴迷与日俱增，尽管同事们称之为一种冒犯性的浪费时间。

汤姆唯一的线索是在一个被遗忘的港口附近发现的一瓶石油。他会沉思数小时，描摹着地图上的线条。他知道这台机器能保护城市日益崩溃的电网。书页上的符号似乎意味着一种隐藏的能源。他能否在没有真实零件的情况下模拟它的运转？他想象着一个完美的金属球体在透明外壳内旋转，由一颗拇指大小的反应堆驱动。

他翻开旧照片相册，希望能分析原型机的每一个细节。但材料的选择似乎很随意——没有逻辑，没有规律。一位前导师，一个傲慢的天才，曾嘲笑他的想法，把一根蜡烛扔进壁橱，锁上了唯一一张完整的原理图。

那天夜里，汤姆点燃了同一根蜡烛，从壁橱里取出地图，终于看到了真相：那幅插图是他自己心灵的映照。那台虚构的机器是真实的——它就是求索本身。而在那一刻，瓶中的石油发出光芒，仿佛大地一直在等待他沉思的终结。
