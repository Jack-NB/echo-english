**连锁故事 (英文)**

The old **electrician** (电工) was fixing a faulty wire near the **equator** (赤道) when a sudden **eclipse** (丧失、形成日或月食) of the sun plunged the jungle into eerie darkness. He wiped his **forehead** (前额) and felt a wave of **fright** (惊恐) — the **gang** (一伙) of poachers nearby had just spotted his toolbox. He grabbed a clove of **garlic** (大蒜) from his pocket, a silly superstition, but it helped him **generalise** (概括) a plan: escape to the village. Running along the **fringe** (边缘) of the swamp, he recalled his **encyclopedia** (百科全书) of survival skills. He wanted to **enquire** (询问) the locals about the strange lights, but there was no time. He thought of the **electron** (电子) flow in his repair kit — if he could short-circuit the fence, the poachers would be stuck. His movements were **graceful** (优雅的) despite the mud. He knew that **harassment** (骚扰) from such criminals would not end here; **henceforth** (今后) he would carry a **kettle** (水壶) of boiling water as a weapon. He felt like an **idiot** (白痴) for not calling for backup, but he decided to **inaugurate** (开创、开始) a new tactic: lure them into the abandoned mine shaft. For a moment, he imagined **intercourse** (性交) between the two rival gangs would distract each other, but that was absurd. He spotted a piece of **ivory** (象牙) stuck in the mud — proof of their illegal trade. He smiled. The eclipse ended, light returned, and he sprinted to safety, his heart pounding like a **kettle** (水壶) whistling on a stove.
