**连锁故事 (English)**

On a rainy **day** (日子), my **company** (公司) sent me to a small **country** (国家) to solve a serious **problem** (问题). I had a long **conversation** (对话) with a local **human** (人) who worked at a **university** (大学). He **stated** (陈述) that the local **food** (食物) supply was failing, and he **wanted** (想要) **both** (两者) government support and private investment. I knew I **must** (必须) act quickly.

I **chose** (选择) to **show** (展示) my plan to the minister. **Any** (任何) delay would make things worse. **My** (我的) **accord** (协议) with the university was **very** (非常) **social** (社会的) in nature — we agreed to share resources. The minister **showed** (展现) interest, but he said the budget was **less** (更少) than expected. Still, we found a way.

The **university** (大学) students **accompanied** (陪伴) me on a field trip. Their **universal** (普遍的) enthusiasm was **heartening** (令人振奋的). We **verified** (验证) that the **problem** (问题) was not shortage but distribution. With **daily** (每日的) effort, we **lessened** (减少) the waste. The **country** (国家) soon recovered. That **conversation** (对话) changed everything — it proved that **humanity** (人性) can overcome any **obstacle** (障碍) when we **converse** (交谈) and **cooperate** (合作). In the end, I felt **fortunate** (幸运的) to have such a **companion** (同伴) as that professor.
