**The Saintly Sceptic and the Smuggled Specimen**

In a foggy coastal town, there lived a **saintly** (圣洁的) old man, Father Anselm, and a **sceptical** (怀疑的) young detective, Iris. One day, Iris found a **seventeenth** (第十七) century map hidden in a **saint** (圣人) statue’s base. The map marked a spot near the harbor. As she examined it, a sudden cold wind made her **shiver** (发抖、哆嗦). She suspected someone was trying to **smuggle** (走私) a rare **specimen** (标本) — a glowing flower said to cure all diseases.

That evening, Iris watched a suspicious sailor who kept **sneezing** (打喷嚏) violently. He carried a **spoon** (勺子) and a small vial of **sour** (酸的) liquid. She followed him to a **stagnant** (停滞的) pond behind the church. There, she saw him use a needle and **stitch** (针脚、缝补) a waterproof bag. The sailor would **stoop** (弯腰) to hide it under the dock. Suddenly, a **submarine** (潜水艇) surfaced silently. Iris realized they planned to **subtract** (减去) the flower from the museum’s collection.

She radioed the coast guard, but time was short. “Will one boat **suffice** (足够)?” she muttered. With a powerful **thrust** (推力) of the engine, she chased them. The chase became a **torment** (折磨) as waves crashed. A **torrent** (激流) of rain poured down, nearly sinking her boat. She grabbed a **trunk** (树干) to steady herself. Just then, the coast guard arrived from the **vicinity** (邻近). The sailor was caught, and the **specimen** (标本) was saved.

Later, Father Anselm smiled, “Even a **sceptic** (怀疑论者) can find faith in justice.” Iris laughed, her **shivery** (发抖的) hands finally warm.

**中文翻译**

在雾气蒙蒙的海滨小镇，住着一位**圣洁的**老神父安塞姆，和一位**怀疑的**年轻侦探艾瑞斯。一天，艾瑞斯在一尊**圣人**雕像底座里发现了一张**第十七**世纪的地图。地图标出了港口附近的一处地点。当她仔细查看时，一阵冷风让她**发抖、哆嗦**。她怀疑有人想**走私**一件稀有**标本**——一朵据说能治愈百病的发光花朵。

当晚，艾瑞斯跟踪一个不停**打喷嚏**的可疑水手。他带着一把**勺子**和一小瓶**酸的**液体。她跟着他来到教堂后面一个**停滞的**池塘边。她看见他用针线**针脚、缝补**一个防水袋。那水手会**弯腰**把它藏在码头下。突然，一艘**潜水艇**无声浮出水面。艾瑞斯意识到他们计划从博物馆**减去**（偷走）那朵花。

她呼叫了海岸警卫队，但时间紧迫。“一艘船够**足够**吗？”她喃喃自语。随着引擎强大的**推力**，她追了上去。追逐成了**折磨**，巨浪拍打。一场**激流**般的大雨倾盆而下，几乎掀翻她的船。她抓住一根**树干**稳住身体。就在这时，海岸警卫队从**邻近**处赶到。水手被抓住了，**标本**得救了。

后来，安塞姆神父笑着说：“即使**怀疑论者**也能在正义中找到信仰。”艾瑞斯笑了，她**发抖的**手终于暖和起来。
