## 连锁故事

**The Ghost, the Warehouse, and the Tin of Wit**

In a small town, a **pregnant** (怀孕的) woman named Martha ran a tiny shop. Her **brisk** (轻快的、活跃的) morning routine was interrupted by a strange **rally** (集会) gathering outside her **doorway** (门口). The crowd was led by a **rebel** (反抗) who shouted against the new tax law. For three **successive** (连续的) days, the noise grew louder.

Martha, a woman of sharp **wit** (机智、才智), decided to **disguise** (伪装) herself as an old peddler and entered the crowd. She noticed a **warehouse** (仓库) nearby, its door slightly open. Inside, she found stacks of **tin** (锡) ingots glinting under a dusty lamp. On one crate, someone had carved a **curse** (诅咒) in old script: "May the **greedy** (贪婪的) owner lose his **certainty** (确定)."

She also spotted a **clause** (条款) in a discarded contract — it mentioned an **axis** (轴) of trade routes and a **casualty** (事故) clause. The **ghost** (鬼魂) of a former worker, it was said, haunted the **warehouse** (仓库), seeking justice for a fatal accident.

Martha, using her **arithmetic** (算术) skills, calculated the **successive** (连续的) losses the owner had suffered. She realized the "curse" was actually a warning. She told the **rebel** (反抗) leader, who turned out to be her old friend, that the real enemy was not the tax, but the dishonest owner who had cheated workers.

Together, they exposed the fraud. The **faithful** (忠实的) townspeople rallied, and the owner fled. Martha's **wit** (机智、才智) saved the day. She even found a hidden **cage** (笼子) of rare birds, which she set free. The **ghost** (鬼魂) finally rested, and the town returned to peace.

## 中文翻译

**《鬼魂、仓库和一罐锡的智慧》**

在一个小镇上，一位**怀孕的**（pregnant）妇女玛莎经营着一家小店铺。她**轻快的**（brisk）晨间惯例被一场奇怪的**集会**（rally）打断了——人群聚集在她的**门口**（doorway）外。领头的是一个**反抗者**（rebel），他大声抗议新税法。连续三天（**successive**），喧闹声越来越大。

玛莎，一位拥有敏锐**机智**（wit）的女人，决定**伪装**（disguise）成老货郎混入人群。她注意到附近有一个**仓库**（warehouse），门微微开着。里面，她发现成堆的**锡**（tin）锭在灰尘覆盖的灯下闪光。在一个板条箱上，有人用古文字刻了一句**诅咒**（curse）：“愿**贪婪的**（greedy）主人失去他的**确定**（certainty）。”

她还在一份被丢弃的合同中看到一条**条款**（clause）——提到了贸易路线的**轴**（axis）和一条**事故**（casualty）条款。据说，一位前工人的**鬼魂**（ghost）常在仓库里游荡，为一场致命事故寻求正义。

玛莎用她的**算术**（arithmetic）技能，计算了主人**连续**（successive）遭受的损失。她意识到“诅咒”其实是一个警告。她告诉那个**反抗者**（rebel）首领——原来他是她的老朋友——真正的敌人不是税收，而是那个欺骗工人的不诚实老板。

他们联手揭露了欺诈。**忠实的**（faithful）镇民们团结起来，老板逃跑了。玛莎的**机智**（wit）挽救了局面。她还发现了一个藏着稀有鸟类的**笼子**（cage），将它们放飞。**鬼魂**（ghost）终于安息，小镇恢复了和平。
