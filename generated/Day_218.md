**连锁故事**

Dr. Amelia felt a wave of **disgust** (厌恶) as she inspected the laboratory’s old **donkey** (驴) — a robotic mule used for testing. She pulled open a rusty **drawer** (抽屉) and found a broken **drone** (无人驾驶飞机) inside, its propellers bent. She pressed a button to **eject** (喷射、驱逐) the damaged battery, which shot out with a hiss. Nature had **endow** (赋予) her with curiosity, so she began to study the drone’s metal surface, now showing signs of **erosion** (侵蚀). She used a water jet to **flush** (冲洗) away the rust, and under the light, tiny metallic particles began to **glitter** (闪烁). She whispered a mental **goodbye** (再见) to the old machine, then used a spark to **ignite** (点燃) a test flame, hoping to **industrialise** (工业化) the repair process. On her workbench lay a small **jewel** (珠宝) — a sensor chip, shaped like a **kidney** (肾). It was only a few **kilometres** (公里) away from the factory, but the delivery had suffered a **lapse** (失效、流逝) in schedule. She signed a new **lease** (租约) for better equipment, though her leg began to **limp** (跛行、柔软的) from standing too long. Her assistant brought her a **lovely** (可爱的) cup of tea, and she smiled, proud of her **nationality** (民族) — a scientist who never gave up.

**中文翻译**

阿梅莉亚博士感到一阵厌恶（disgust），因为她检查实验室里那台老旧的机器驴（donkey）——一只用于测试的机器人骡子。她拉开一个生锈的抽屉（drawer），发现里面有一架破损的无人机（drone），螺旋桨都弯了。她按下按钮，将损坏的电池喷射（eject）出来，电池嘶嘶地飞了出去。大自然赋予（endow）了她好奇心，她开始研究无人机的金属表面，上面已出现侵蚀（erosion）的痕迹。她用水枪冲洗（flush）掉锈迹，在灯光下，微小的金属颗粒开始闪烁（glitter）。她默默地对这台旧机器说了声再见（goodbye），然后用电火花点燃（ignite）了测试火焰，希望将修复过程工业化（industrialise）。她的工作台上放着一颗小珠宝（jewel）——一枚形似肾（kidney）的传感器芯片。工厂距离这里只有几公里（kilometre），但交付已出现失效（lapse）延误。她签了一份新租约（lease）以获取更好的设备，但因为站得太久，她的腿开始跛行（limp），显得柔软无力。她的助手给她端来一杯可爱的（lovely）茶，她微笑着，为自己的民族（nationality）——一个永不放弃的科学家——感到骄傲。
