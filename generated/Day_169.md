In a kingdom where **morality** (道德) was the **principal** law, a young **prince** (王子) named Aldric dreamed of a **paradise** (天堂) beyond the gray walls. One day, a jealous cousin plotted **revenge** (报复) against him, poisoning the king’s mind. The **vanguard** (先锋) of the royal guard—led by a fearless **champion** (冠军)—was ordered to arrest Aldric. As they marched, they passed a **chessboard** (棋盘) table where two old men played, their moves as **deceptive** (骗人的) as the court’s whispers. Aldric fled on a **caravan** (大篷车) van (先导、货车), clutching a bar of **chocolate** (巧克力) given by his **dearly** (深情地) beloved maid. She had whispered, “Do not **deceive** (欺骗) yourself; the **horror** (恐怖) lies in the castle, not in your heart.” He drank **juicy** (多汁的) orange juice (果汁) by a stream, then hid in a **showery** (阵雨般的) cave as rain poured from **cloudless** (晴朗的) skies—a strange omen. At the cave’s **threshold** (门槛), he found a **vocalist** (歌手) singing an **autonomous** (自主的) melody of freedom. She told him of a **hierarchical** (等级制的) system where the king ruled by fear, not by **grief** (悲痛) for his lost son. Aldric realized the **princess** (公主) he loved was actually his half-sister—a truth that brought **grievous** (令人悲痛的) pain. He chose to return, not for revenge, but to **vocalize** (发声) the truth. The people rallied, and the false king was overthrown. Aldric became a **princely** (王子的) ruler, and the kingdom finally tasted **paradisiacal** (天堂般的) peace.

**中文翻译**

在一个以**道德**（morality）为根本法律、由一位年轻**王子**（prince）阿尔德里克梦想着灰色城墙之外**天堂**（paradise）的王国里，有一天，一位嫉妒的表兄密谋对他实施**报复**（revenge），毒害了国王的心智。皇家卫队的**先锋**（vanguard）——由一位无畏的**冠军**（champion）率领——奉命逮捕阿尔德里克。他们行进时，路过一张摆着**国际象棋**（chess）的桌子，两位老人对弈，他们的走法如同宫廷的窃窃私语一样**骗人**（deceptive）。阿尔德里克乘着一辆大篷车（caravan）的**货车**（van）逃走，手里紧握着一块**巧克力**（chocolate），那是他**深情地**（dearly）爱慕的侍女所赠。她曾低声说：“不要**欺骗**（deceive）自己；**恐怖**（horror）在城堡里，不在你心中。”他在溪边喝着**多汁的**（juicy）**果汁**（juice），然后躲进一个**阵雨般的**（showery）洞穴，尽管天空**晴朗无云**（cloudless）却降下大雨——这是个奇怪的预兆。在洞穴的**门槛**（threshold）处，他遇到一位**歌手**（vocalist），正唱着一首**自主的**（autonomous）自由旋律。她告诉他，这个**等级制度**（hierarchy）下，国王靠恐惧统治，而非为失去儿子而**悲痛**（grief）。阿尔德里克意识到他深爱的**公主**（princess）其实是他同父异母的妹妹——这个真相带来**令人悲痛的**（grievous）痛苦。他选择返回，不是为了报复，而是为了**发声**（vocalize）真相。人民团结起来，假国王被推翻。阿尔德里克成为一位**王子的**（princely）统治者，王国终于尝到了**天堂般的**（paradisiacal）和平。
