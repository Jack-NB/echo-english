**The Chain Story**

Maya stood **against** (相对、相反) the cold wind on the cliff, watching the sun **rise** (上升) over the ocean. She felt a surge of **energy** (精力) as the light touched her face, but she knew she had to **control** (控制) her excitement — the mission required calm. She checked the **space** (空间) in her backpack, a **case** (情况) that demanded careful planning. She had to **hold** (握着、坚持) her nerve, because every **possible** (可能的) outcome depended on her **ability** (能力) to adapt. A wild **animal** (动物) stirred in the bushes nearby, but there was **enough** (足够) distance for safety. She reminded herself of the **value** (价值) of patience; her **role** (角色) as the team leader was crucial. Last **week** (周末), she had practiced alone, knowing each **individual** (个人的、独特的) skill mattered. She raised her **hand** (手) to signal the others, then **open** (打开) the map. Ten (十) kilometers remained — a long **term** (条款、期限) journey that would test them all. But she wouldn't turn **away** (离开) now. With **boldness** and **sharpened** instincts, she stepped forward, her heart racing. The **sunrise** burned brightly, and she knew the **uphold** of her promise would carry them through. **Evaluating** every step, she felt **enabled** to face the unknown. **Dividing** the route into segments, she whispered, "**Handling** this is our **opening**." And so they moved, not as ten separate souls, but as one **indivisible** force.

**中文翻译**

玛雅迎着（against）悬崖上的寒风，看着太阳从海面上升起（rise）。当光线触及她的脸庞时，她感到一股精力（energy）涌动，但她知道必须控制（control）自己的兴奋——任务需要冷静。她检查了背包里的空间（space），这是一个需要周密计划的情况（case）。她必须坚持（hold）住勇气，因为每一种可能的（possible）结果都取决于她适应环境的能力（ability）。附近的灌木丛中，一只野生动物（animal）惊动了，但距离足够（enough）保证安全。她提醒自己耐心是很有价值的（valuable），她作为队长的角色（role）至关重要。上个周末（week），她独自练习过，深知每一个个体的（individual）技能都很重要。她举起手（hand）向队友们示意，然后打开（open）地图。十（ten）公里——这是一段漫长的、有期限的（term）旅程，将考验所有人。但她现在不会转身离开（away）。带着勇气和敏锐的本能，她迈步向前，心跳加速。日出（sunrise）灿烂地燃烧，她知道维护（uphold）承诺会带他们走完全程。评估（evaluating）每一步，她感到自己被激发（enabled）去面对未知。将路线划分（dividing）成段，她轻声说：“处理（handling）这一切是我们的开端（opening）。”于是他们出发了，不是十个分离的灵魂，而是一个不可分割（indivisible）的整体。
