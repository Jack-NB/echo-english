**连锁故事 (英文)**

On a **weekday** (工作日), a **vegetarian** (素食者) named Leo woke up with a **grin** (咧嘴笑). He had **haste** (匆忙) to catch the ferry to the **harbor** (港口), where he worked as a **preside** (主持) engineer for a **radar** (雷达) system. His **nickname** (昵称) among colleagues was “The **Furnace** (炉子),” because his ideas always **condense** (凝结、浓缩) into solid plans.

He grabbed a **toast** (烤面包) and a **brochure** (手册) about new **textile** (纺织品) materials, then rushed past the **canteen** (食堂). In the lab, he needed to **differentiate** (区分) two signals that **denote** (表示) different frequencies. His **posture** (姿势) was tense as he adjusted the **vacuum** (真空) tube; a sudden **rip** (撕裂) in the cable made him jump. He fixed it quickly, then inflated a small **balloon** (气球) to test air pressure — it popped, making him laugh.

The day ended well. Leo’s calm **posture** (姿势) returned, and he smiled, thinking how his **furnace**-like energy always turned chaos into order. Even his **haste** (匆忙) had become a skill.
