**英文连锁故事（300-500字）**

In a massive **factory** (工厂) near the river, the chief **authority** (权力、权威人士) called an emergency meeting. He was the head of a workers' **association** (协会), and his word carried weight. Outside, an old **tree** (树、爬上树) stood by the gate, its branches swaying as workers began to **arrive** (到、达到) one by one. Everyone was **ready** (准备好的) for a change, tired of the **cheap** (便宜的、不值钱的) wages and the lack of **patent** (专利、专利的) protection for their inventions.

The boss wanted to cancel the morning **flight** (航班) to the capital, hoping to prevent the news from **spread** (传播) to the press. But the workers had already counted the **expense** (花费) of their silence. They wanted the company to become **famous** (有名的) for fairness, and **indeed** (确实), they had evidence to **establish** (建立、查实) their claims. They had spent months gathering documents, learning how to **observe** (观察) every illegal move. Their **chief** (首领、主要的) negotiator was a calm woman who kept the files in a **safe** (安全的) place — **nor** (也不) could the boss find them, **nor** could he bribe anyone. She planted her **foot** (脚、走) firmly and declared that there would be no more hiding; it was time for an open **debate** (讨论).

The meeting lasted hours. In the end, the **authority** (权力、权威人士) had to surrender, agreeing to raise wages and respect every **patent** (专利、专利的) filed by workers. The **association** (协会) celebrated, and the old **tree** (树、爬上树) seemed to nod in approval as the news of their victory began to **spread** (传播) across the country.

**中文翻译**

在河边的一座大型**工厂**里，首席**权威人士**召开了一次紧急会议。他是一个工人**协会**的头目，说话很有分量。门外，一棵老**树**立在门边，树枝随风摇曳，工人们陆续**到达**。大家都**准备好**迎接改变，厌倦了**不值钱**的工资以及自己的发明缺乏**专利**保护。

老板想取消飞往首都的早晨**航班**，希望阻止消息**传播**给媒体。但工人们早已计算过沉默的**花费**。他们希望公司因公平而**有名**，**确实**，他们有证据来**查实**自己的主张。他们花了数月收集文件，学会了如何**观察**每一个非法举动。他们的**首席**谈判代表是一位冷静的女性，她把文件放在一个**安全**的地方——老板既找不到，**也不**能贿赂任何人。她稳稳地站住**脚**，宣布不再躲藏，是时候进行公开**讨论**了。

会议持续了几个小时。最终，**权威人士**不得不屈服，同意提高工资并尊重工人们提交的每一项**专利**。**协会**举行了庆祝，那棵老**树**似乎也在赞许地点头，而他们胜利的消息开始在全国**传播**开来。
