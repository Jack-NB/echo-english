**连锁故事**

The young mathematician (数学的) Harold felt he was **nowhere** (无处) in life. His **outer** (外面的) appearance was **awkward** (笨拙的), and his speech often remained **vague** (模糊地) when he tried to **contend** (竞争、争辩) in academic debates. One morning, he received a small **allowance** (津贴) from his university, barely enough to fix the broken **cable** (缆、电缆) of his old computer. He decided to **skip** (跳过) breakfast and work on his research about how stars **emit** (放射) light. He consulted a **manual** (手册) that described the **delicate** (易碎的、精致的) instruments needed for observation.

A senior professor, known for his **prestige** (声望), **prescribed** (开处方) a new approach: “Stop being so **silly** (愚蠢的). You are **competent** (有能力的), but you hide inside your **shell** (壳) like a turtle.” Harold laughed, feeling the **bell** (钟、铃) of truth in those words. He remembered a **tale** (故事) his grandmother told him about a **hat** (帽子) that could grant confidence. That afternoon, he finally spoke clearly, and his ideas were heard. He realized that confidence was not about luck, but about believing in his own ability.

**中文翻译**

年轻的数学家哈罗德觉得自己在人生中**无处**（nowhere）可去。他**外面的**（outer）形象**笨拙**（awkward），说话也总是**模糊**（vague）。在学术辩论中，他努力**竞争**（contend），却总不占上风。一天早晨，他收到了学校的一小笔**津贴**（allowance），勉强够修好旧电脑的**电缆**（cable）。他决定**跳过**（skip）早餐，专心研究恒星如何**放射**（emit）光线。他查阅了一本**手册**（manual），上面描述了观测所需的**精致**（delicate）仪器。

一位以**声望**（prestige）闻名的老教授给他**开出了处方**（prescribe）般的新建议：“别再那么**愚蠢**（silly）了。你很有**能力**（competent），却总躲在**壳**（shell）里，像只乌龟。”哈罗德笑了，感到这番话像**钟**（bell）声一样敲醒了他。他想起祖母讲过的关于一顶**帽子**（hat）能赐予勇气的**故事**（tale）。那天下午，他终于清晰地发言，想法得到了认可。他明白，自信不是靠运气，而是相信自己。
