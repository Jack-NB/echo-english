**英文连锁故事**

In the crumbling town of **Terminal** (终点、末端), a **peasant** (农民) named Milo was unloading a **parcel** (包裹) of wool from his **sheep** (羊). The package was heavy, and the weight began to **irritate** (激怒) his sore back, making him **tremble** (颤抖) with fatigue. He longed for **liberty** (自由) from his toil, but the village elder had a **tribute** (贡品、称赞) to pay to the local lord.

Suddenly, a **summon** (召唤) rang out—a horn blast from the castle. Milo’s heart sank. The lord had been **scold** (斥责) the farmers for weeks over poor harvests. Now, a **diplomatic** (外交的、老练的) envoy arrived to **displace** (取代) the old tax collector, hoping to **offset** (抵消) the unrest. But the envoy’s smooth words only **evoke** (唤起) suspicion among the villagers.

In the **midst** (中间) of the crowd, an old woman whispered that the lord was a **devil** (魔鬼) in disguise. Her words **echo** (回声) through the square, and soon the air grew tense. Just then, a strange **mammal** (哺乳动物)—a shaggy, horned beast—stumbled out of the forest, collapsing at the edge of the town. It was the lord’s hunting trophy, but it had died of exhaustion.

The envoy, seeing the chaos, ordered a **funeral** (葬礼) for the creature, hoping to calm the people. As the pyre was lit, Milo felt the **tremble** (颤抖) of the ground beneath him—a sudden **slump** (暴跌) in the economy had just been announced, and the price of wool had plummeted. His dreams of **liberty** (自由) faded into the smoke.

**中文翻译**

在破败的**终点**小镇上，一个名叫米洛的**农民**正从他的**羊**身上卸下一包**羊毛**。包裹很重，重量开始**激怒**他酸痛的背，让他因疲惫而**颤抖**。他渴望**自由**，但村长必须向当地领主缴纳一份**贡品**。

突然，一声**召唤**响起——城堡传来的号角。米洛的心一沉。领主几周来一直在**斥责**农民收成不佳。现在，一位**外交**使者到来，要**取代**老税吏，希望**抵消**动荡。但使者圆滑的言辞只**唤起**了村民的怀疑。

在人群**中间**，一位老妇低声说领主是伪装的**魔鬼**。她的话在广场上**回荡**，气氛顿时紧张起来。就在这时，一只奇怪的**哺乳动物**——一头毛茸茸、长角的野兽——跌跌撞撞地走出森林，倒在镇边。这是领主的狩猎战利品，但它已经累死了。

使者看到混乱，下令为这头野兽举行**葬礼**，希望安抚民众。当火堆点燃时，米洛感到脚下地面在**颤抖**——刚刚宣布经济**暴跌**，羊毛价格暴跌。他**自由**的梦想消散在烟雾中。
