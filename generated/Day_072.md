**The Chain Story**

In a small town, a **dominant** (支配的) businessman named Leo tried to **dominate** (支配) every local market. He kept a fast **pace** (速度) in his work, believing speed was the key **element** (元素) of success. He would **impose** (强加于) his own rules on smaller shops, and when the annual **vote** (投票) came, he demanded everyone say “**yes**” (是) to his proposals. But his **narrow** (狭窄的) mindset made him blind to new ideas.

One day, he decided to **transfer** (转移) his business online, hoping to **copy** (复制) the success of big cities. However, consumer habits **vary** (改变) from place to place. He refused to **participate** (参与) in community meetings, even though his **advisor** (顾问) tried to **advise** (建议) him differently. To expand, he took a huge **loan** (贷款), but his **demonstration** (演示) of new products failed to attract customers. He had forgotten the **fundamental** (基本的) rule: trust is the **origin** (起源) of all trade.

Luckily, a kind competitor **recommended** (推荐) him to a mentor. The mentor’s **valuable** (有价值的) advice was simple: “Add genuine service as an **additional** (额外的) benefit to your customers.” Leo signed a new **contract** (合同) with local farmers, promising fair prices. His **contraction** (收缩) of arrogance and **expansion** (扩展) of humility slowly rebuilt his reputation. The town’s **voters** (选民) finally welcomed him back, and he learned that true success is not about **domination** (支配) but **participation** (参与).

**中文翻译**

在一个小镇上，一位**有支配欲的**商人利奥试图**支配**每一个本地市场。他保持快速的**节奏**工作，相信速度是成功的**要素**。他常把自己的规则**强加于**小店铺，当年度**投票**来临时，他要求所有人对他的提案说“**是**”。但他**狭窄**的思维让他对新想法视而不见。

一天，他决定将业务**转移**到线上，希望**复制**大城市的成功。然而，消费者习惯因地区而**改变**。他拒绝**参与**社区会议，尽管他的**顾问**试图**劝告**他改变策略。为了扩张，他借了巨额**贷款**，但他的新产品**演示**未能吸引顾客。他忘记了**根本**的规则：信任是贸易的**起源**。

幸运的是，一位善良的竞争对手**推荐**他给一位导师。导师的**宝贵的**建议很简单：“把真诚的服务作为**额外的**福利加给你的顾客。”他与当地农民签署了新**合同**，承诺公平价格。他**缩小**了傲慢，**扩展**了谦逊，慢慢重建了声誉。镇上的**选民**终于欢迎他回归，他明白了真正的成功不是**支配**，而是**参与**。
