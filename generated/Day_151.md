**The Departure Dilemma**

On the night of her departure (离开), Lena felt a strange thrill (激动) running through her veins. She was leaving her small, fertile (肥沃的) valley town to join a scientific expedition studying alien (外星人) signals. The project aimed to **correlate** (关联) radio wave patterns with potential life forms — a **correlation** that had divided the whole scientific community.

She had packed her **array** (系列、序列) of equipment: sensors, notebooks, and even a small rocket (火箭) model given by her grandfather. The rocket's **rocketry** design was old, but it held sentimental value. As she walked past the town square, she saw a fool (傻瓜) juggling fire — a local entertainer whose **foolish** antics always made her laugh. Tonight, however, she had no time for laughter.

Her engagement (订婚) ring felt heavy on her finger. She had promised to marry Mark, but now she was choosing science over love. It was a real dilemma (困境): stay and build a family, or leave and chase the stars. This **dilemmatic** choice haunted her. She opted (选择) to leave, but her **optional** future with Mark remained uncertain.

At the edge of town, she met her old teacher, Professor Hill, who had helped her devise (设计) a new signal-filtering device (装置). The device was designed to **exaggerate** (夸大) faint signals — though some critics called it an **exaggeration** of hope. Hill smiled and said, "You will inherit (继承) my research **inheritance** — use it well."

The military **troop** (军队) stationed nearby provided transport. Their **troopers** were professional, but their cold eyes made Lena feel like an **alien** (外星人) among humans. She climbed into the vehicle, clutching a small flag that said "ours (我们的)" — a symbol of **ourselves** and our shared dreams.

As the truck rumbled away, she thought of the **tribe** (部落) of scientists waiting at the observatory. They were a **tribal** community bound by curiosity. She whispered a spell (拼写、咒语) from her childhood — a **spelling** charm her grandmother taught her — and felt a wave of courage.

The road was long, but her heart was full. She would **float** (漂浮) among the stars, **floating** free from doubt. And perhaps, one day, she would return — not as a fool (傻瓜), but as a woman who had faced her **dilemma** and chosen her own path.

**中文翻译**

在离开（departure）的那个夜晚，莉娜感到一阵奇异的激动（thrill）在血管中奔涌。她即将离开自己那个肥沃（fertile）的小山谷城镇，加入一支研究外星人（alien）信号的科学考察队。该项目旨在将（correlate）无线电波模式与潜在生命形式关联起来——这种相关性（correlation）曾让整个科学界产生分歧。

她打包好了自己的一系列（array）设备：传感器、笔记本，甚至还有祖父送的小火箭（rocket）模型。那火箭（rocketry）设计虽然老旧，却充满纪念意义。走过镇广场时，她看见一个傻瓜（fool）在耍火——一个本地艺人，他那愚蠢的（foolish）把戏总是逗她发笑。但今晚，她没有时间笑。

订婚（engagement）戒指在她手指上显得沉重。她曾答应嫁给马克，但现在她选择科学而非爱情。这真是个困境（dilemma）：留下组建家庭，还是离开追逐星辰。这个两难的（dilemmatic）选择困扰着她。她选择了（opt）离开，但那条可选的（optional）与马克的未来依然不确定。

在镇边，她遇到了她的老教授希尔，他曾帮助她设计（devise）一种新的信号过滤装置（device）。该装置旨在夸大（exaggerate）微弱信号——尽管有些批评者称这不过是希望的夸大（exaggeration）。希尔笑着说：“你将继承（inherit）我的研究遗产（inheritance）——好好利用它。”

驻扎在附近的军队（troop）提供运输。他们的骑兵（troopers）很专业，但冷漠的眼神让莉娜觉得自己在人类中像个外星人（alien）。她爬上车辆，紧握一面写着“我们的（ours）”的小旗——象征着我们自己（ourselves）和人类共同的梦想。

随着卡车隆隆驶离，她想起了正在天文台等待的部落（tribe）科学家们。他们是一个因好奇而凝聚的部落式的（tribal）社群。她轻声念出童年时的一个咒语（spell）——外婆教她的拼写（spelling）护身咒——顿时感到一阵勇气涌上心头。

路很长，但她的心很充实。她将在群星间漂浮（float），自由地漂浮（floating）于疑虑之上。也许有一天，她会回来——不再是个傻瓜（fool），而是一个面对困境（dilemma）并选择了自己道路的女人。
