### 2. 连锁故事

In a small town, the mayor made a **nominal** (名义上的) promise to repair the old bridge. He didn't intend to act, but he had to **notify** (通知) the council. Near the bridge stood a giant **oak** (橡木) tree, whose leaves looked **pale** (苍白的、暗淡的) in the autumn light. A student sat under it, reading a **paperback** (平装书) about ancient architecture. The book described how the **pavement** (路面) of Roman roads was built stone by stone, and how a worker could drink a **pint** (品脱) of ale after a hard day. The book also mentioned that the **pope** (罗马教皇) once ordered a grand cathedral, where priests would **rehearse** (排演、预演) their chants for weeks. The student dreamed of becoming an architect, hoping to **repay** (偿还) his parents' sacrifices. Suddenly, his boss called to **sack** (解雇) him for being late too often. Shocked, the student felt like a **skeleton** (骨架) stripped of flesh. He walked to the town square, where a **spectacular** (壮观) fountain stood beside a marble **statue** (雕塑) of a hero. A **stereo** (立体声) speaker played music, and a **strand** (缕) of wire hung loose, causing a **tangle** (纠纷、纠缠) of cables. The student sighed and entered an ancient **temple** (庙宇) nearby, hoping to **transcend** (超越) his misery. Inside, he saw a sign: "**Utilise** (利用) every failure as a lesson." He smiled and resolved to rebuild his life.

### 3. 中文翻译

在一个小镇上，市长做了一项**名义上的**（nominal）承诺，要修复那座老桥。他并不打算真的行动，但还是不得不**通知**（notify）议会。桥旁矗立着一棵巨大的**橡木**（oak）树，秋光下它的叶子显得**苍白而暗淡**（pale）。一个学生坐在树下，读着一本**平装书**（paperback），内容关于古代建筑。书中描述了罗马道路的**路面**（pavement）如何一石一石铺成，以及工人辛苦一天后如何能喝上一**品脱**（pint）麦酒。书中还提到，**罗马教皇**（pope）曾下令建造一座宏伟的大教堂，祭司们会连续数周**排演**（rehearse）圣歌。学生梦想成为建筑师，希望**偿还**（repay）父母的牺牲。突然，老板打电话来**解雇**（sack）他，因为他迟到太多次。震惊之下，学生觉得自己像被剥去血肉的**骨架**（skeleton）。他走到镇广场，那里有一座**壮观的**（spectacular）喷泉，旁边立着一尊英雄的**雕塑**（statue）。一台**立体声**（stereo）音箱播放着音乐，一根**缕**（strand）电线松脱，造成电缆**纠缠**（tangle）。学生叹了口气，走进附近一座古老的**庙宇**（temple），希望能**超越**（transcend）自己的痛苦。庙内有一块牌子，写着：“**利用**（utilise）每一次失败作为教训。”他微微一笑，决心重建自己的生活。
