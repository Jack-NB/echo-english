**English Story:**

In a remote village, a kind old woman tried to **nourish** (滋养) every stray animal. Her small **outfit** (机构、配备) was just a wooden shed, but she treated a sick **panda** (熊猫) with herbs that could **penetrate** (穿透) deep into its wounds. Nearby, a tall **pine** (松树) swayed, and she often sent a **postcard** (明信片) to her grandson, attached with a tiny **quiz** (小测验) to teach him about nature.

One day, a **rag** (破布) of cloth caught fire, sparking a **rebellion** (叛乱) among the village cats, who demanded a **refund** (退款) for their stolen fish. The old woman remained **resilient** (有弹力的), calming them with a gentle **roar** (咆哮、轰鸣) that was more like a lullaby. She refused to **segregate** (隔离) the animals by species, believing every creature deserved the same **sensation** (感觉) of warmth.

She wore a **sleeve** (袖子) that was too long, making her look **slender** (细长的、苗条的), but she never slipped on the **slippery** (滑的) moss. She knit a woolen **sock** (袜子) for a cold fox, and rested on her **sofa** (沙发) while gazing toward the **southwest** (西南), where the sun set behind the hills. Her **nourishment** (营养品) and **resilience** (弹性) taught the village that even the smallest acts of care could **penetrate** (穿透) the hardest hearts.

**中文翻译：**

在一个偏远的村庄里，一位善良的老妇人试图滋养（nourish）所有流浪动物。她的小机构（outfit）只是一个木棚，但她用能穿透（penetrate）伤口深处的草药治疗一只生病的熊猫（panda）。附近一棵高大的松树（pine）随风摇曳，她常给孙子寄一张明信片（postcard），附上一个小测验（quiz）教他认识自然。

一天，一块破布（rag）着了火，引发了村里猫群的叛乱（rebellion），它们要求为被偷的鱼退款（refund）。老妇人保持着有弹力的（resilient）心态，用更像摇篮曲的温和咆哮（roar）安抚它们。她拒绝按物种隔离（segregate）动物，相信每个生灵都值得同样的感觉（sensation）温暖。

她穿着一只太长的袖子（sleeve），显得细长的（slender），但从不在滑的（slippery）苔藓上滑倒。她为一只冷狐狸织了一只羊毛袜子（sock），然后坐在沙发（sofa）上，望向西南（southwest）方——太阳从山后落下的方向。她的营养品（nourishment）和弹性（resilience）教会了村庄：即使最小的关怀也能穿透（penetrate）最坚硬的心。
