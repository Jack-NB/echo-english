**The Chain Story**

In the old town of Verdigris, the **adjective** (形容词) "brilliant" was often used to describe the **alloy** (合金) of copper and tin that formed the **bearing** (轴承) of the great clock tower. The mechanism, however, began to **bewilder** (使迷惑) even the master clockmaker, for the gears turned with a **bloody** (血腥的) red rust that no **centigrade** (百分度的) thermometer could explain. The town’s **choir** (唱诗班) would **clap** (击掌、拍手) in rhythm during rehearsals, but their joy was merely a **cloak** (隐匿、外衣、遮掩) for the fear that the tower might collapse. Old Marta, who always carried a **comb** (梳子) in her apron pocket, remembered the tower’s **debut** (初次登场) a century ago—a proud moment when every **dwelling** (住宅) in the square had been decorated with flags.

As the frost began to **harden** (硬化) the streets, the clockmaker decided to **inhale** (吸入) the cold air and climb the spiral stairs. He did not **mourn** (哀悼) the old machine yet, but the broken gears would **necessitate** (迫使) a full replacement. A fallen beam threatened to **obstruct** (妨碍) his path, and he noticed that the rust was not ordinary rust—it was a strange **oxide** (氧化物) that glowed faintly. From the top window, he saw a **panorama** (全景) of the town, and in the corner of the workshop, an ancient **rake** (耙子) lay forgotten, its wooden handle worn smooth by generations of hands. He realized that the true solution was not to replace the metal, but to **uncloak** (揭露) the secret of the glowing oxide—a discovery that would make the old clock tower shine again.

**中文翻译**

在绿锈老镇上，“brilliant”这个**形容词**常被用来形容那座大钟楼中由铜和锡组成的**合金**——它们构成了钟楼的**轴承**。然而，机械装置开始**使迷惑**最顶尖的钟表匠，因为齿轮上长满了**血腥的**红锈，任何**百分度的**温度计都无法解释。镇上的**唱诗班**排练时常**击掌**打拍子，但他们的欢乐只是**遮掩**对钟楼可能倒塌的恐惧。总是把**梳子**揣在围裙口袋里的老玛尔塔，还记得钟楼一百年前的**初次登场**——那时广场上的每一座**住宅**都挂满了旗帜。

当霜冻开始**硬化**街道时，钟表匠决定**吸入**冷空气，爬上螺旋楼梯。他并不**哀悼**那台旧机器，但破损的齿轮将**迫使**他进行全面更换。一根掉落的横梁差点**妨碍**他的去路，他注意到那些锈迹并非普通铁锈——而是一种微微发光的奇异**氧化物**。从顶窗望去，他看到了整个城镇的**全景**，而在工坊角落里，一把被人遗忘的古老**耙子**静静躺着，木柄已被几代人的双手磨得光滑。他意识到，真正的解决办法不是更换金属，而是**揭露**那种发光氧化物的秘密——一个能让老钟楼重焕光彩的发现。
