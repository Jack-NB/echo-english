**English Story**

In a gray fortress, a **prisoner** (囚犯) named Jack was plotting his escape. He had once been a **salesman** (推销员) who sold fake medicine, and now he faced a life sentence. Every morning, he read the **summary** (摘要) of his case, but he believed the system was a **monopoly** (垄断) controlled by the wealthy. The guard gave him a small **dose** (剂量) of sedative, but Jack hid it under his tongue. He knew the **gross** (总的) injustice of his trial, yet he kept his **laughter** (笑声) silent, waiting for the right moment.

One night, he followed a hidden **trail** (小径、踪迹) behind his cell wall. He had to **discard** (抛弃、丢弃) all his fear. His plan was **absolute** (绝对的) in its precision. He would **classify** (分类) the guard’s routines, find the **festival** (节日) celebration as cover, and begin his **quest** (探索、寻求) for freedom. He **conceived** (怀孕、设想) the idea months ago, but he never let anyone **underestimate** (低估) his will. He made a silent **bet** (打赌) with himself: he would win or die trying.

The prison’s **density** (密度) of patrols was high, but he knew the **prescription** (处方) for escape: patience and stealth. He faced the **resistance** (抵抗) of cold iron bars, but his hands moved like a ghost. At dawn, he slipped through a drainage pipe. As he ran into the forest, he whispered, “Thank you, **sir** (先生),” to the old guard who had secretly left the gate unlocked.

**中文翻译**

在一座灰色堡垒中，一名叫杰克的**囚犯**（prisoner）正密谋越狱。他曾是一名贩卖假药的**推销员**（salesman），如今面临终身监禁。每天早上，他都会阅读自己案件的**摘要**（summary），但他相信整个系统是被富人控制的**垄断**（monopoly）体系。狱警给他注射了一小**剂量**（dose）的镇静剂，但杰克把它藏在舌下。他深知审判的**总的**（gross）不公，却保持沉默的**笑声**（laughter），等待时机。

一天夜里，他沿着牢房墙后一条隐秘的**小径**（trail）前行。他必须**抛弃**（discard）所有恐惧。他的计划在精确性上是**绝对的**（absolute）。他要**分类**（classify）狱警的巡逻规律，利用**节日**（festival）庆典作掩护，开始他的自由**探索**（quest）。他数月前就**设想**（conceive）了这个计划，但从不让人**低估**（underestimate）他的意志。他跟自己打了个无声的**赌**（bet）：要么成功，要么死去。

监狱巡逻的**密度**（density）很高，但他知道逃脱的**处方**（prescription）就是耐心和隐蔽。他面对冰冷铁栏的**抵抗**（resistance），但双手如幽灵般灵活。黎明时分，他从排水管钻出。当他跑进森林时，他低声对那位偷偷留下门锁未关的老狱警说：“谢谢您，**先生**（sir）。”
