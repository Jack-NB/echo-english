**英文短文**

The archaeologist felt a growing **suspicion** (怀疑) that the ancient site held secrets. She refused to **lag** (落后) behind her team, so she formed a bold **hypothesis** (假设): the ruins could **revive** (复苏、唤醒) a forgotten civilization. She decided to **undertake** (承担) the excavation herself, despite the **deficiency** (不足、缺乏) of funding. She quickly managed to **enrol** (登记) local volunteers, who preferred the **peaceful** (和平的) countryside to the noisy city. Soon, she began to **realise** (认识到、体会到) that the pottery fragments **resembled** (像) designs from a **spiritual** (精神的) cult. She felt **fortunate** (幸运的) to find a **mild** (温和的) climate that protected the relics. The **temptation** (诱惑) to sell a few artifacts for **wine** (酒) money was strong, but she refused to **cater** (迎合) to greedy collectors. Instead, she convinced the government to **donate** (捐赠) funds, and she uncovered a **grave** (坟墓) with a **historic** (有历史意义的) inscription. Finally, she decided to **incorporate** (合并) her findings into a museum exhibition, ensuring the site would never be forgotten again.

**中文翻译**

考古学家感到一种日益增长的怀疑（suspicion），认为这片古代遗址隐藏着秘密。她拒绝落后（lag）于团队，于是提出了一个大胆的假设（hypothesis）：这些废墟能够复苏（revive）一个被遗忘的文明。她决定亲自承担（undertake）这次发掘，尽管资金不足（deficiency）。她迅速登记（enrol）了当地志愿者，他们更喜欢和平的（peaceful）乡村，而不是喧嚣的城市。很快，她开始认识到（realise），那些陶器碎片像（resemble）某个精神（spiritual）教派的设计。她感到幸运（fortunate），温和的（mild）气候保护了这些文物。用几件文物换取酒（wine）钱的诱惑（temptation）很强烈，但她拒绝迎合（cater）贪婪的收藏家。相反，她说服政府捐赠（donate）资金，最终发现了一座带有历史意义的（historic）铭文的坟墓（grave）。最后，她决定将（incorporate）她的发现合并进博物馆展览，确保这片遗址永远不会再被遗忘。
