### 2. 连锁故事

At dawn, the **sunrise** (日出) painted the sky in fiery hues, but the camper was too **soak** (浸) in sweat to enjoy it. He had just escaped a **bizarre** (奇异的) accident: his tent had collapsed in a storm, leaving him **susceptible** (易受感染的) to a cold. His **agony** (极度痛苦) was real—every muscle ached. **Conversely** (相反地), his dog seemed thrilled, chasing leaves with a **dedicate** (致力于) focus that was almost absurd.

He tried to **extinguish** (扑灭) the campfire, but the wind kept **alternate** (交替) directions, blowing sparks everywhere. He sent his dog to **fetch** (取来) his **glove** (手套), but the pup returned with a muddy **stripe** (条纹) of dirt on its nose. The man sighed; he knew he would **incur** (招致) more trouble if he lingered. His map was torn, now **invalid** (无效的), and he had to **loosen** (放松) his grip on reality to improvise.

He remembered the nearby **orchestra** (管弦乐队) hall—a strange landmark in the wilderness. Inside, he found a **pottery** (陶器) exhibition, each piece a **spectacle** (景象、奇观) of craftsmanship. A curator offered him a **quantitative** (定量的) analysis of the clay’s purity, but he just needed a phone. From his **viewpoint** (观点), the world had gone mad—yet he smiled, for the chaos was oddly beautiful.

### 3. 中文翻译

黎明时分，日出（sunrise）将天空染成火红的色调，但露营者汗流浃背，根本无法享受这美景——他浑身都浸（soak）透了汗水。他刚刚逃过一场奇异（bizarre）的事故：帐篷在暴风雨中倒塌，让他变得易受感染（susceptible），容易着凉。他的极度痛苦（agony）是真实的——每块肌肉都在酸痛。相反地（conversely），他的狗却兴奋不已，追逐落叶时那种致力于（dedicate）专注的样子近乎荒谬。

他试图扑灭（extinguish）篝火，但风总是交替（alternate）改变方向，把火星吹得到处都是。他让狗去取来（fetch）他的手套（glove），但小狗回来时鼻子上沾了一条纹（stripe）状的泥巴。男人叹了口气，他知道如果再逗留，就会招致（incur）更多麻烦。他的地图已经撕破，现在完全无效（invalid）了，他不得不放松（loosen）对现实的掌控，随机应变。

他想起了附近的管弦乐队（orchestra）大厅——那是荒野中一个奇怪的地标。走进去，他发现了一个陶器（pottery）展览，每一件作品都是工艺的奇观（spectacle）。一位策展人向他提供了关于黏土纯度的定量（quantitative）分析，但他只需要一部电话。从他的观点（viewpoint）来看，世界已经疯了——然而他却笑了，因为这混乱竟有一种奇异的美丽。
