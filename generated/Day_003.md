**连锁故事 (English)**

It was late at night. **Make** (制作) a decision, he told himself: **than** (比) waiting any longer, he’d rather act. **His** (他的) **time** (时间) was precious. He wanted to **say** (说) something important about his **work** (工作), **which** (哪个) had become chaotic. **When** (当) he hesitated, he realized he **should** (应该) focus on one **part** (部分) at a time — **your** (你的) own plan, he reminded himself.

He decided to **use** (使用) every tool available. **All** (一切) would **follow** (跟随) if he stayed patient. **She** (她) — his mentor — had always told him: “**Who** (谁) controls the process controls the outcome.” **Each** (每个) step mattered. **Some** (一些) tasks were easy, **other** (其他) ones were hard. **If** (如果) he failed, he would learn.

He opened his notebook. The **maker** (制造者) in him wanted to **remake** (重制) the old design. A **timely** (及时的) **saying** (谚语) came to mind: “A **worker** (工人) without a **workshop** (工坊) is lost.” He felt **partially** (部分地) stuck, but his **partner** (伙伴) — a **fellow** (同伴) engineer — had **always** (总是) been **useful** (有用的). Together, they could **participate** (参与) in the project fully.

He thought about **whoever** (无论谁) had doubted him. **Whenever** (无论何时) doubt crept in, he remembered: **shouldn’t** (不应该) give up. His **follower** (追随者) spirit was strong. **Another** (另一个) idea struck him: **otherwise** (否则) the deadline would be missed. **Even if** (即使) tired, he pushed on.

Finally, he smiled. The **overall** (全面的) plan was solid. **Alone** (独自) yet **wholesome** (健康的), he felt complete. **Her** (她的) advice echoed: “**Yours** (你的东西) will shine if you believe.” And so it did.

**中文翻译**

夜深了。他告诉自己：**制作**一个决定吧——**比**继续等待，他宁愿行动。**他的** **时间**宝贵。他想**说**一些关于他**工作**的重要事情，**哪个**已经变得混乱。**当**他犹豫时，他意识到自己**应该**一次只专注**部分**——**你的**自己的计划，他提醒自己。

他决定**使用**所有可用的工具。**一切**都会**跟随**，如果他保持耐心。**她**——他的导师——总是告诉他：“**谁**控制过程，谁就控制结果。”**每个**步骤都很重要。**一些**任务容易，**其他**任务困难。**如果**他失败了，他会学到东西。

最后，他笑了。**全面的**计划很扎实。**独自**却**健康地**，他感到完整。**她的**建议回响：“**你的东西**会发光，只要你相信。”事实确实如此。
