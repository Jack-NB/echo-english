**英文故事** (300-500字):

On a rainy Tuesday, a passenger (乘客) named Leo sat by the window of a train, staring at the **scoreboard** (记分牌) outside a stadium. The final **score** (分数) was 3:2, but his mind was elsewhere. He had just received a **lesson** (课、教训) about life: never ignore the **obvious** (明显的) signs of stress in a relationship. His **relation** (关系) with his brother had been strained for years, and tonight he was finally going to fix it. He pulled out his phone and checked the **date** (日期) — exactly one month since their last **discussion** (讨论). He decided to **adopt** (采用) a new strategy: instead of arguing, he would **represent** (代表) his own feelings calmly. The train passed a **mile** (英里) marker, and Leo realized how far he had traveled emotionally. He made a **regular** (定期的) promise to call his brother every Sunday. His **voice** (声音) was shaky when he left a voicemail, but the **characteristic** (特征) warmth of his brother’s reply — even in text — made him smile. He remembered their childhood **movie** (电影) nights, and how they would **join** (参加) forces to build forts. Now, he needed to **operate** (运转) on their bond like a surgeon. The city’s **pollution** (污染) faded behind him as the train pulled into a small town. He stepped off, ready to **extend** (延伸) an olive branch. His plan seemed **appropriate** (恰当的) — not a **match** (比赛) to win, but a bridge to rebuild. As he walked to his brother’s door, he whispered, “This time, we’ll write a new ending.”

**中文翻译**：

在一个下雨的星期二，一位名叫 Leo 的乘客（passenger）坐在火车窗边，凝视着体育场外的记分牌（scoreboard）。最终分数（score）是 3:2，但他的思绪早已飘远。他刚刚上了一课（lesson）关于人生：永远不要忽视关系中那些明显的（obvious）压力信号。他与哥哥的关系（relation）多年来一直紧张，而今晚他终于要修复它。他掏出手机看了看日期（date）——距离他们上次讨论（discussion）正好一个月。他决定采用（adopt）一种新策略：不再争吵，而是平静地代表（represent）自己的感受。火车驶过一个英里（mile）标记，Leo 意识到自己在情感上已经走了多远。他做了一个定期的（regular）承诺：每周日给哥哥打电话。他留下语音留言时，声音（voice）有些颤抖，但哥哥回复中那种独有的（characteristic）温暖——即使是文字——也让他笑了。他想起童年一起看电影（movie）的夜晚，他们总是联手（join）搭建堡垒。现在，他需要像外科医生一样运转（operate）他们的关系。城市的污染（pollution）在火车驶入小镇时渐渐远去。他走下车，准备延伸（extend）出橄榄枝。他的计划看起来恰当的（appropriate）——不是一场要赢的比赛（match），而是一座要重建的桥。当他走向哥哥的门时，他低声说：“这一次，我们要写一个新的结局。”
