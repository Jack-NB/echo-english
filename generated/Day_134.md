**连锁故事**

In the **noble** (贵族的) castle, a **guy** (家伙) named Arthur worked as a **operator** (操作人员) of the ancient clock tower. One day, he noticed that rust had begun to **undermine** (逐渐削弱) the iron gears. Fearing a collapse, he made a **deliberate** (深思熟虑的、故意的) decision to **suspend** (暂停) the clock’s operation. He knew that a simple **substitute** (代替) part would not be enough; the whole mechanism needed **enrich** (丰富) with fresh oil and careful cleaning.

Arthur’s **outlook** (前景) seemed grim, for the repair cost was far beyond his modest **fare** (车费) savings. Yet he refused to **pollute** (污染) his conscience by ignoring the problem. He consulted a **prominent** (突出的) engineer, whose **verbal** (语言的) instructions were precise. Together, they worked for days, **tolerate** (容忍) each other’s quirks and **peculiar** (独特的、特殊的) habits. The engineer explained that the tower’s **hemisphere** (半球) shaped dome needed special support, and they had to **rear** (培育) a new set of gears from **pure** (纯粹的、纯的) bronze.

On **behalf** (代表) of the entire village, Arthur thanked the engineer. The repaired clock tower stood **thick** (厚的) and strong, its chimes echoing across the land, a testament to their patience and skill.

**中文翻译**

在贵族（noble）的城堡里，一个名叫亚瑟的家伙（guy）担任古老钟楼的操作人员（operator）。一天，他发现铁齿轮开始被逐渐削弱（undermine）的锈蚀侵蚀。担心坍塌，他深思熟虑地（deliberate）决定暂停（suspend）钟楼的运转。他知道简单的代替（substitute）零件不够，整个机构需要丰富（enrich）的润滑油和仔细清洁。

亚瑟的前景（outlook）看似暗淡，因为维修费用远超他微薄的车费（fare）积蓄。然而他拒绝因无视问题而污染（pollute）自己的良心。他咨询了一位突出的（prominent）工程师，其语言的（verbal）指示非常精确。他们一起工作了数日，容忍（tolerate）彼此的怪癖和独特的（peculiar）习惯。工程师解释说，钟楼的半球（hemisphere）形穹顶需要特殊支撑，他们必须培育（rear）一套纯粹的（pure）青铜新齿轮。

代表（behalf）整个村庄，亚瑟感谢了工程师。修好的钟楼厚实（thick）而坚固，钟声响彻大地，见证着他们的耐心与技艺。
