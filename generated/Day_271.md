On a cold **January** (一月) morning, the **left** (左边) wing of the old city hall was unusually quiet. Inside, a **magistrate** (地方法官) sat behind his desk, trying to **inflict** (使遭受) a fair sentence on a petty thief who had stolen a **jug** (罐) of milk. The thief claimed he had been struck by **influenza** (流行性感冒) and needed nourishment.

The magistrate, a man of **majesty** (威严), decided to **invoke** (请求、援引) an ancient **Latin** (拉丁语) proverb about mercy. He glanced at the **infrared** (红外线的) security monitor, which showed nothing suspicious. Then he remembered his own **leading** (主要的) role in the community—he was the **living** (生活) symbol of justice. He thought of his **living room** (客厅), where his wife had just bought new **linen** (亚麻布) curtains, and how she had paid for them in **instalment** (分期付款).

Suddenly, a **locomotive** (机车) roared past the window, shaking the room. The magistrate’s assistant, a young man who often used the **Internet** (因特网) to research cases, suggested they check the thief’s record. The assistant’s **lash** (鞭打) of criticism against the police was harsh, but the magistrate calmed him down.

Outside, the **June** (六月) sun was warm, though **July** (七月) would soon bring heat. The magistrate remembered that his own **leading** (领导的) advisor had once been a **Latin** teacher. He smiled and decided to let the thief go with a warning, but not before giving him a **living** (生活) lesson about honesty. The thief, grateful, promised to repay the milk money by **July** (七月).

As the man left, the magistrate felt a sense of peace—no need to **inflict** (使遭受) harsh punishment. He turned to his assistant and said, “Sometimes justice is about **invoke** (援引) compassion, not **lash** (抨击) the fallen.” The assistant nodded, and they both returned to their work, knowing that even a **magistrate** (地方法官) must keep **living** (生活) in balance.

**中文翻译**

在一个寒冷的**一月**早晨，老市政厅的**左边**侧楼异常安静。里面，一位**地方法官**坐在桌后，试图对一名偷了一罐牛奶的小偷**施加**公正的判决。小偷声称自己得了**流行性感冒**，需要营养。

这位**威严**的法官决定**援引**一句古老的**拉丁语**谚语，关于仁慈。他瞥了一眼**红外线的**监控屏幕，没有发现任何可疑之处。然后他想起自己在社区中的**主要**角色——他是**生活**中正义的象征。他想到了自己的**客厅**，妻子刚买了新的**亚麻布**窗帘，而且是用**分期付款**的方式支付的。

突然，一辆**机车**轰鸣着驶过窗外，震动了房间。法官的助手，一个经常用**因特网**查案的年轻人，建议他们查一下小偷的档案。助手对警方的**鞭打**式批评很尖锐，但法官让他冷静下来。

外面，**六月**的阳光温暖，虽然**七月**很快就会带来酷热。法官想起自己的**领导的**顾问曾经是一位**拉丁语**教师。他笑了，决定放了小偷，只给一个警告，但在此之前，他要给小偷上一堂关于诚实的**生活**课。小偷感激不已，答应在**七月**前还清牛奶钱。

那人离开后，法官感到一种平静——无需**施加**严厉的惩罚。他转向助手说：“有时正义在于**援引**同情，而不是**抨击**跌倒的人。”助手点点头，他们继续工作，知道即使是一位**地方法官**也必须保持**生活**的平衡。
