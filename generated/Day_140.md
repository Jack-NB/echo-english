**连锁故事**

The chef (厨师) was furious. He had just received a **pumping** (抽吸的) machine to **pump** (泵) water from the flooded basement of his **tropical** (热带的) island restaurant. The machine was a piece of **junk** (垃圾) — it made a loud **clicking** (点击声) sound but refused to work. He had to **pack** (打包) the fresh fish quickly before they spoiled. The **interior** (内部) of his kitchen was a mess, and the **liability** (责任) for the ruined ingredients was entirely on the supplier who had made a low **bid** (出价) on the equipment. The supplier was **rude** (粗鲁的) on the phone, and the chef’s **criterion** (标准) for doing business with him was now clearly unmet. He made a **gradual** (逐渐的) decision to file a **lawsuit** (诉讼), despite his **objection** (反对) to legal battles. His **surname** (姓) was well-known in the food industry, and he didn’t want bad publicity. He decided to **activate** (激活) an emergency plan: he would find another **outlet** (出口) to sell the fish, maybe through the small **tunnel** (隧道) that connected his kitchen to the beach market. He was not **ignorant** (无知的) of the fact that he needed to **squeeze** (挤压) every minute out of the day. He finally managed to **click** (点击) the “confirm order” button on his tablet, and a **reliable** (可靠的) delivery van arrived. The day was saved, and the chef learned never to trust cheap bids again.

**中文翻译**

那位厨师（chef）气坏了。他刚收到一台“抽吸式”（pumping）机器，用来从他那家热带（tropical）岛屿餐厅被淹的地下室里抽水（pump）。这台机器就是个垃圾（junk）——它发出“咔哒咔哒”的点击声（clicking），却根本不肯工作。他必须赶紧把鲜鱼打包（pack），免得坏掉。厨房内部（interior）一片狼藉，这些食材毁掉的责任（liability）完全在于那个供应商——他当初对设备出价（bid）太低。供应商在电话里态度粗鲁（rude），而厨师与他做生意的标准（criterion）显然已经不再达标。他逐渐（gradual）做出了提起诉讼（lawsuit）的决定，尽管他心里对打官司有所反对（objection）。他的姓（surname）在餐饮界很有名，他可不想惹上坏名声。他决定激活（activate）一个应急方案：另找一个出口（outlet）把鱼卖掉，也许可以走那条连接他厨房和海滩市场的小隧道（tunnel）。他并非无知（ignorant）——他很清楚，自己必须把一天里的每一分钟都挤压（squeeze）出来用。最后，他终于成功点击（click）了平板电脑上的“确认订单”按钮，一辆可靠的（reliable）送货卡车到了。这一天总算保住了，而厨师也学到了教训：再也不信便宜的投标。
