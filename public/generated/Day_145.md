**连锁故事 (英文)**

Lily was feeling **pessimistic** (悲观的) as she sat by the window, listening to a sad **tune** (曲调) from her old radio. She had just received a harsh **apology** (道歉) from her boss, but it felt hollow. Outside, the traffic was **loud** (大声的), and she tried to **portray** (描绘) the scene in her sketchbook, but a sudden **burst** (迸发、爆发) of rain ruined her drawing. The **excitement** (兴奋) she had felt about her new job had faded. She remembered her college **geography** (地理) class, where the professor said that rainy days were **prevalent** (普遍的) in this region. She fell into **silence** (沉默), thinking about the **theoretical** (理论上的) gap between dreams and reality. A news report about a **murder** (谋杀) case made her shiver, and she admired the **rigorous** (严格的) work of the police. The **waiter** (服务员) at the café brought her a bowl of **corn** (玉米) soup, but she couldn’t focus on **oneself** (自己) or the food. Her **shadow** (阴影) stretched across the floor as the lamp flickered. She bit her **tongue** (舌头) nervously, wondering why her favorite **hobby** (兴趣) — writing — no longer brought joy. She closed her **laptop** (笔记本电脑), deciding that tomorrow she would face everything with a braver heart.
