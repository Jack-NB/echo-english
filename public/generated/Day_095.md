**连锁故事 (Chain Story):**

I decided to **surround** (周围) myself with fresh energy, so I packed my bag and checked the **bottom** (底下) of my closet for my old hiking boots. The **expansion** (扩张) of my comfort zone was overdue. **Whereas** (然而) my friends preferred city trips, I craved nature. I had a valid **license** (执照、许可) to drive, so I planned a long **journey** (旅行) to the mountains. I could **anticipate** (预期、先发制人) the crisp air already, but I refused to **deprive** (剥夺) myself of this adventure just because of bad weather. My **finance** (财政) was tight, yet I saved enough. The **absence** (缺席) of my usual routine felt liberating. I read the **guideline** (指导方针、指导原则) for mountain safety carefully. My **route** (路线) was marked on the map. **Whenever** (无论何时) I felt tired, I would rest on a **solid** (固体的) rock and **tap** (提取、利用) the energy from my snacks. I told **myself** (我自己、我亲自) this was a test of will. I even carried a small **seed** (种子、播种) to plant at the summit as a symbol. At the top, I saw a group of hikers **protest** (抗议) against a new construction project. I decided to **submit** (顺从、递交) my name to their petition. The wind carried a **musical** (音乐的、音调优美的) hum through the pines. It was the best decision I had made in years.
