**连锁故事 (英文)**

In the first **chapter** (章节) of her life, Emma felt like a mere extra, **merely** (仅仅) watching others achieve greatness. **Meanwhile** (与此同时), her best friend Leo was already an **undergraduate** (本科生) at a top university, where professors would **assign** (分配) challenging projects. Leo’s **mission** (任务) was to study how plants **absorb** (吸收) nutrients, a topic that seemed to **belong** (属于) to no single department. Emma, feeling **weak** (弱的) in comparison, decided to tackle a personal **affair** (事情): publishing her own **poetry** (诗歌). She knew that a **publication** (出版) was not a **necessity** (必要) for happiness, but it was her dream.

To **adjust** (调整) her mindset, she began to **perceive** (察觉) her own talent clearly. She started writing daily, finding **joy** (欢乐) in every line. One day, she received an email that would **announce** (宣布) a poetry contest. The prize was a trip along the famous **highway** (公路) to a literary festival. Emma’s **usual** (通常的) shyness faded, and she submitted her work. The **exposure** (暴露) of her poems to the public was terrifying, yet liberating. As she waited for results, she realized that success was not about being strong, but about facing her own **weakness** (弱点). Finally, her poem won, and she understood that every **assignment** (任务) in life is just a step toward self-discovery.

**中文翻译**

在爱玛生命的第一**章**（章节）里，她觉得自己只是个旁观者，**仅仅**（merely）看着别人成就伟业。**与此同时**（meanwhile），她最好的朋友里奥已经是一所顶尖大学的**本科生**（undergraduate），教授们经常**分配**（assign）具有挑战性的项目。里奥的**任务**（mission）是研究植物如何**吸收**（absorb）营养，这个课题似乎不**属于**（belong）任何单一学科。爱玛觉得自己很**弱**（weak），于是决定处理一件私人**事务**（affair）：出版自己的**诗歌**（poetry）。她知道**出版**（publication）并不是幸福的**必要**（necessity），但这是她的梦想。

为了**调整**（adjust）心态，她开始清晰地**察觉**（perceive）自己的才华。她每天坚持写作，在每一行诗句中找到**欢乐**（joy）。一天，她收到一封邮件，**宣布**（announce）了一场诗歌比赛。奖品是沿着著名的**公路**（highway）前往文学节的旅行。爱玛**通常的**（usual）害羞消失了，她提交了作品。她的诗歌**暴露**（exposure）在公众面前既可怕又令人解脱。在等待结果时，她意识到成功不在于强大，而在于直面自己的**弱点**（weakness）。最终，她的诗获奖了，她明白了人生中的每一项**任务**（assignment）都只是通往自我发现的一步。
