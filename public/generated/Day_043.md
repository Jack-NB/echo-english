## **连锁故事**

The **movement** (运动) of the clouds suggested a change in **weather** (天气). A **mistake** (错误) in the forecast made me **infer** (推断) that rain was coming, so I decided to **enter** (进入) a small cafe. The waiter said there was no **charge** (收费) for water, but my **medicine** (医药) needed to be taken with food. I ordered a sandwich, hoping it would **engage** (吸引) my appetite. The **executive** (经理) of the cafe, a **wide** (宽的) man with a smile, came over to greet me. He spoke about the **variety** (多样化) of their menu, and I felt like I was in a **race** (比赛) to decide what to eat. My main **object** (目标) was to stay dry, but I also needed to **manage** (管理) my time before my appointment at the **hospital** (医院). The nurse had sent an **official** (官方的) notice about my check-up. I had a **huge** (大的) respect for her honesty. She didn't **hire** (雇佣) extra staff, but she always treated everyone like a guest in a **hotel** (酒店). I promised myself to **treat** (对待) her kindly. Outside, the rain had stopped, and the sun broke through the clouds — a perfect ending to a chaotic morning.

## **中文翻译**

云层的**运动**（movement）暗示着**天气**（weather）要变了。天气预报中的一个**错误**（mistake）让我**推断**（infer）要下雨，于是我决定**进入**（enter）一家小咖啡馆。服务员说水不**收费**（charge），但我的**医药**（medicine）需要就着食物吃。我点了一份三明治，希望它能**吸引**（engage）我的食欲。咖啡馆的**经理**（executive），一个**宽**（wide）肩膀的男人，笑着走过来打招呼。他滔滔不绝地讲着菜单的**多样化**（variety），我感觉自己像在参加一场**比赛**（race）来决定吃什么。我的主要**目标**（object）是躲雨，但也需要**管理**（manage）好时间，因为之后要去**医院**（hospital）赴约。护士发了我一份**官方**（official）的体检通知。我对她的诚实怀有**巨大**（huge）的敬意。她没有**雇佣**（hire）更多员工，但总像对待**酒店**（hotel）客人一样对待每个人。我暗暗发誓要好好**对待**（treat）她。外面，雨停了，阳光穿透云层——这个混乱的早晨迎来了完美的结局。
