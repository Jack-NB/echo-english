**The Weary Correspondent’s Roundabout Journey**

The **weary** (疲倦的) young **correspondent** (通讯记者) climbed out of the old **aeroplane** (飞机) at dawn, his bones aching. He had been sent to inspect (检查) a coastal village, but the road was **roundabout** (迂回的), winding through hills. He decided to **abide** (遵守) the local guide’s advice and take a boat **ashore** (上岸) instead. As the boat docked, he noticed a **chimney** (烟囱) rising from a factory, and beside it, a broken **claw** (爪) of a giant **crab** (蟹) washed up on the sand. He nearly stumbled into a **ditch** (沟渠) by the path.

The village was poor, with **cement** (水泥) walls cracked and dry fields that needed **irrigate** (灌溉). The locals were **indignant** (愤慨的) about the government’s neglect. The correspondent counted **innumerable** (无数的) broken tools and measured the **magnitude** (大小) of the damage. He felt a strange **mercury** (水银)like energy in the air — the factory was polluting everything. A **maid** (侍女) showed him a **memorial** (纪念碑) to the village’s lost fishermen. He wrote his report, **irrespective** (不考虑的) of the political risks, and later helped dig a new **ditch** (抛弃) for the old one, hoping to restore the land.

**中文翻译：**

那位疲惫的年轻通讯记者在黎明时分从旧飞机上爬下来，浑身骨头酸痛。他被派去检查一个沿海村庄，但道路迂回曲折，蜿蜒穿山。他决定遵守当地向导的建议，改乘小船上岸。船靠岸时，他注意到工厂里升起一根烟囱，旁边沙滩上有一只巨大的蟹爪被冲上来。他差点被路边的沟渠绊倒。

村庄很贫穷，水泥墙开裂，干涸的田地需要灌溉。当地人对政府的忽视感到愤慨。记者数了无数件破损的工具，测量了损失的规模。他感到空气中有一股水银般的奇异能量——工厂正在污染一切。一个侍女带他看了一座纪念村里失踪渔民的纪念碑。他不考虑政治风险，写下了报道，后来还帮忙挖掘了一条新沟渠来取代旧沟渠，希望恢复土地。
