## 二、连锁故事（英文）

In the moonlit town of Verity, a sudden **raid** (袭击) by masked marauders forced the villagers to seek **refuge** (避难) in the ancient church basement. Their leader, a **resolute** (坚决的) old woman named Marta, refused to flee. She unrolled a worn **rug** (地毯) to cover the cold stone floor, then let out a **sarcastic** (讽刺的) laugh when the intruders demanded gold. "You'll find only **scrap** (废品) here," she said, gesturing to the rusted tools that might **shatter** (粉碎) any hope of treasure.

From the tower above, a guard raised his **trumpet** (喇叭) and blew **aloud** (大声地) to summon help. The sound startled a curious **ape** (猿) that had escaped from a nearby circus, which now swung down and knocked over a stack of **audio** (音频) equipment, sending a deafening screech through the **basement** (地下室). In the chaos, Marta grabbed a bucket of paint and began to **blend** (混合) colors into a thick paste, splashing it onto the floor. The invaders slipped and began to **bound** (跳跃) awkwardly like frogs, their fury a mere **breeze** (微风) compared to the storm Marta was brewing.

She threw a leather **cushion** (垫子) at the chandelier, causing it to swing and **dazzle** ( dazzle) the attackers with reflected candlelight. The distraction gave the villagers time to **deplete** (耗尽) the marauders' arrows by luring them into a narrow corridor. Marta then pulled out an old rotary phone and began to **dial** (拨号) the sheriff's number, her fingers steady despite the **discrepancy** (差异) between her calm expression and her pounding heart.

By dawn, the raiders were captured. The villagers laughed, realizing that what had seemed like a disaster was merely a test of their courage — and that Marta's "scrap" had saved them all.

## 三、中文翻译

在月光照耀的维里蒂小镇，一场突如其来的**袭击**（raid）迫使村民们在古老教堂的地下室寻求**避难**（refuge）。他们的领袖——一位**坚决的**（resolute）老妇人玛尔塔——拒绝逃走。她铺开一张破旧的**地毯**（rug）盖住冰冷的石板地，然后对着闯进来的强盗发出**讽刺的**（sarcastic）笑声。“你们在这儿只能找到**废品**（scrap），”她指着那些锈迹斑斑的工具说，那些东西足以**粉碎**（shatter）任何寻宝的希望。

塔楼上，一名守卫举起**喇叭**（trumpet）**大声地**（aloud）吹响求援信号。声音惊动了一只从附近马戏团逃出来的**猿**（ape），它荡下来撞翻了一堆**音频**（audio）设备，在**地下室**（basement）里发出刺耳的尖啸。混乱中，玛尔塔抓起一桶油漆，把各种颜色**混合**（blend）成浓稠的糊状物，泼在地板上。入侵者们滑倒在地，像青蛙一样笨拙地**跳跃**（bound），他们的愤怒与玛尔塔酝酿的风暴相比，不过是**微风**（breeze）。

她把一个皮革**垫子**（cushion）扔向吊灯，吊灯摇晃起来，反射的烛光**使入侵者眼花**（dazzle）。这一分神给了村民时间，他们引诱强盗进入狭窄的走廊，**耗尽**（deplete）了他们的箭矢。玛尔塔随后掏出一部老式转盘电话，开始**拨号**（dial）给治安官，她的手指稳稳当当，尽管她平静的表情和狂跳的心脏之间存在明显**差异**（discrepancy）。

黎明时分，强盗们全部被俘。村民们笑了，意识到这场看似灾难的事件不过是对他们勇气的考验——而玛尔塔口中的“废品”最终救了所有人。
