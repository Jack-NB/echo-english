**连锁故事**

You must **kill** (杀) your old habits before they **kill** (杀) your dreams. To do that, you need a **specific** (具体的、详细的) plan that **highly** (高度地) focuses on daily action. First, **purchase** (购买) a notebook, because it will become your **director** (指挥者) — guiding every step. This small act is **significant** (有意义的、重要的) because it changes your **feeling** (感受) about progress.

When you lose motivation, **replace** (替代) negative thoughts with a question: “Can **yourself** (你自己、你亲自) do better?” Then **print** (打印) your goal list and stick it on the wall. The **shape** (形状) of your success will slowly appear — like a **thirty** (三十) day calendar marked with small wins. Every morning, stand on solid **ground** (地面、场所) and look at your phone **screen** (屏幕) only after finishing one task. The **struggle** (斗争、奋斗) is real, but you must **lay** (放置) one brick at a time. Remember, even the vast **sea** (大海) was crossed by ships that never **forget** (忘记) their destination. As you **expand** (扩大) your skills, you will finally **cross** (跨过、穿过) the bridge from failure to mastery.
