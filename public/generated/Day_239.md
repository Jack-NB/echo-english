**The Shabby Wolf and the Sturdy Barn**

In a **shabby** (破旧的) old town, a **wolf** (狼) with a **sore** (疼痛的) paw limped toward a **sturdy** (坚固的) **barn** (谷仓). He had tried to **shave** (刮、剃) his fur with a rusty blade, but the cut had become infected, forming a painful **tumor** (肿块). As he approached, he heard a sharp **whistle** (口哨、汽笛) — a farmer’s signal. The wolf, **wary** (机警的) of danger, decided to **spit** (吐出) out the bitter medicine he had been chewing.

He hid behind a **storey** (楼层) of hay bales, watching a **shipment** (装运、货物) of **steak** (牛排) being unloaded. His **toe** (脚趾) caught on a nail, and he yelped. The farmer, a man with a **geometry** (几何) textbook under his arm, turned around. He was a scholar, not a hunter, but his **statute** (法规) allowed him to shoot intruders. The wolf felt a **menace** (威胁) in the air — a **turmoil** (混乱) of fear and hunger.

Suddenly, a **semiconductor** (半导体) chip fell from the farmer’s pocket. The wolf, curious, sniffed it. The farmer laughed and said, “You’re not so fierce.” He measured the distance with a **radius** (半径) of rope, then tossed the wolf a piece of steak. The wolf, still **wary** (机警的), devoured it and fled into the night, his **shabby** (破旧的) coat shining under the moon.

**中文翻译**

在一座破旧的（shabby）老镇上，一只脚趾（toe）受伤、身上有疼痛的（sore）痛处的狼（wolf），一瘸一拐地走向一座坚固的（sturdy）谷仓（barn）。他曾想用生锈的刀片刮（shave）掉自己的毛，但伤口感染了，形成了一个疼痛的肿块（tumor）。走近时，他听到一声尖锐的口哨（whistle）——那是农夫的信号。狼机警地（wary）感到危险，便吐出（spit）了嘴里嚼着的苦药。

他躲在一堆干草垛后面，那草垛堆得有一层楼（storey）高，看着一车装运（shipment）来的牛排（steak）被卸下。他的脚趾（toe）被钉子划破了，痛得叫出声。农夫转过身来，腋下夹着一本几何（geometry）课本。他是个学者，不是猎人，但法规（statute）允许他射杀入侵者。狼感到空气中弥漫着威胁（menace）——那是恐惧与饥饿交织的混乱（turmoil）。

突然，一块半导体（semiconductor）芯片从农夫口袋里掉出来。狼好奇地嗅了嗅。农夫笑了：“你也没那么凶。”他用一根半径（radius）长的绳子量了量距离，然后扔给狼一块牛排。狼仍然机警地（wary）看着，但很快吞下肉，消失在夜色中，他那破旧的（shabby）皮毛在月光下闪着光。
