**Chain Story:**

In the futuristic city of Veridia, **Dr. Lena** was asked to **scan** (扫描) a mysterious artifact. The city's strict **regulation** (规定) required every discovery to be logged. She worked as a **nurse** (护士) by day, but her secret lab at night would **expose** (暴露) the artifact's hidden energy. This strange **being** (生物) inside the crystal seemed to hold the **truth** (真相) about an ancient civilization.

The **distance** (距离) from her lab to the artifact vault was short, but she had to **count** (数数) every step to avoid the motion sensors. A sudden **hit** (打) on the wall made her jump — it was a stray drone. She found a **ticket** (票) stuck in the drone's claw: a warning. She decided to **combine** (结合) the artifact's energy with her own device, but the council had issued a **ban** (禁止) on such experiments.

She took a **bit** (一点) of the energy sample. It seemed **impossible** (不可能的) to harness, yet she knew the **nuclear** (原子的) core inside was the **basis** (基础) of everything. She used a small **boost** (往上推、增加) of voltage to stabilize it. Outside, **rain** (雨) began to fall, washing the city's neon glow. She had to **reserve** (预留) enough power for the final test. Then, her loyal **dog** (狗) barked — the signal that the system was ready.
