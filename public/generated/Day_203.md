## 连锁故事 (English)

On the **eve** (前夕) of the grand ballet (芭蕾舞) premiere, a weary **attendant** (服务员) named Lily was polishing the golden rails of the opera house. Suddenly, she heard a violent **clash** (冲突) coming from the dressing room. A **comic** (喜剧演员) and a costume (服装) designer were arguing over a torn sequin dress.

Lily rushed in and tried to make them **converge** (会聚、集中) on a solution, but their **correspondence** (通信) had broken down completely — they hadn't spoken to each other for weeks. The designer insisted that the **handwriting** (笔迹) on the repair note was not hers. Just then, the director **designate** (指定) for the show entered and called for calm.

"Tonight is not about **invasion** (侵略) of personal space," he said, "but about **procession** (队伍) of dancers moving as one." He pointed to a **portrait** (肖像) of the founder hanging on the wall. "He would **recognise** (意识到、认可) that art demands sacrifice."

The **messenger** (报信者) arrived breathless, warning of a potential **outbreak** (爆发) of flu among the corps. The **jewelry** (珠宝) worn by the lead dancer had also gone missing. A **layoff** (临时解雇、操作停止) of the night staff was now being considered, which could trigger an **explode** (爆炸) of union anger.

Lily, however, had an **oral** (口述的) tradition from her grandmother: "When chaos gathers, let the music lead." She found the missing jewels inside a hollowed book of **handwriting** (笔迹) samples. The **attendant** (服务员) became the hero of the night. The **ballet** (芭蕾舞) proceeded without further **clash** (冲突), and the **comic** (喜剧演员) later joked, "I finally saw a real **converge** (会聚) — of talent and tears!"

## 中文翻译

在盛大芭蕾舞首演的前夕，一位疲惫的服务员莉莉正在擦拭歌剧院的金色栏杆。突然，她听到化妆间传来剧烈的冲突声。一位喜剧演员和一位服装设计师正为一件撕破的亮片裙争吵不休。

莉莉冲进去，试图让他们会聚到一个解决方案上，但他们的通信已经完全破裂——两人已经数周没有说话了。设计师坚持说，维修单上的笔迹不是她的。就在这时，被指定负责这场演出的导演走进来，要求大家冷静。

“今晚不是关于个人空间的侵略，”他说，“而是关于舞者如一人般前进的队伍。”他指着墙上挂着的创始人肖像说：“他会意识到，艺术需要牺牲。”

报信者气喘吁吁地赶来，警告说舞蹈团可能爆发流感，而领舞佩戴的珠宝也不翼而飞。临时解雇夜班工作人员的计划正在酝酿，这很可能引爆工会的怒火。

然而，莉莉有一个来自祖母的口述传统：“当混乱会聚时，让音乐引领。”她在一本空心的笔迹样本书中找到了丢失的珠宝。这位服务员成了当晚的英雄。芭蕾舞在没有进一步冲突的情况下顺利进行，那位喜剧演员后来开玩笑说：“我终于看到了真正的会聚——才华与泪水的交汇！”
