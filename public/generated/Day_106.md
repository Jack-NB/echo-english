**The Chain of the Skyward Shoulder**

Lena, a **keen** (热忱的) young historian, had always dreamed of uncovering a forgotten chapter of her **ethnic** (民族的) community’s past. One rainy afternoon, while cataloging old documents in a dusty archive, she discovered a letter describing a **theft** (偷窃) of ancient relics—a crime that had been **restricted** (限制) from official records. Her **intention** (意图、打算) was clear: she would **resist** (抵挡、抵抗) the temptation to ignore it and instead **compensate** (补偿) for the silence of history by telling the truth.

The letter mentioned a **supreme** (最高的、最优的) judge, a man of legendary **honest** (诚实的) reputation, who had once **admired** (钦佩、羡慕、欣赏) the relics so much that he ordered a **guard** (保卫) to protect them day and night. Yet the thief was clever—he made the soldiers **lean** (倾斜、倚靠、瘦的) against their spears, slipping through their **thin** (薄的、稀薄的) line of defense under the cover of a **sky** (天空) that offered no moonlight. The judge, feeling **anxious** (焦虑的) about the loss, decided to **shoulder** (肩膀) the blame himself, even though his **inner** (内部的、内心的) voice screamed that he was innocent.

Lena felt a surge of **admiration** (钦佩) for this judge. She realized that his **generous** (慷慨的) act of self-sacrifice was not weakness but strength. She wrote a paper arguing that true **democratic** (民主的) justice must allow for such **compensatory** (补偿性的) gestures. Her **keenly** (敏锐地) argued thesis caught the attention of a famous **historian** (历史学家), who praised her **irresistible** (不可抗拒的) logic. Soon, Lena’s **unrestricted** (不受限制的) research reshaped the **historical** (历史的) narrative of her people, turning a forgotten crime into a lesson about **supremacy** (至高无上) of truth over fear.

**中文翻译：**

莉娜是一位热忱的年轻历史学家，一直梦想着揭开自己民族历史中被遗忘的一章。一个雨天的下午，在整理一间布满灰尘的档案馆里的旧文件时，她发现了一封信，信中描述了一起古代文物失窃案——这起罪行被官方记录限制隐瞒了。她的意图很明确：她要抵抗忽视这件事的诱惑，而是通过讲述真相来补偿历史的沉默。

信中提到一位最高的法官，一个以诚实闻名的人物，他曾非常钦佩这些文物，以至于下令让一名卫兵日夜保卫它们。然而小偷很狡猾——他让士兵们倚靠着长矛，趁着没有月光的天空掩护，溜过了他们薄薄的防线。法官对损失感到焦虑，决定自己肩负起责任，尽管他内心深处的声音尖叫着他是无辜的。

莉娜对这位法官涌起一股钦佩之情。她意识到，他慷慨的自我牺牲之举不是软弱，而是力量。她写了一篇论文，论证真正的民主正义必须允许这种补偿性的姿态。她敏锐论证的论文引起了一位著名历史学家的注意，后者称赞了她不可抗拒的逻辑。很快，莉娜不受限制的研究重塑了她民族的历史叙事，将一桩被遗忘的罪行变成了关于真理至高无上于恐惧的一课。
