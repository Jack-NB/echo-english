**连锁故事（英文）**

On a **sunny** morning, a **tail** (尾巴) of a stray cat got stuck in an old **typewriter** (打字机) at the flea market. The cat’s **accent** (口音) was a loud meow, which, in **accordance** (符合、一致) with the shopkeeper’s rules, meant trouble. This was the **anniversary** (周年纪念) of the shop’s opening, and the owner had placed a **banana** (香蕉) peel near the machine as a joke. Suddenly, a **bloom** (花) of dust burst from the keys, and the machine started typing a **catastrophe** (大灾难) story. The shopkeeper couldn’t **certify** (证明) how it happened, but the flying keys began to **collide** (碰撞) with each other, forming a tangled **cord** (绳索) of letters. In **denial** (否认) of the mess, he shouted that his **dormitory** (集体宿舍) days had taught him nothing. Meanwhile, a customer squeezed a **lemon** (柠檬) over the keys, and every **ounce** (盎司) of juice made the letters stick. A **pedestrian** (行人) outside watched the scene, then decided to **sail** (航行、帆) away on a nearby canal boat, eating a **salad** (色拉) from a food stall. The whole **scenario** (场景) was absurd, but it became the best **sightseeing** (游览) attraction of the day.

**中文翻译**

在一个阳光明媚的早晨，一只流浪猫的**尾巴**（tail）卡在了跳蚤市场一台旧**打字机**（typewriter）里。猫的**口音**（accent）是一声响亮的大叫，这**符合**（accordance）店主的规矩，意味着麻烦来了。这天正是店铺开业的**周年纪念**（anniversary），店主开玩笑地在机器旁放了一块**香蕉**（banana）皮。突然，一阵灰尘如**花**（bloom）般从按键中喷出，机器开始自动打出一个关于**大灾难**（catastrophe）的故事。店主无法**证明**（certify）这是怎么发生的，但飞出的按键开始互相**碰撞**（collide），缠成了一条乱糟糟的字母**绳索**（cord）。他**否认**（denial）自己搞砸了，大喊自己的**集体宿舍**（dormitory）岁月什么都没教会他。与此同时，一位顾客把一颗**柠檬**（lemon）挤在按键上，每一**盎司**（ounce）果汁都让字母黏得更紧。窗外的一位**行人**（pedestrian）看着这一幕，决定乘着附近的运河小船**航行**（sail）离开，手里还端着一份小吃摊买的**色拉**（salad）。整个**场景**（scenario）荒诞无比，却成了当天最棒的**游览**（sightseeing）景点。
