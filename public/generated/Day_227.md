**连锁故事**

The **mute** (沉默的) old man, once a **steward** (管家) of a grand estate, sat on a **straw** (稻草) mat outside his **slum** (贫民窟) hut. He watched the city’s **afterward** (后来) chaos unfold. A young chef tried to **roast** (烤) a chicken but made a terrible **blunder** (大错) — he forgot the salt. From a nearby **booth** (电话亭), a man lit a **cigar** (雪茄烟) and called to **commend** (推荐) the chef to a **commonwealth** (联邦) culinary contest. The chef, feeling **angel** (天使)-like luck, began to **congratulate** (祝贺) himself. But a **cricket** (蟋蟀) jumped onto his **elbow** (手肘), startling him. He needed a **diversion** (转移) from the embarrassment, so he joined an **ecology** (生态) club. There, he met a woman who talked about **equity** (公平) in farming and the local **guild** (协会) of bakers. She said his **viable** (可行的) idea of rooftop gardens could win the contest. The old man smiled, his **mute** silence broken by a whisper: “Go.”

**中文翻译**

那个沉默的（mute）老人，曾经是一座大庄园的管家（steward），坐在贫民窟（slum）小屋外的稻草（straw）垫上，看着城市后来（afterward）的混乱。一位年轻厨师想烤（roast）一只鸡，却犯了一个大错（blunder）——忘了放盐。附近电话亭（booth）里，一个男人点起雪茄烟（cigar），打电话推荐（commend）厨师参加联邦（commonwealth）烹饪大赛。厨师觉得自己像天使（angel）一样走运，开始庆祝（congratulate）起来。可一只蟋蟀（cricket）跳上他的手肘（elbow），吓了他一跳。他需要转移（diversion）注意力来化解尴尬，便加入了生态（ecology）俱乐部。在那里，他遇见一位谈论农业公平（equity）和当地面包师协会（guild）的女士。她说他那个屋顶花园的可行（viable）方案能赢得比赛。老人微笑着，沉默（mute）被一句低语打破：“去吧。”
