**连锁故事**

The **sovereign** (君主、至高无上的) of a small island kingdom faced a **turbulent** (动荡的、狂暴的) storm season. His **vicious** (恶毒的) rival spread rumors that he had lost his **ample** (充足的) grain reserves. In truth, every **atom** (原子) of the royal warehouse was still sealed. To calm the people, he hosted a grand **banquet** (宴会) under a **bleak** (萧瑟的、无希望的) grey sky. His speech was **concise** (简明的): "We will not **disperse** (分散) our strength." He announced a **dividend** (被除数、股息) for every farmer, calculated by simple division. A **documentary** (纪录片) crew filmed the event, and the king **exempt** (免除) all fishing taxes for the year. Every **facet** (方面) of his policy was praised. But his **freight** (货运) ships had to **hover** (盘旋) offshore, waiting for the storm to pass. The sailors began to **hum** (哼、嗡嗡声) a war song, which did not **impair** (损害) morale. The king decided to **levy** (征收) a small port fee to rebuild the docks. Meanwhile, on the **mainland** (洲、大陆), the rival's spies reported the king's resilience. By **northeast** (东北) winter, the island was calm, and the **sovereignty** (主权) of the king stood unshaken, his **amplified** (放大的) voice echoing across the waves.

**中文翻译**

一个海岛王国的君主（sovereign）面临着动荡（turbulent）的暴风季节。他恶毒（vicious）的对手散布谣言，说他失去了充足的（ample）粮食储备。事实上，皇家仓库的每一个原子（atom）都完好密封。为安抚民众，他在萧瑟（bleak）的灰天下举办了一场盛大宴会（banquet）。他的演讲简明（concise）：“我们不会分散（disperse）力量。”他宣布给每位农民发放股息（dividend），用简单的除法计算。一个纪录片（documentary）摄制组拍摄了现场，国王还免除（exempt）了当年所有捕鱼税。他政策的每一个方面（facet）都受到称赞。但货运（freight）船只能在近海盘旋（hover），等待风暴过去。水手们开始哼（hum）起战歌，这并没有损害（impair）士气。国王决定征收（levy）一笔小额港口费来重建码头。与此同时，在大陆（mainland）上，对手的间谍报告了国王的韧性。到东北（northeast）的冬天，岛屿恢复了平静，国王的主权（sovereignty）坚不可摧，他放大的（amplified）声音在海浪上回荡。
