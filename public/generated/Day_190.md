**英文连锁故事（约 420 字）**

The young geologist (地质学家) Dr. Lin was sent to **quantify** (量化) the mineral deposits near a remote town. She first needed to check the **specifications** (规格) of her equipment, then **situate** (坐落在) her base camp on a flat plateau. Her **respective** (各自的) tasks were divided with her assistant: she would map the rocks, he would collect samples. As they worked, a sudden **riot** (骚乱、暴乱) broke out in the nearby village over a mining dispute. Lin decided to **skate** (滑冰) across the frozen river to avoid the chaos, but she slipped and took a nasty **tumble** (暴跌、跌倒), landing face-first in the snow. Wiping the **sweat** (汗水) from her brow, she realized her field notebook was lost. Using a **graph** (图表) she had drawn earlier, she tried to **infer** (推断) the missing data — a pure **inference** (推理) based on the **analogy** (类比) with a previous site. Meanwhile, a local **rapper** (说唱歌手) mocked her plight in a **rap** (说唱音乐、轻敲) about clumsy scientists. She ignored him and focused on the **succession** (连续) of rock layers. The **monarch** (君主) of the region, a kind old king, heard of her trouble and sent a **transit** (运输) truck to carry her equipment. But the truck broke down, and a **multitude** (大量、多数) of villagers came to help push it. One man offered her a leaf of **coca** (古柯植物) — she refused, knowing it could be processed into **cocaine** (可卡因). Instead, she drank water and began to **forge** (伪造、建立) a new map from memory. It was hard, and at one point she felt **despair** (绝望) creep in. But her assistant returned with the lost notebook, and together they completed the survey. That night, she looked at the stars and smiled — her **geological** (地质的) career was just beginning.

**中文翻译**

年轻的地质学家林博士被派往一个偏远小镇附近，去**量化**当地的矿藏储量。她首先需要检查设备的**规格**，然后把营地**坐落在**一块平坦的高原上。她和助手**各自**的任务分工明确：她负责绘制岩石图，他负责采集样本。就在工作时，附近村庄因采矿纠纷爆发了一场**骚乱**。林决定**滑冰**穿过结冰的河流以避开混乱，却脚下一滑，重重地**跌倒**，脸朝下摔进雪里。她擦去额头上的**汗水**，发现野外笔记本丢了。她借助之前画的一张**图表**，试图**推断**丢失的数据——这是一种纯粹基于与先前地点**类比**的**推理**。与此同时，一位当地**说唱歌手**用一首**说唱**嘲笑她这个笨手笨脚的科学家。她不予理会，专注于岩层的**连续**分布。该地区的**君主**——一位仁慈的老国王——听说她的困境后，派了一辆**运输**卡车来运送她的设备。但卡车中途抛锚，**大量**村民赶来帮忙推车。一个男人递给她一片古柯叶——她拒绝了，因为她知道那可能被加工成**可卡因**。她改喝了口水，开始凭记忆**建立**一张新地图。这很艰难，有一刻她感到**绝望**涌上心头。但助手带着丢失的笔记本回来了，两人一起完成了勘测。那天夜里，她仰望星空，微微一笑——她的**地质**生涯才刚刚开始。
