## 2. 连锁故事 (英文)

At the **frontier** (边境) of the small kingdom, a young navigator named Leo was struggling to **steer** (引导) his **vessel** (船) through a dense fog. He had spent weeks trying to **compute** (估计) the exact distance to the shore, but every calculation seemed **faulty** (有缺陷的). The crew was beginning to **starve** (挨饿), and their morale was sinking fast.

Suddenly, Leo realized that the fog was an **illusion** (错觉)—a trick of the light reflecting off the ice. He shouted a **slogan** (口号) to his men: “Trust the compass, not your eyes!” This **decisive** (决定性的) moment changed everything. He recalled a **seemingly** (表面上) trivial **episode** (情节) from his childhood: his grandfather had taught him to read the stars, a skill he had almost forgotten.

That night, a rare **occurrence** (发生) took place: the clouds parted, revealing the North Star. Leo felt a **superiority** (优势) over the sea at last. He used the star as a **spectator** (观众) of his own courage, guiding the ship **beside** (在…旁边) the jagged rocks. The **indication** (迹象) of land was unmistakable—the scent of pine trees and the faint glow of a lighthouse.

He opened the **refrigerator** (冰箱) in the galley, hoping to find something to **tempt** (引诱) the crew’s appetite. Inside, there was only a half-rotten fish. But Leo’s **awake** (醒着的) mind knew that hope, not food, would save them. As dawn broke, he **awakened** his men with a smile: “We are **beside** ourselves with joy—land ho!”

The journey proved that even the smallest **trivial** (琐碎的) detail—a star, a scent, a memory—could become the **decisive** (决定性的) force in a man’s life. And the **indication** (迹象) of their success was not the treasure they found, but the **superiority** (优势) they had won over their own fear.

## 3. 中文翻译

在小王国的边境（frontier），一位名叫利奥的年轻航海者正努力在浓雾中驾驶（steer）他的船（vessel）。他花了好几周时间试图估计（compute）到岸边的确切距离，但每一次计算似乎都有缺陷（faulty）。船员们开始挨饿（starve），士气急剧低落。

突然，利奥意识到这片浓雾是一种错觉（illusion）——是光线在冰面上反射造成的把戏。他向手下们喊出一条口号（slogan）：“相信罗盘，别相信眼睛！”这个决定性的（decisive）时刻改变了一切。他回想起童年时一个表面上（seemingly）琐碎的（trivial）情节（episode）：祖父曾教他读星星，而这项技能他几乎已经遗忘。

那天夜里，发生（occurrence）了一件罕见的事：云层散开，露出了北极星。利奥终于感到自己在大海上占据了优势（superiority）。他把星星当作自己勇气的观众（spectator），引导船只沿着嶙峋的礁石旁边（beside）行驶。陆地出现的迹象（indication）不容置疑——松树的气息和灯塔微弱的光晕。

他打开厨房里的冰箱（refrigerator），希望能找到点东西引诱（tempt）船员们的食欲。里面只有半条腐烂的鱼。但利奥清醒的（awake）头脑知道，能救他们的不是食物，而是希望。黎明破晓时，他带着微笑唤醒（awakened）了船员们：“我们欣喜若狂——陆地到了！”

这次旅程证明，即使是最琐碎的（trivial）细节——一颗星、一缕气味、一段记忆——也能成为人一生中决定性的（decisive）力量。他们成功的迹象（indication）不是找到的宝藏，而是他们战胜自身恐惧后赢得的优势（superiority）。
