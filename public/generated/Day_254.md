**连锁故事（英文）**

On the foggy harbor, the old captain adjusted the **tariff** (关税) documents while sipping a glass of **whisky** (威士忌酒). His crew was about to **unload** (卸下、卸货) a shipment of **lumber** (木材) wrapped in **waterproof** (防水材料) sheets. Suddenly, a **twinkle** (闪烁) of light appeared from the distant lighthouse — a signal from the customs officer. The captain felt a **timid** (胆怯的) shiver, for he knew the **warranty** (保修期) on his cargo had expired. He quickly put on his **veil** (面纱) of calmness, hiding his anxiety. On the dock, a young woman in a **volleyball** (排球) uniform was practicing her serves, oblivious to the tension. Nearby, a mechanic was checking a huge **coil** (圈、线圈) of rope, muttering about the **thermal** (热量的、热的) engine problems. The captain walked to the officer and made an **enquiry** (咨询) about the delay. The officer, with a smile of **hypocrisy** (伪善), suggested a bribe. The captain refused, feeling **amazed** (吃惊、惊讶) at such corruption. He knew that true integrity was **wholesome** (有益健康的) for the soul. As he turned away, a young sailor offered him a piece of bread with **bait** (饵) — a joke about fishing. The captain laughed, then decided to **invert** (颠倒、反转) the cargo order to avoid inspection. But the officer had already called for backup. The captain realized he needed to **interface** (接口) with the port authority via radio. Just then, a dignified **madam** (夫人) stepped forward, waving a legal order. She was the port director, and she **unveiled** (揭幕) the officer’s scam. The captain thanked her, and the **lumber** (木材) was finally cleared. As the sun rose, the captain smiled, knowing that honesty always wins. He raised his glass of **whisky** (威士忌酒) to the new day, while the young woman’s **volleyball** (排球) bounced cheerfully across the deck.

**中文翻译**

在雾气弥漫的港口，老船长一边整理着关税（tariff）文件，一边啜饮着一杯威士忌酒（whisky）。他的船员正准备卸下（unload）一批用防水材料（waterproof）包裹的木材（lumber）。突然，远处的灯塔闪烁（twinkle）出一道亮光——那是海关官员的信号。船长感到一阵胆怯的（timid）战栗，因为他知道货物的保修期（warranty）已经过期。他迅速戴上一层面纱（veil）般的冷静，掩饰内心的焦虑。码头上，一个穿着排球（volleyball）队服的年轻女子正在练习发球，对紧张的气氛浑然不觉。不远处，一名机械师正在检查一大圈（coil）绳索，嘟囔着关于热量（thermal）发动机的问题。船长走向官员，就延误事宜提出咨询（enquiry）。官员带着伪善（hypocrisy）的微笑，暗示他行贿。船长拒绝了，对如此腐败感到吃惊（amaze）。他知道，真正的正直对灵魂是有益健康的（wholesome）。当他转身离开时，一名年轻水手递给他一块上面放着饵（bait）的面包——那是个关于钓鱼的玩笑。船长笑了，然后决定颠倒（invert）货物顺序以避开检查。但官员已经呼叫了增援。船长意识到他需要通过无线电与港务局接口（interface）联系。就在这时，一位庄重的夫人（madam）走上前来，挥舞着一份法律文件。她是港口主管，她揭穿了官员的骗局。船长感谢了她，木材（lumber）终于顺利通关。当太阳升起时，船长微笑着，深知诚实总能获胜。他举起威士忌酒（whisky）杯，迎接新的一天，而年轻女子的排球（volleyball）在甲板上欢快地弹跳着。
