**连锁故事 (Chain Story)**

On his **ninety**th (九十) birthday, old Professor Keller sat on his porch, watching the **plentiful** (丰富的) autumn harvest sway in the wind. He remembered how his life seemed to **revolve** (围绕) around one single question: whose ideas were **theirs** (他们的) and whose were truly his own. In his youth, he had sought **harmony** (和谐) between tradition and innovation, but a sudden **breakdown** (崩溃、故障) of his car on a lonely road taught him to **dash** (猛冲) forward without fear. When bandits appeared, he had to **flee** (逃跑) into the woods, hiding behind an old **rubber** (橡胶) tree. Despite the danger, he kept his **humorous** (幽默的) spirit, joking that his emergency **kit** (装备) contained only a **magnify** (放大)ing glass and a rusty compass. Years later, as a young soldier, he learned that the **senate** (参议院) had declared war; at just **sixteen** (十六), he felt the world **spin** (旋转) around him. His unit drove an old **tank** (坦克、水箱) across muddy fields, and he became **thoughtful** (深思熟虑的) about the cost of conflict. After the war, he met a **veteran** (老兵) who showed him a different **angle** (角) on life — not every battle is won with force. That lesson made him **brave** (勇敢的) enough to become a teacher, passing on wisdom instead of wounds.

**中文翻译**

在九十岁生日那天，老教授凯勒坐在门廊上，望着风中摇曳的丰富秋收。他想起自己的人生似乎始终围绕着一个问题：哪些想法是他们的，哪些才是真正属于自己的。年轻时，他追求传统与创新之间的和谐，但一次汽车在荒路上的突然故障教会了他无畏地猛冲向前。当强盗出现时，他不得不逃进树林，躲在一棵老橡胶树后面。尽管身处危险，他仍保持幽默的精神，开玩笑说他的应急装备里只有一枚放大镜和一个生锈的指南针。多年后，作为一名年轻士兵，他得知参议院已宣战；年仅十六岁的他感到世界在旋转。他的部队开着一辆旧坦克穿过泥泞的田野，而他对战争的代价变得深思熟虑。战争结束后，他遇到一位老兵，老兵向他展示了生活的另一个角度——并非每场战斗都要靠武力取胜。那个教训让他足够勇敢，选择成为一名教师，传递智慧而非伤痕。
