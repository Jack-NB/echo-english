**连锁故事 (English Story)**

On a cold winter morning, **poverty** (贫穷) forced young Tom to work at a tiny diner in the **south** (南、南方的) of the city. He wanted to **advance** (前进、提高) his life, so he saved every coin he earned. The **smoke** (烟) from the kitchen made his eyes water, but he stayed **practical** (实际的) and focused. His **male** (男性、男性的) boss, a tough old man, ran a **commercial** (商业的) business that sold cheap meals to truck drivers. Tom often felt his **peer** (凝视、同伴、贵族) workers staring at him, and he feared a sudden **attack** (攻击) from the cruel kitchen manager. He dreamed of a **holiday** (假日) by the sea, but his **limited** (有限的、有限责任的) salary made that impossible. Every day, he had to **push** (推) heavy carts of vegetables, and by the time he turned **twenty** (二十), he was exhausted. One night, the **police** (警察) raided the diner for serving spoiled food. Tom was questioned, but he told them the truth. A kind detective suggested he watch an old **film** (电影) about a poor boy who became rich — it gave him hope. That night, Tom wrote a **review** (复习、回顾、评论) of his own life, deciding to **attempt** (试图、企图) a new path. He studied at night, slowly building **wealth** (财富) through small investments. Eventually, he learned to **associate** (联系、结交) with honest mentors, and he began to **prefer** (偏好) books over gossip. Ten years later, Tom owned three restaurants. He never forgot his roots, and he hired young people from **poverty** (贫穷) — because he knew that a single spark of courage could change everything.

**中文翻译**

在一个寒冷的冬日早晨，贫穷（poverty）迫使年轻的汤姆在城南（south）一家小餐馆打工。他想前进（advance）自己的人生，所以把赚到的每一分钱都存起来。厨房里的烟（smoke）熏得他眼睛发酸，但他保持实际（practical）并专注。他的男性（male）老板是个顽固的老人，经营着一家商业（commercial）的餐馆，专门给卡车司机卖便宜饭菜。汤姆常常感到同伴（peer）们在凝视他，他害怕那个凶恶的厨房经理会突然攻击（attack）他。他梦想着去海边度假（holiday），但他有限的（limited）工资让这不可能实现。每天他都要推（push）沉重的菜车，到二十岁（twenty）时，他已经筋疲力尽。一天晚上，警察（police）因出售变质食物突袭了这家餐馆。汤姆被盘问，但他说了真话。一位好心的警探建议他看一部关于穷小子变富翁的老电影（film）——这给了他希望。那晚，汤姆写下对自己生活的回顾（review），决心试图（attempt）走一条新路。他晚上学习，通过小额投资慢慢积累财富（wealth）。最终，他学会了与诚实的导师结交（associate），并开始偏好（prefer）书籍而非闲谈。十年后，汤姆拥有三家餐厅。他从未忘记自己的根，并且雇佣那些贫穷（poverty）的年轻人——因为他知道，一星勇气的火花就能改变一切。
