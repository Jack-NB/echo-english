**连锁故事**

The old professor sat (坐在) in his study, trying to **seek** (寻求) a way to explain **everything** (每件事) about human nature. He received a **message** (消息) from his former student, who now worked **per** (每、经) contract for a global charity. The student wanted to **promote** (促进) a new project, but he couldn’t **figure** (算计、人物、数字) out the budget. The professor knew the **road** (路) to success was full of **limit** (限制) — **sometimes** (有时) you have to accept **fear** (恐惧) and move on. He decided to **supply** (供应) both advice and encouragement. Their **argument** (争论、论据) about strategy **related** (讲述、使相互关联) to a deeper question: what makes an **effective** (有效的) leader? The professor warned him to **prevent** (阻止) the common **pattern** (模式、图案) of over-planning. “Treat the project like a **baby** (宝贝),” he wrote. “**Search** (搜索) for its core value, and let the idea grow by **itself** (它自己).” The student smiled, realizing that the professor’s wisdom was **everywhere** (到处) — in **every** lesson, in **every** failure, and in **every** small success. He **performed** (执行) the plan with **fearless** (无畏的) confidence, and soon the project **transfigured** (变形，美化) into a **remarkable** (非凡的) achievement.

**中文翻译**
