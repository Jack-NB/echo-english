**Chain Story**

In a small village, an old man uncovered a family **heritage** (遗产) hidden beneath a dusty **mask** (面具) carved from ancient wood. He threw on his worn **coat** (外套) and walked into the **countryside** (农村), where the morning air was full of **dynamic** (动力、动态的) energy. He studied the **format** (版式、设计) of an old letter, which seemed **secondary** (次要的) to the real treasure — a **coin** (硬币) stamped with a strange design. Beside the coin lay a **deposit** (存款) receipt from a forgotten bank. He decided to **install** (安装) a small safe to protect it, feeling a **tremendous** (巨大的) sense of excitement.

His **voluntary** (自愿的) decision to share the discovery brought **accuracy** (精确度) to the village’s history. The coin was made of pure **silver** (银), and its shine removed the **shame** (羞愧) of past poverty. With **patience** (耐心), historians examined its **ambiguous** (模棱两可的) markings, which made some people **suspicious** (可疑的) of a forgery. But the old man remained **polite** (礼貌的), and his **courage** (勇气) inspired everyone to accept the truth.
