**连锁故事 (英文)**

The mayor (市长) was about to **kick** (踢) a stone in frustration when his phone rang. It was the hospital **ward** (病房) doctor. A patient had nearly **drown** (淹没) in a flooded basement, and now needed a **timely** (及时的) decision. The mayor rushed to the **cottage** (村舍) area where the flood had hit. He saw **cattle** (牛) stranded on a hill, and workers trying to **wrap** (包裹) sandbags. The city’s **altitude** (高度) made drainage difficult. He realized that his **subjective** (主观的) optimism had delayed action. Taking every **precaution** (预防), he ordered the team to **allocate** (分配) rescue boats. The **empire** (帝国) of his political career felt **tense** (紧张的) — **versus** (对抗) the chaos of nature. His **landlord** (房东) cousin, who owned the flooded buildings, came to **beg** (乞求) for help. The mayor felt **glad** (高兴的) that no lives were lost, but his **ignorance** (无知) of flood risks had been exposed. He promised a **prosperous** (繁荣的) recovery plan, and the city began to look **timely** (及时的) again.

**中文翻译**

市长正要沮丧地**踢**（kick）一块石头时，电话响了。是医院**病房**（ward）的医生打来的。一名病人在被水淹没的地下室里差点**淹死**（drown），现在急需一个**及时的**（timely）决定。市长冲到受灾的**村舍**（cottage）区域，看到**牛**（cattle）被困在山坡上，工人们正忙着**包裹**（wrap）沙袋。城市的**海拔**（altitude）使得排水十分困难。他意识到自己**主观的**（subjective）乐观延误了行动。他采取了每一项**预防**（precaution）措施，命令团队**分配**（allocate）救援船。他政治生涯的“**帝国**”（empire）此刻感到**紧张**（tense）——正**对抗**（versus）着自然的混乱。他那拥有被淹建筑的**房东**（landlord）表哥跑来**乞求**（beg）帮助。市长感到**高兴**（glad）的是没有人员伤亡，但他对洪水风险的**无知**（ignorance）已经暴露无遗。他承诺制定一个**繁荣的**（prosperous）重建计划，城市也重新变得**及时**（timely）高效起来。
