## 2. 连锁故事（英文）

As he spoke, an elderly woman whispered, “We cannot let this **die** (死) down. The crowd applauded. The mayor, now convinced, decided to **maintain** (保持、维持) a weekly review and **attend** (出席、照看) every session personally.
