**连锁故事**

In a small coastal town, a **duplicate** (重复) of an old map was found, showing a hidden island. To reach it, they had to cross a **ferry** (渡船), but the journey was delayed because the engine would **evaporate** (蒸发) its fuel too fast. On the ferry, passengers exchanged **gossip** (闲话) about a local hero, a **hopeful** (有希望的) young sailor named Tom. Tom decided to **immerse** (浸) himself in the study of navigation, hoping to **impart** (传授) his knowledge to others. But a rival captain tried to **intimidate** (恐吓、威胁) him, saying the island was cursed. Tom stayed **neat** (整洁的) in his uniform, refusing to **omit** (省略) any safety check. He knew that if he worked hard, he would **prosper** (成功). Fresh water was **scarce** (稀少的、罕见的) on board, and one night a sudden **thunder** (闪电) storm struck, causing the **timber** (木材) deck to shake. Tom grabbed an **accessory** (配件) tool kit, feeling a deep **awe** (敬畏) for the ocean's power. After the storm, he took a quick **bath** (洗澡) in the rain, while a cruel bully tried to **bully** (欺负) the new crew. Tom stood firm, and the **carriage** (运输、四轮马车) of supplies was secured. Finally, they reached the island, where a giant stone **cube** (立方) marked the hidden treasure. Tom's courage made him a legend.

**中文翻译**

在一个沿海小镇，有人发现了一张旧地图的**重复**（duplicate）副本，显示了一座隐藏的岛屿。要到达那里，他们必须乘**渡船**（ferry）过海，但旅程被耽搁了，因为发动机的燃料**蒸发**（evaporate）得太快。渡船上，乘客们互相传播着关于一位本地英雄——一位**有希望的**（hopeful）年轻水手汤姆的**闲话**（gossip）。汤姆决定**浸**（immerse）身于航海学的研究，希望把自己的知识**传授**（impart）给别人。但一位对手船长试图**恐吓、威胁**（intimidate）他，说那座岛被诅咒了。汤姆保持**整洁的**（neat）制服，拒绝**省略**（omit）任何安全检查。他知道只要努力工作，自己就会**成功**（prosper）。船上的淡水**稀少的、罕见的**（scarce），一天夜里突然一场**闪电**（thunder）风暴袭来，使得**木材**（timber）甲板剧烈摇晃。汤姆抓起一个**配件**（accessory）工具箱，对海洋的力量感到深深的**敬畏**（awe）。风暴过后，他在雨中匆匆**洗澡**（bath），一个残忍的恶霸试图**欺负**（bully）新船员。汤姆坚定地站着，确保了**运输、四轮马车**（carriage）物资的安全。最终，他们到达了岛屿，一块巨大的石头**立方**（cube）标记着隐藏的宝藏。汤姆的勇气使他成为了传奇。
