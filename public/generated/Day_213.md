**连锁故事**

Under the dense **vegetation** (植物) of a forgotten island, Dr. Elena, an **astronomy** (天文学) professor, had set up a makeshift observatory. She had to **chop** (砍) through thick vines every morning just to reach her telescope. Her scientific **doctrine** (学说、教义) insisted that every star had a hidden pattern, but her colleagues’ **indignation** (愤怒) at her unorthodox methods was growing. Elena remained **prudent** (谨慎的), knowing that her stubborn **disposition** (倾向、安排、性情) often **perplexed** (使困惑、使复杂化) others, yet she never let that **anchor** (锚) her down.

One night, she spotted a signal that made her **damn** (谴责、该死的) her own eyes. Her **detector** (探测器) beeped wildly, and with **earnest** (认真) concentration, she recorded the data. Her **feeble** (虚弱的) flashlight flickered as she sketched a new **galaxy** (星系) on her notepad. “**Hello** (你好), universe,” she whispered, with a touch of **irony** (反讽) — she had spent years chasing this moment, and now it felt like a **mold** (模子、模型) had been broken. The authorities, however, wanted to **prosecute** (起诉) her for trespassing on protected land, calling her a **savage** (野蛮人、凶猛的) who ignored rules. But Elena knew that true discovery required constant **surveillance** (监视) of the sky, not of human laws.

**中文翻译**

在一片密不透风的**植物**（vegetation）之下，天文学（astronomy）教授埃琳娜博士搭建了一个临时观测站。她每天早晨都要**砍**（chop）开粗壮的藤蔓才能到达望远镜前。她的科学**学说**（doctrine）坚信每颗星星都有隐藏的规律，但同事们对她非正统方法的**愤怒**（indignation）与日俱增。埃琳娜保持**谨慎**（prudent），她知道自己的固执**倾向**（disposition）常常**使**别人**困惑**（perplex），但她从不让自己被这些**锚**（anchor）所束缚。

一天夜里，她发现了一个信号，让她忍不住**谴责**（damn）自己的眼睛。她的**探测器**（detector）疯狂鸣叫，她以**认真**（earnest）的态度记录数据。**虚弱**的**（feeble）**手电筒闪烁不定，她在笔记本上勾画出一个新的**星系**（galaxy）。“**你好**（hello），宇宙，”她低声说，带着一丝**反讽**（irony）——她花了数年追逐这一刻，如今却感觉一个**模子**（mold）被打破了。而当局却想以擅闯保护区为由**起诉**（prosecute）她，称她是个无视规则的**野蛮人**（savage）。但埃琳娜知道，真正的发现需要的是对天空的持续**监视**（surveillance），而不是对人类律法的遵从。
