**连锁故事：**

It was a damp **moist** (潮湿的) evening when the **jazz** (爵士乐) club at the **junction** (连接) of two narrow streets began to fill. A **missionary** (传教士) named Father Joe, who had just returned from abroad, sat in the corner, his **naked** (裸体的) wrist showing a cheap watch. He was not there to preach but to meet an old friend. Outside, a **postman** (邮递员) named Sam was delivering a last letter, his **overcoat** (大衣) soaked by the drizzle. Suddenly, a shout broke the rhythm: a man accused another of **plagiarism** (剽窃) over a song. The accused pleaded for **pardon** (原谅), but the crowd showed no **pity** (同情). In the chaos, a **kidnap** (绑架) attempt was foiled by Sam, who threw his mailbag like a **loop** (环) around the culprit’s legs. The **plight** (困境) of the victim was over, and a **prototype** (原型) of a new alarm was praised. Meanwhile, Father Joe realized his **portfolio** (投资组合、作品集、文件夹) of sermons had been left at the train station. He rushed out, but the **overflow** (溢出) of rainwater had flooded the road. He arrived late, but the stationmaster was **punctual** (准时的) and had kept the folder safe. Yet, the whole city was **redundant** (解雇的) with news of the event, and by dawn, the story had grown **rotten** (腐烂的) with exaggeration. Sam, now a hero, wrapped his **scarf** (围巾) tighter and smiled. The **missionary**’s **mission** was complete, though he had missed his train. The **junction** where it all happened became a legend, and the **jazz** played on, **jazzy** and bright, until the **moisture** (水分) evaporated in the morning sun.

**中文翻译：**

那是一个潮湿的傍晚，两条窄街交汇处的爵士乐俱乐部开始热闹起来。一位刚从国外回来的传教士乔神父坐在角落里，裸露的手腕上戴着一块廉价手表。他不是来布道的，而是来见一位老朋友。外面，邮递员山姆正在送最后一封信，他的大衣被细雨浸透了。突然，一声喊叫打破了节奏：一个人指控另一个人剽窃了一首歌。被指控者请求原谅，但人群毫无同情。混乱中，山姆扔出邮包，像一条环一样缠住了绑匪的腿，制止了一起绑架企图。受害者的困境结束了，一种新警报器的原型得到了称赞。与此同时，乔神父意识到自己装有布道稿的作品集落在了火车站。他冲了出去，但溢出的雨水已淹没了道路。他迟到了，但站长很准时，保管好了文件夹。然而，整个城市都在喋喋不休地报道这件事，到了黎明，故事已经因夸张而变得腐烂不堪。山姆成了英雄，他紧了紧围巾，笑了。传教士的使命完成了，尽管他错过了火车。发生这一切的那个交汇处成了传奇，爵士乐继续演奏着，花哨而明亮，直到晨光中水分蒸发殆尽。
