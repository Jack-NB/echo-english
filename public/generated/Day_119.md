**The Chain Story**

In a city where a **bomb** (炸弹) had once shattered the peace, a young chemist named Leo was testing a new **soda** (苏打) formula that bubbled with **random** (随机的) energy. His lab’s **output** (产量、输出) of experimental fizz had grown so large that he needed to **prolong** (延长) his work hours, but he lacked his supervisor’s **approval** (赞成) for overtime. After a quick **extension** (延伸) of his shift request, he was told that an emergency **surgery** (外科手术) at the nearby hospital required his presence—his sister was the patient. He had to **shut** (关上) his equipment, and as the alarm began to **ring** (响起), he rushed to the hospital’s rooftop **platform** (平台) where a helicopter waited.

The pilot tried to **enforce** (强迫) safety rules, but Leo’s phone **battery** (电池) died, causing great **convenience** (便利) loss—he couldn’t call anyone. He noticed a **silicon** (硅) chip embedded in his coat, which reminded him of a previous experiment that had **impress** (留下印象)ed his professor. His **hunger** (饥饿、渴望) for knowledge and his sister’s survival drove him forward. He grabbed a handful of **rice** (稻、大米) from his pocket, a symbol of his rural childhood, and prayed this wasn’t a **tragedy** (悲剧). Finally, a **policeman** (警察) arrived with a spare charger, and Leo was able to coordinate the rescue. His sister survived, and the city’s **bomb** (炸弹) scare turned out to be a false alarm—just a faulty **soda** (苏打) machine. From then on, every **random** (随机的) event in his life reminded him that even chaos could be turned into **output** (产量、输出) of hope, **prolong** (延长)ed by courage, and sealed with **approval** (赞成) of fate. The **extension** (延伸) of that day’s hours became his new **surgery** (外科手术) on the mind, where he learned to **shut** (关上) fear, let courage **ring** (响起), and stand on the **platform** (平台) of resilience. He never let **enforce** (强迫)ment break him, kept his **battery** (电池) of compassion charged, valued every **convenience** (便利) of friendship, and found that **silicon** (硅) valleys could **impress** (留下印象) even the hardest hearts. His **hunger** (饥饿、渴望) for life was stronger than any **rice** (稻、大米) field memory, and he vowed that no **tragedy** (悲剧) would define him—because a **policeman** (警察) once told him, “The city’s greatest **bomb** (炸弹) is an unspoken story.”

**中文翻译**
