**连锁故事**

Professor Aldric, a **dominant** (主导的) figure in the field of linguistics, spent his whole life studying the **domain** (领域) of ancient languages. One morning, he received an **extravagant** (奢侈的) invitation from a mysterious collector, offering him a rare manuscript. Aldric packed his bag, ignoring the **flock** (一群) of curious pigeons outside his window. The manuscript turned out to be a **hybrid** (杂种) text, mixing Greek and Sanskrit — a discovery of **infinite** (无限) value. Yet, the professor felt that the text’s **intrinsic** (内在的) beauty mattered more than its monetary worth. He called it a true **masterpiece** (杰作). But the collector, a man of **vulgar** (粗俗的) taste, only cared about selling it. Aldric refused, and soon the news spread like **aviation** (航空) — fast and unstoppable. His **gentle** (温和的) refusal turned into a scandal. The press dragged his name through the **mud** (泥), and his quiet life was shattered. He decided to escape, using an old map for **navigation** (导航), heading toward a coastal town. On the train, he noticed a **ribbon** (缎带) tied around a suitcase — the same color as the collector’s scarf. A cold **thread** (线) of fear ran through him. His head began to **ache** (疼). Rumors began to **circulate** (循环、流通) that he had stolen the manuscript. He started to **cough** (咳) nervously on the dusty train. When he finally reached the harbor, he stood on the ship’s **deck** (甲板), clutching his precious **diploma** (文凭) from Oxford — his only proof of identity. The sea stretched before him, endless and calm. He realized that true knowledge never needs validation from the vulgar crowd.

**中文翻译**

阿尔德里克教授是语言学领域的一位**主导**人物，毕生研究古语言的**领域**。一天早晨，他收到一封**奢侈的**邀请，来自一位神秘的收藏家，要献给他一份珍贵手稿。他收拾好行李，无视窗外一群好奇的鸽子。那手稿竟是一部**杂种**文本，混合了希腊语和梵语——具有**无限**的价值。然而教授觉得，文本的**内在的**美远比金钱重要。他称之为真正的**杰作**。但那位品味**粗俗的**收藏家只想卖掉它。阿尔德里克拒绝了，消息像**航空**一样飞速传播。他**温和的**拒绝变成了一桩丑闻。媒体把他的名字拖进**泥**里，他平静的生活被粉碎了。他决定逃跑，用一张旧地图进行**导航**，前往一座海滨小镇。火车上，他注意到一个手提箱上系着**缎带**——颜色和收藏家的围巾一模一样。一条恐惧的**线**穿过他的身体。他的头开始**疼**。谣言开始**循环、流通**，说他偷了手稿。他在尘土飞扬的火车上紧张地**咳**了起来。终于到达港口时，他站在船的**甲板**上，紧握着他牛津大学的**文凭**——他身份的唯一证明。大海在他面前无尽而平静地延展。他意识到，真正的知识从不需要粗俗之人的认可。
