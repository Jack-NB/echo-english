**连锁故事**

The judge claimed **jurisdiction** (管辖权) over the case, but the defendant's lawyer looked at him with **scorn** (轻蔑、鄙视), then gave a **shove** (推) to the courtroom door and left. Outside, a **tram** (有轨电车) rattled by, its windows fogged with **nitrogen** (氮) from the cold air. The lawyer boarded and thought of the **abound** (充满) injustices in the city. He wrapped his **cape** (披肩) tighter, feeling like a **cherry** (樱桃) destined to be crushed. He reached the **dock** (码头), where an **eminent** (著名的) professor was waiting, known for his **rhetoric** (修辞) skills. "The **ending** (结局) of this **epoch** (时代) is near," the professor said, stroking his **jaw** (下巴). "We need **purify** (涤罪) the legal system." The lawyer nodded. "But my hands are tied with a **rope** (绳索) of bureaucracy," he replied. The professor laughed, then added **pepper** (辣椒) to his soup from a nearby stall. The lawyer noticed a **hen** (母鸡) pecking at crumbs **sideways** (侧身地), its movements **intermittent** (断断续续的), as if mocking their futile debate.

**中文翻译**

法官宣称对该案拥有管辖权（jurisdiction），但被告律师轻蔑（scorn）地看着他，猛推（shove）开法庭门走了出去。外面，一辆有轨电车（tram）叮当驶过，车窗因冷空气中的氮（nitrogen）而蒙上雾气。律师上车后，想到这座城市中充满（abound）的不公。他裹紧披肩（cape），感觉自己像一颗注定被碾碎的樱桃（cherry）。他来到码头（dock），一位著名的（eminent）教授正在等候，此人以修辞（rhetoric）技巧闻名。“这个时代（epoch）的结局（ending）即将到来，”教授抚着下巴（jaw）说，“我们需要涤罪（purify）法律体系。”律师点头：“但我的双手被官僚主义的绳索（rope）捆住了。”教授大笑，从旁边摊位往汤里加了辣椒（pepper）。律师注意到一只母鸡（hen）正侧身（sideways）啄食面包屑，动作断断续续（intermittent），仿佛在嘲讽他们徒劳的争论。
