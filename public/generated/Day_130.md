## 二、连锁故事（英文）

The **sequence** (顺序) of events on that foggy morning seemed almost written by fate. Dr. Elena, a **systematic** (系统的) marine biologist, stood on the cliff, staring at the **horizon** (地平线), where the grey sky melted into the sea. It was **apparent** (明显的) that a storm was coming, but her **guidance** (指导) from the research institute was clear: collect data at all costs.

Beneath her, a **passive** (被动的) tide rolled gently, hiding a strange **creature** (生物) that had washed ashore. It was no ordinary fish—its eyes glowed with a **fierce** (凶猛的) light, and its scales seemed to **mix** (混合) the colors of fire and ice. Elena knelt down, unsure what would **trigger** (触发) a reaction. The creature’s tail twitched at the **cue** (提示) of her flashlight, but she knew that a sudden move could **disable** (失去能力) it permanently.

The local fishermen had warned her that the species was **hostile** (敌对的) to humans, yet Elena trusted the **integrity** (诚实正直) of her own observations. She used a **precise** (精确的) measuring device to scan its body, trying to **suppress** (压制) her own fear. A cold wind began to **blow** (吹), rattling the **bone** (骨) necklace she wore—a gift from an old native elder who had taught her to respect every living thing.

Suddenly, the creature opened its mouth, releasing a sound like a **gate** (门) creaking open. Elena realized that the ocean here was **abundant** (丰富的) with unknown life, and that this encounter was only the beginning of a much larger discovery.

## 三、中文翻译

那个雾蒙蒙的早晨，一连串事件的**顺序**（sequence）仿佛由命运写就。埃琳娜博士，一位**系统的**（systematic）海洋生物学家，站在悬崖上，凝视着**地平线**（horizon），灰蒙蒙的天空与大海融为一体。**明显的**（apparent）是，暴风雨即将来临，但研究所给她的**指导**（guidance）很明确：不惜一切代价收集数据。

在她脚下，**被动的**（passive）潮水轻轻翻滚，隐藏着一只被冲上岸的奇怪**生物**（creature）。它不是普通的鱼——它的眼睛闪烁着**凶猛的**（fierce）光芒，鳞片仿佛**混合**（mix）了火与冰的颜色。埃琳娜蹲下身子，不确定什么会**触发**（trigger）它的反应。在电筒的**提示**（cue）下，它的尾巴抽动了一下，但她知道，贸然行动可能会让它永久**失去能力**（disable）。

当地渔民曾警告她，这个物种对人类**敌对**（hostile），但埃琳娜相信自己观察的**诚实正直**（integrity）。她用一台**精确的**（precise）测量仪器扫描它的身体，努力**压制**（suppress）内心的恐惧。一阵冷风开始**吹**（blow）来，吹动了她脖子上的**骨**（bone）项链——那是当地一位老长者送给她的礼物，他教会她尊重每一个生命。

突然，那生物张开嘴，发出一种像**门**（gate）吱呀打开的声音。埃琳娜意识到，这片海洋里**丰富的**（abundant）未知生命，而这次相遇，仅仅是一场更大发现的开始。
