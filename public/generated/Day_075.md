In the ancient **kingdom** (君主统治的国家) of Montara, the old **king** (君主) carried a heavy **burden** (负担) on his shoulders: his people's **salary** (薪水) had been cut for three **annual** (每年的) cycles, and the **mountain** (山) passes were blocked. Every morning, he climbed the **garden** (花园) terrace, hoping the **earthquake** (地震) that had destroyed the eastern road would not **arise** (形成) again. His **religious** (虔诚的) advisors told him to pray, but he knew that prayer alone could not **ease** (减轻) the crisis.

One day, a young engineer arrived with a strange **telephone** (电话) device. "Your Majesty," he said, "I can **guarantee** (保障) a better future, but first I need your **request** (请求) for a new **foundation** (地基) for the bridge." The king laughed. "We have no time for **engine** (引擎) experiments! Our **lunch** (午饭) is dry bread." The engineer smiled. "The bridge's **existence** (存在) is **partly** (部分地) the reason your roads fail. Let me build a wind-powered **engine** (引擎) to pump water."

The king was skeptical, but he agreed. Within months, the **foundation** (地基) held firm, and the **engine** (引擎) worked. The **salary** (薪水) rose again, and the **mountain** (山) village became a **symbol** (象征) of hope. The **king** (君主) finally felt **comfort** (舒适) knowing his **burden** (负担) was lighter. "Your invention is a **symbol** (象征) of wisdom," he said. "And your **guarantee** (保障) was honest." The engineer bowed. "All I did was turn a **request** (请求) into a plan."

From that day, the **annual** (每年的) festival celebrated the **earthquake** (地震) that shook their fate. The people learned that **ease** (减轻) comes from action, not prayer. And the **king** (君主), now **comfortable** (舒适的) in his new role, often said: "A **burden** (负担) shared with a good **engine** (引擎) becomes a **foundation** (基础) for progress." The **telephone** (电话) rang daily with **requests** (请求) from other lands, and the **garden** (花园) bloomed again. **Partly** (部分地) because of the engineer, **partly** (部分地) because of the king, the **existence** (存在) of Montara was no longer a struggle—it was a story of **religious** (虔诚的) faith in human ingenuity.

在古老的蒙塔拉王国，年迈的君主肩上扛着沉重的负担：百姓的薪水已被削减了三个年度周期，而山路也完全被封锁。每天清晨，他爬上花园的平台，祈祷那场摧毁东边道路的地震不要再形成。虔诚的顾问们劝他祈祷，但他知道，仅靠祈祷无法减轻危机。

一天，一位年轻的工程师带着一个奇怪的电话装置到来。“陛下，”他说，“我能保障一个更好的未来，但我首先需要您对桥梁新地基的请求。”国王笑了。“我们可没时间做引擎实验！我们的午饭只有干面包。”工程师微笑着。“桥梁的存在，部分地就是您道路失败的原因。让我建一台风力引擎来抽水吧。”

国王半信半疑，但还是同意了。几个月内，地基稳固了，引擎运转了。薪水再次上涨，山村成了希望的象征。君主终于感到舒适，因为他的负担变轻了。“你的发明是智慧的象征，”他说，“你的保障也是诚实的。”工程师鞠躬。“我只是把一个请求变成了计划。”

从那天起，每年的节日都纪念那场撼动命运的地震。人们懂得了：减轻来自行动，而非祈祷。而国王，现在对自己的新角色感到舒适，常说：“一个负担若与好引擎分担，就会变成进步的基础。”电话每天响个不停，都是他国的请求，花园再次繁茂。部分地因为工程师，部分地因为国王，蒙塔拉的存在不再是一场挣扎——而是一个对人类才智怀有虔诚信念的故事。
