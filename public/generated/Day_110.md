**连锁故事**

In a **contemporary** (同时代的、现代的) city, a **grand** (宏伟的) stadium hosted a soccer (足球) final. The **prevailing** (流行的) mood was electric, but a **cautious** (谨慎的) coach warned his team: "Don't fall for their **trick** (诡计)!" A **curious** (好奇的) reporter watched from the **pool** (池塘) of light near the tunnel. She noticed a player **shed** (脱去) his jacket, revealing a strange **mechanism** (机制、方法) strapped to his arm. It was a **convert** (转变) device—it could **induce** (引起) a sudden **drought** (旱灾) on the field! The reporter felt **sympathy** (同情) for the fans. She tried to **assure** (使确信) the referee, but he was busy checking the **edition** (版本) of the rules. Meanwhile, a **classmate** (同学) of hers, now an engineer, identified the device's **portion** (部分) that stored energy. They **buried** (埋) the gadget in a sandpit, and the match continued. The team’s **pride** (自豪) was saved, and the **assured** victory felt earned. That night, the reporter wrote a story about **mechanical** (机械的) cheating, and her **editorial** (社论的) piece sparked a new rule. The **precaution** (预防措施) became mandatory. And the **grandeur** (壮丽) of fair play prevailed.
