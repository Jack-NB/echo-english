**英文连锁故事**

The **agency** (机构) was a small **staff** (员工) of ten, but its **capacity** (容量) for creative work was enormous. The **boss** (老板), a **western** (西方的) man named Mr. Cole, believed that every **possibility** (可能) deserved a chance. He told his team: “**Unless** (除非) we **adapt** (改编、适应) to the market, we will fail.” So they decided to **compete** (竞争) for a major **application** (应用、申请) contract from a tech giant.

The **participant** (参与者) from the client side was a strict woman who demanded a **union** (一致、联合) of ideas. She saw the **threat** (威胁) of losing her job if the project failed. Meanwhile, Mr. Cole felt a wave of **emotion** (情感) as he watched his **staff** (员工) work late nights. He **wished** (希望) he could **fix** (固定、修理) the broken printer himself, but he knew his role was to **educate** (教育) the team on new software.

The project’s **scale** (规模) was huge, and **unemployment** (失业) in the region made everyone desperate to succeed. They used a **marriage** (结婚) of traditional methods and digital tools. Finally, after weeks of stress, they delivered a perfect proposal. The client smiled, and the **agency** (机构) celebrated. Mr. Cole realized that **true** **possibility** (可能) only comes when a team works as one. *The end.*

**中文翻译**

这家机构（agency）只有十名员工（staff），但其创作容量（capacity）却大得惊人。老板（boss）是一位西方（western）人，名叫科尔先生，他相信每一种可能（possibility）都值得一试。他对团队说：“除非（unless）我们适应（adapt）市场，否则就会失败。”于是他们决定与一家科技巨头竞争（compete）一份重大应用（application）申请合同。

客户方的参与者（participant）是一位严厉的女士，她要求各种想法必须达成联合（union）。她感到如果项目失败，自己将面临失业的威胁（threat）。与此同时，科尔先生看着员工（staff）们熬夜工作，心中涌起一股情感（emotion）。他希望（wish）自己能亲手修理（fix）那台坏掉的打印机，但他知道自己的职责是教育（educate）团队使用新软件。

项目的规模（scale）巨大，而该地区的失业（unemployment）让每个人都拼命想成功。他们采用了传统方法与数字工具的结合（marriage）。最终，经过数周的压力，他们交付了一份完美的方案。客户笑了，机构（agency）欢呼庆祝。科尔先生意识到，真正的可能（possibility）只有在团队万众一心时才会出现。*完。*
