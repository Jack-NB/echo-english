**A Chain Story**

After a tense **interview** (采访、面试) at a tech firm, Maya tried to **stop** (阻止) her nervous habits. She knew her **financial** (财政的) future depended on this job. Her goal was to **reach** (达到) the senior level, but first she needed to appear truly **professional** (专业的). She noticed a small **sign** (标牌) on the desk reading “Be Yourself.” It felt like a tiny **earth** (地球) of encouragement in a vast office. The whole **project** (项目) she was applying for involved a new **generation** (一代人) of green energy devices. The company’s **standard** (标准) was extremely high, almost like a **war** (战争) for talent. Every candidate had a **story** (故事) to tell, and hers was **rich** (丰富的) with volunteer experience. **Later** (后来), she realized her first answer was wrong, but she tried **again** (再次) with more confidence. The **air** (空气) in the room felt heavy, yet she kept speaking. That **night** (夜晚), she received a call saying her application did **contain** (包含) all the required documents. The **main** (主要的) reason she got the job, they said, was her calm persistence. She would **soon** (很快地) start her new journey. Her **interviewer** later told her that her **storyline** was the most **enriching** one they had heard all week. The company would **standardize** its hiring process because of her feedback. She felt **unreachable** heights were now possible. Her **financial** worries began to **dissolve**, and she knew this was just the **beginning** of a **generative** career.
