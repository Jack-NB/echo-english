**The Chain Story**

The young **priest** (牧师) named Adam was known for his **thorough** (彻底的) devotion to his flock. One evening, a strange **creep** (爬、蔓延) of unease spread through the village as a **disastrous** (灾难性的) storm began to brew. The rain **contaminated** (污染) the river, and the **pigeon** (鸽子) that nested near the church fell sick with **diabetes** (糖尿病) from the spoiled grain. Adam, though **drunk** (醉酒) on grief for his people, refused to give up. He put on his worn **leather** (皮革) boots and helped the villagers **rotate** (轮流) the water barrels to collect clean rain. He even used his old **tire** (轮胎) as a float to cross the flooded road. The task was **dual** (双重的): save the livestock and the children. Watching him, the villagers felt deep **empathy** (共鸣) for his struggle. He worked **overtime** (超出的时间), until his hands were like **worm** (虫) tracks in mud. Suddenly, a **ski** (滑雪) instructor from the nearby hill arrived, offering dry clothes. Adam could barely **utter** (说) a thank-you, exhausted but relieved. The doctor warned that the infection could **multiply** (乘) if not treated, but Adam’s tireless effort inspired everyone. That night, as the storm passed, the village began to heal. Adam smiled, knowing that even small deeds could **swim** (游泳) against the tide of despair.

**中文翻译**

年轻牧师（priest）亚当以对教众彻底（thorough）的奉献而闻名。一天傍晚，一种不安的蔓延（creep）笼罩村庄，一场灾难性的（disastrous）暴风雨开始酝酿。雨水污染（contaminated）了河流，栖息在教堂附近的鸽子（pigeon）因吃了发霉的谷粒患上了糖尿病（diabetes）。亚当虽然因人民的苦难而悲痛得如醉酒（drunk），却拒绝放弃。他穿上破旧的皮革（leather）靴子，帮助村民轮流（rotate）搬运水桶收集干净的雨水。他甚至用旧轮胎（tire）作浮具，涉过洪水淹没的道路。任务是双重的（dual）：拯救牲畜和孩子。看着他，村民们对他的挣扎产生了深深的共鸣（empathy）。他超时（overtime）工作，双手像泥土里的虫（worm）痕一样脏。突然，附近山上的滑雪（ski）教练赶来，递上干衣服。亚当几乎说不出（utter）感谢的话，精疲力竭但如释重负。医生警告说，如果不治疗，感染会成倍（multiply）扩散，但亚当不知疲倦的努力鼓舞了所有人。那夜暴风雨过后，村庄开始康复。亚当笑了，深知即使是微小的善举，也能逆流游泳（swim）对抗绝望之潮。
