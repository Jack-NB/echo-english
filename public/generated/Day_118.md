**The Cloud, the Poet, and the Interference**

A single **cloud** (云) drifted over the plain (平的、朴素的) village, casting a soft shadow. Old Tom, a **poet** (诗人) with a **soul** (精神、灵魂) full of wonder, felt truly **alive** (活着的) beneath it. He tried to **exert** (用、运用) his imagination, but the noise from a nearby factory began to **interfere** (妨碍、介入) with his thoughts. This **interference** (干涉) annoyed him, yet he noticed something strange: the workers looked **alike** (与…相似) in their gray uniforms, marching like a line of **pioneer** (先驱) ants.

Tom remembered the town would **punish** (惩罚) anyone who dared to complain. But his **anger** (怒气、使发怒) grew like a **tiger** (老虎) in his chest. He glanced at the factory’s **menu** (菜单) of daily tasks, posted on the wall—each item more tedious than the last. A young **pilot** (飞行员) who had crash-landed nearby, now repairing his plane, shook his head at the **violent** (暴力的) work conditions. “This place would **bore** (使厌烦、钻孔) anyone to death,” he said.

Tom smiled, pulled out a notebook, and began to **update** (更新) his old poems. He added a line about the **cream** (奶油) of human kindness rising to the top, even under a **ton** (吨、大量) of hardship. He wrote: “The cloud is not the sky’s enemy; it is the sky’s **cloudy** (多云的) memory.” His **poetic** (诗意的) lines spread quickly. The workers, once **bored** (无聊的), now read them aloud. The factory owner, afraid of a **punitive** (惩罚性的) revolt, changed the rules. And Tom? He simply watched the cloud pass, knowing that even the **soulless** (没有灵魂的) machines could not stop a **living** (活的) word.

**中文翻译**

一朵云飘过平坦的村庄，投下柔和的影子。老诗人汤姆满怀惊奇的精神，觉得在云下真正地活着。他试图运用想象力，但附近工厂的噪音开始妨碍他的思绪。这种干涉让他恼火，但他注意到一件怪事：工人穿着灰色制服，看上去彼此相似，像一排先驱蚂蚁一样行进。

汤姆记得镇上会惩罚任何胆敢抱怨的人。但他的怒气像老虎一样在胸中膨胀。他瞥了一眼工厂墙上贴的“菜单”——每日任务清单，每项都比上一项更乏味。一位附近迫降、正在修飞机的年轻飞行员，对这暴力的工作条件摇了摇头。“这地方能把人无聊死，”他说。

汤姆笑了，掏出笔记本，开始更新他的旧诗。他加了一句：人性的奶油即使在一吨重压下也会浮到表面。他写道：“云不是天空的敌人；它是天空多云的记忆。”他充满诗意的句子迅速传开。工人们不再无聊，大声朗读。工厂主害怕惩罚性的反抗，改了规矩。而汤姆呢？他只是看着云飘过，知道即使是没灵魂的机器，也挡不住一个活着的词。
