**英文故事**

At **dawn** (黎明), a **sportsman** (运动员) named Leo slammed on the **brake** (刹车) of his old truck, skidding to a halt beside a **dairy** (乳制品) farm. He grabbed (抓取) a **certificate** (证书) from the glovebox—his **practitioner** (从业者) license—and checked his **calendar** (日历): today was the day he had to **verify** (核实) the farm’s safety standards. His **cousin** (堂兄弟姐妹) Mia, a veterinary student, joined him. They saw a pile of rotten hay beginning to **decay** (腐烂), which made the air **miserable** (悲惨的) to breathe. “This is a serious **proposition** (命题),” Leo said, “we must **qualify** (合格) this place before any animal gets hurt.” Mia wiped her face with a **tissue** (纸巾) and pointed at a **sole** (唯一的) rusty gate. “The **toll** (损失) of ignoring this could be huge,” she warned. Just then, a stray **spark** (火星) from the truck’s engine ignited the dry hay. In seconds, flames spread like a **compulsory** (强制的) force. Leo shouted, “Call the fire department!” But Mia had already dialed. As they ran, Leo noticed a **cyberspace** (网络空间) map on his phone showing the farm’s layout—the fire was near the fuel tank. They sprinted to the tank and **grabbed** (抓取) a fire extinguisher. After the chaos, the farm owner arrived, holding a **certificate** (证书) of insurance. Leo sighed, “At least we **verified** (核实) the danger.” The **dawn** (黎明) light now felt like a warning, not a promise.

**中文翻译**

黎明时分，运动员莱奥猛踩刹车（brake），卡车滑行着停在一家乳制品（dairy）农场旁。他从手套箱里抓出（grab）一张证书（certificate）——他的从业者（practitioner）执照——然后查看日历（calendar）：今天是他必须核实（verify）农场安全标准的日子。他的堂兄妹（cousin）米娅，一名兽医学生，和他一起。他们看到一堆开始腐烂（decay）的干草，空气闻起来悲惨（miserable）得令人窒息。“这是一个严肃的命题（proposition），”莱奥说，“我们必须让这个地方合格（qualify），不能让任何动物受伤。”米娅用纸巾（tissue）擦了擦脸，指着唯一（sole）一扇生锈的大门。“忽视这里的损失（toll）可能巨大，”她警告道。就在这时，卡车引擎迸出的一颗火星（spark）点燃了干草。几秒钟内，火焰像一种强制（compulsory）的力量蔓延开来。莱奥大喊：“叫消防队！”但米娅已经拨通了电话。他们奔跑时，莱奥注意到手机上的网络空间（cyberspace）地图显示了农场的布局——火势正逼近油罐。他们冲向油罐，抓取（grab）灭火器。混乱过后，农场主赶来，手里拿着一张保险证书（certificate）。莱奥叹了口气：“至少我们核实（verify）了危险。”此时的黎明（dawn）光更像一种警告，而非承诺。
