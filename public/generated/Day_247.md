## 二、连锁故事（英文）

**The Violet Wagon**

In a small town, a painter named Leo was mixing a **violet** (紫色) pigment when his old **wagon** (货车) rumbled past, making him **yawn** (呵欠) with boredom. Suddenly, black **fumes** (烟) poured from the truck’s engine. Leo grabbed a **parachute** (降落伞) from his studio, thinking it was a **superfluous** (多余的) prop — but now he needed it to escape the smoke. He jumped onto the wagon’s roof, where a small **turbine** (涡轮) fan spun wildly. He used its **aerial** (天线) to signal for help, wiping sweat from his **brow** (眉毛). The mechanic said he had to **defer** (推迟) the repair, which made Leo’s temper **flare** (爆发、闪光). He felt **humiliated** (使丢脸) by the delay, and grew **jealous** (嫉妒的) of the birds flying free above. Just then, a flock of crows began a **massacre** (大屠杀) of the local cornfield — a **monotonous** (单调的) sight he’d seen every autumn. But today, he decided to **rejoice** (高兴) instead of complain. With a **sly** (狡猾的) grin, he used his **speciality** (专长) — painting — to create a giant **strawberry** (草莓) mural on the wagon, hoping to attract tourists. The result was a perfect **synthesis** (合成) of art and survival.

## 三、中文翻译

**紫色货车**

在一个小镇上，一位名叫利奥的画家正在调制**紫色**（violet）颜料，这时他那辆破旧的**货车**（wagon）隆隆驶过，让他无聊地打了个**呵欠**（yawn）。突然，黑色的**烟**（fume）从卡车引擎里喷涌而出。利奥从画室里抓起一顶**降落伞**（parachute），原本以为这只是个**多余的**（superfluous）道具——但现在他需要靠它逃离浓烟。他跳上货车顶，那里有一个小**涡轮**（turbine）风扇疯狂旋转。他借用它的**天线**（aerial）发出求救信号，一边擦去**眉毛**（brow）上的汗水。修车师傅说必须**推迟**（defer）修理，这让利奥的脾气瞬间**爆发**（flare）。他觉得这次延误让他**丢脸**（humiliate），甚至开始**嫉妒**（jealous）天上自由飞翔的鸟儿。就在这时，一群乌鸦开始对当地玉米地展开一场**大屠杀**（massacre）——这是每年秋天他都看腻了的**单调**（monotonous）景象。但今天，他决定**高兴**（rejoice）起来，而不是抱怨。他露出一个**狡猾的**（sly）笑容，用自己的**专长**（speciality）——绘画——在货车上画了一幅巨大的**草莓**（strawberry）壁画，想吸引游客。结果，艺术与求生完美地**合成**（synthesis）在了一起。
