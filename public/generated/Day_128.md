## 二、连锁故事（英文）

**The Golden Test**

In a small town, there lived a man whose **fame** (名声) spread far beyond its borders. He was a **logical** (合理的) thinker, always seeking to **fulfil** (履行) his promises to **mankind** (人类). His **monetary** (货币的) success, however, never went to his **nose** (鼻子) — he remained humble and kind.

One day, he was forced into a **combat** (战斗) of wits against a rival. The rival's **motive** (动机) was pure jealousy, a **variation** (变化) of the **harsh** (严厉的) reality of competition. To win, our hero needed more than just intelligence; he needed the **provision** (提供) of resources and a calm **temper** (脾气). He refused to **exhaust** (耗尽) his energy on anger.

As the **holder** (持有者) of a rare **linguistic** (语言的) gift, he could speak seven languages. Even his **mine** (矿) of knowledge seemed endless. He treated his **servant** (仆人) with respect, and in **reflection** (反射), his kindness mirrored back to him. He shared his **bread** (面包) with the poor, and soon his reputation turned **golden** (金色的).

In the end, his **fulfilment** (完成) came not from victory, but from the **motivated** (激励的) people he left behind. His story became a **provisional** (临时的) legend, a **reflective** (反思的) tale for all who sought to **undermine** (暗中破坏) others for their own gain.

## 三、中文翻译

**金色的考验**

在一个小镇上，住着一位名声（fame）远扬的人。他是一位思考讲求逻辑（logical）、始终履行（fulfil）对全人类（mankind）承诺的人。他在货币（monetary）上的成功从未让他趾高气扬（鼻子 nose 上扬），他依然谦逊和善。

一天，他被迫与一位对手进行一场智力的较量（combat）。对手的动机（motive）纯粹是嫉妒，这是激烈竞争中残酷（harsh）现实的一种变体（variation）。要想获胜，我们的主人公不仅需要智慧，还需要资源的供给（provision）和平和的脾气（temper）。他拒绝在愤怒中耗尽（exhaust）自己的精力。

作为一项罕见语言（linguistic）天赋的持有者（holder），他能说七种语言。他知识的矿藏（mine）似乎取之不尽。他尊重自己的仆人（servant），而这种尊重也在反射（reflection）中回到了他身上。他与穷人分享面包（bread），很快他的声誉变得如金色（golden）般闪耀。

最终，他的满足感并非来自胜利，而是来自他所激励的人们。他的故事成了一个临时的传奇，一段引人反思的寓言，警示那些试图通过暗中破坏（undermine）他人来谋取私利的人。
