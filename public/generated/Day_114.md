**Chain Story**

The traveler took a **straight** (直接的) path through the city, but soon realized he had to **exceed** (超过、越过) his usual speed to catch the last train. At a **glance** (一瞥) at his watch, he saw time was running out. He decided to **implement** (实施、工具) a new plan: he would cut through the old market, a shortcut he had never tried. Unfortunately, years of **chronic** (慢性的、长期的) neglect had left the streets full of rubble, and he tripped, spilling coffee from his **cup** (杯子). A kind old man nearby, who lived on a small **pension** (养老金), helped him up just as a sudden **storm** (风暴) began to break. The rain made the ground slippery, and the traveler felt his spirits **sink** (下沉). Yet the old man pointed out that the rain was actually **beneficial** (有益的) for the dusty streets, washing away the dirt. They hurried to a shelter, carrying a heavy **load** (装货、装载) of groceries the old man had bought. Inside, the traveler noticed a **romantic** (浪漫的、不切实际的) painting on the **shelf** (架子) of a small café, depicting a distant **destination** (目的地) he had once dreamed of. The old man smiled and offered to **accompany** (陪伴、为……伴奏) him to the station, explaining that he used to work there as a guard, holding a giant **lock** (锁) for the night gates. As they walked, the old man told stories of his time serving on a **jury** (陪审团), and his dry **humor** (幽默、诙谐) made the journey feel shorter. The traveler realized that this unplanned encounter was truly **desirable** (称心的、可取的), and with a quick **motion** (动、提议) of gratitude, he shook the old man’s hand and boarded his train, forever grateful for that rainy detour.

**中文翻译**

旅行者沿着一条**直接**（straight）的道路穿过城市，但很快意识到他必须**超过**（exceed）平时的速度才能赶上最后一班火车。他**一瞥**（glance）手表，发现时间不多了。他决定**实施**（implement）一个新计划：穿过旧市场，一条从未走过的小路。不幸的是，多年**慢性的**（chronic）疏于维护让街道布满碎石，他绊倒了，手里的**杯子**（cup）咖啡洒了出来。旁边一位靠微薄**养老金**（pension）生活的善良老人扶起了他，就在这时一场突如其来的**风暴**（storm）开始袭来。雨水让地面变得湿滑，旅行者感到心情**下沉**（sink）。但老人指出，这场雨对尘土飞扬的街道其实是**有益的**（beneficial），冲走了污垢。他们匆忙躲进一处避雨棚，手里还提着老人刚买的一**大包**（load）杂货。棚内，旅行者注意到小咖啡馆**架子**（shelf）上挂着一幅**浪漫的**（romantic）画，描绘着一个他曾梦想过的遥远**目的地**（destination）。老人微笑着提出**陪伴**（accompany）他去车站，说他以前在那里当门卫，晚上要挂一把巨大的**锁**（lock）。一路上，老人讲起自己当年在**陪审团**（jury）任职的故事，他那种冷面**幽默**（humor）让旅程变得不再漫长。旅行者意识到，这次意外的相遇真是**称心的**（desirable），他怀着感激的**动作**（motion）握了握老人的手，登上了火车，永远感激那次雨天里的绕道。
