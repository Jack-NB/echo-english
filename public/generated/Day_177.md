In the dimly lit **chamber** (室), a lonely **violin** (小提琴) rested on a velvet cushion. Beside it, an old man began to **weep** (哭泣), remembering his lost love. He had shown her every **courtesy** (礼貌) in his youth, but fate had decreed their **doom** (厄运、注定). Now, only ghosts **inhabit** (栖息) this mansion.

He knew that solitude was hardly **preferable** (更好的) to companionship, yet he had grown accustomed to it. On the table lay a **sketch** (素描、草图) of her face, drawn with trembling hands. Suddenly, a loud **bang** (巨响、刘海) from the storm outside shook the windows, and the **enlighten** (启发) moment struck him — he must leave this place.

He packed a **mixture** (混合) of letters and photographs into a rusty **pan** (平底锅), as if it were a treasure chest. A single **pearl** (珍珠) from her necklace slipped into his pocket. He owned only a small **acre** (英亩) of land, but his guilt was **conspicuous** (显著的、显眼的) to every villager. His heart felt **fragile** (脆弱的), yet his resolve was **gigantic** (巨大的). He decided to **overturn** (掀翻、推翻) the old narrative of his life.

He would **propel** (推进) himself forward, leaving behind the sorrows he had once **sown** (播种、母猪) in silence. The wind howled, but he stepped out into the rain, carrying the violin and the pearl — ready to live again.

**中文翻译**

在昏暗的**室**（chamber）内，一把孤独的**小提琴**（violin）静卧在天鹅绒垫上。旁边，一位老人开始**哭泣**（weep），想起了他失去的爱人。年轻时他对她百般**礼貌**（courtesy），但命运早已注定了他们的**厄运**（doom）。如今，只有幽灵**栖息**（inhabit）在这座宅邸中。

他知道孤独并不比陪伴**更好**（preferable），但他已习惯了。桌上放着一幅她的**素描**（sketch），是颤抖着手画下的。突然，窗外风暴传来一声**巨响**（bang），而这一瞬的**启发**（enlighten）击中了他——他必须离开这里。

他把信件和照片的**混合**物（mixture）装进一只生锈的**平底锅**（pan），仿佛那是宝箱。一颗她项链上的**珍珠**（pearl）滑入他的口袋。他只拥有一小片**英亩**（acre）土地，但他的愧疚在村民眼中无比**显眼**（conspicuous）。他的心变得**脆弱**（fragile），但他的决心却**巨大**（gigantic）。他决定**推翻**（overturn）自己旧日的人生叙事。

他要**推进**（propel）自己向前，抛开那些他曾默默**播种**（sow）的悲伤。风在呼啸，但他踏入雨中，带着小提琴和珍珠——准备好重新生活。
