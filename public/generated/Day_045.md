**The Chain Story**

On a freezing **cold** (冷的) winter morning (早晨), I received an unexpected **contact** (联系) from an old friend. She had worked in the **department** (部门) of a tech company, but was now facing a harsh **reality** (现实、真实的事物) — her project was about to collapse. She asked for my **advice** (建议), so I **commented** (评论) that she should first check the **board** (板) of directors’ latest decision. The **consequence** (后果、结果) of ignoring it would be terrible. Her **style** (风格) had always been to **fight** (打斗) for every detail, but that often led to **waste** (浪费) of time. I reminded her that every **stage** (阶段) of an **operation** (运营、操作) needs a proper **tool** (工具) — and sometimes the best tool is a clear plan. **Despite** (尽管) her **worry** (担心), she decided to **predict** (预测) the market trend using data. We shared a quick **meal** (饭) at her office, and she laughed, saying she felt like a ****worried** (焦虑的) child. I smiled and said, “Don’t be so ****wasteful** (浪费的) with your energy. Your ****prediction** (预测名词) skills are ****predictable** (可预见的) good!” She finally realized that her ****unpredictable** (不可预测的) fears were the real enemy. By evening, she had drafted a new proposal, and I could see her ****realistic** (现实的) smile returning. The ****industrial** (工业的) world is harsh, but with the right ****cooperation** (合作), even a ****frigid** (严寒的) morning can turn into a ****stylish** (时髦的) success.
