**A Wooden Medal and a Prone Predator: A Story of Comparison and Repair**

It was **probable** (很可能的) that the old wooden (木制的) ship would not survive another storm. Captain Gray, a man known for his **comparative** (比较的) calm in crisis, stared at a faded cartoon (卡通) of a brave sailor pinned to the cabin wall—a strange source of strength. In his pocket lay a bronze medal (奖章) from his navy days, a symbol of honor he refused to detach (分离) from his jacket.

The ship’s hull was not **durable** (耐用持久的); years of saltwater had made it **partial** (部分的) to rot. And beyond the waves, a **predator** (食肉动物) —a great shark—circled with cruel (残忍的) patience. Gray knew he had to **mend** (修补) the leak fast, using a **progressive** (渐进的) repair method: layer by layer, plank by plank. The **ratio** (比率) of water rushing in to wood he could patch was alarming.

He remembered an old **tactic** (策略) from his training: use wet cloth to **constrain** (限制) the flow. His **dentist** (牙科医生) back home had once joked that Gray’s teeth were as strong as his stubbornness—now that stubbornness kept him **prone** (俯卧的) on the deck, working. A sip of cold tea helped **refresh** (提神) his mind. He called to his crew to be **cooperative** (合作的); together they sealed the breach. The shark, losing interest, swam away. Gray laughed, thinking how the sea always tested a man’s **probable** limits—and how a simple wooden patch could beat a metal monster.

**中文翻译**

那艘老旧的木制（wooden）船很可能（probable）无法再撑过一场风暴。格雷船长以比较（comparative）冷静著称，他盯着墙上贴着的一幅褪色的卡通（cartoon）画——画中是一位勇敢的水手——这竟成了他奇怪的力量来源。他口袋里装着一枚海军时代的青铜奖章（medal），这是他拒绝从夹克上分离（detach）的荣誉象征。

船体并不耐用（durable）；多年的海水侵蚀使它部分（partial）腐烂。而在海浪之外，一个食肉动物（predator）——一条大鲨鱼——带着残忍（cruel）的耐心盘旋着。格雷知道必须赶紧修补（mend）漏洞，采用一种渐进（progressive）的修复法：一层一层，一块木板接一块木板。水涌入的速度与他能补上的木板的比率（ratio）令人心惊。

他想起训练时的一个旧策略（tactic）：用湿布来限制（constrain）水流。他家乡的牙科医生（dentist）曾开玩笑说，格雷的牙齿和他一样倔强——如今正是这股倔强让他俯卧（prone）在甲板上干活。一口凉茶让他提神（refresh）。他呼喊船员们要合作（cooperative）；众人合力封住了缺口。鲨鱼失去兴趣，游走了。格雷笑了，心想大海总是在考验一个人可能（probable）的极限——而一块简单的木片竟能打败一个金属怪物。
