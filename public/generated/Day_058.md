In a small coastal town, there lived a **sort** (种类) of restless inventor named Leo. He wanted to **enhance** (提高) his old boat's speed, so he drew a **round** (圆的) propeller design on paper. He tried to **connect** (连接) the engine to a new gearbox, but the bolts wouldn't fit. Determined to move **forward** (前进的), he decided to **handle** (处理) the problem himself. His only **debt** (债务) was to the local hardware store, and he couldn't afford new parts — his **diet** (饮食) had become instant noodles for weeks. The lack of **connection** (联系) between the gears frustrated him. He painted the hull a bright **color** (颜色) to cheer himself up, then kicked the **door** (门) of his workshop in anger. The metal was **hot** (热的) from welding, and he burned his hand. His **personality** (人格) was stubborn, so he refused to give up. **Eventually** (最终), he decided to **manufacture** (制造、捏造) a custom gear from scrap metal. He stared at the **ocean** (海洋) outside, thinking of the **north** (北、北方的) wind that would push his sails. He added a **chemical** (化学的) coating to prevent rust, which he could now **afford** (承担得起) after selling his old radio. He sprinkled a little **sugar** (糖) into his coffee, smiled, and knew the boat would sail by dawn.

在一座沿海小镇上，住着一位爱折腾的发明家利奥。他想**提高**（enhance）旧船的速度，于是在纸上画了一个**圆的**（round）螺旋桨设计。他试图**连接**（connect）发动机和新变速箱，但螺栓装不上。他决心**前进的**（forward）方向不变，决定自己**处理**（handle）这个问题。他唯一的**债务**（debt）是欠五金店的，他已经几周买不起新零件，**饮食**（diet）全靠方便面。齿轮之间缺乏**联系**（connection）让他十分沮丧。他把船身漆成亮眼的**颜色**（color）来振奋自己，然后气得踢了一脚车间的**门**（door）。焊接过的金属**热的**（hot）发烫，他烧伤了手。他的**人格**（personality）很固执，拒绝放弃。**最终**（eventually），他决定用废金属**制造**（manufacture）一个定制齿轮。他望着外面的**海洋**（ocean），想着**北方的**（north）风会推动船帆。他加了一层**化学的**（chemical）防锈涂层——卖掉旧收音机后，他现在**承担得起**（afford）这笔开销了。他在咖啡里撒了一点**糖**（sugar），微笑着知道，黎明前船一定能下水。
