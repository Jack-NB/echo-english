Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a **conscientious** (认真的) young engineer named Leo. He was known for his **conscience** (良心) and always checked every detail twice. One day, a **coronavirus** (冠状病毒) outbreak forced the town to **evacuate** (疏散) the entire valley. Leo, however, stayed behind to operate a giant **crane** (起重机) that was needed to move a **crystal** (水晶) sculpture — the town’s symbol — to a safe place.

As the crisis **culminated** (达到高潮) in a fierce storm, Leo found shelter in a dusty **cupboard** (碗橱) inside an old factory. His heart filled with **dismay** (沮丧) when he saw the storm had broken the factory’s roof. But he noticed a **feather** (羽毛) floating down from a broken bird’s nest — a sign of hope. He grabbed a bag of **fertiliser** (化肥) to block the leaking water, remembering the **feudal** (封建的) era when villagers used such bags to build barriers.

That night, he lit a small **fireplace** (壁炉) to warm himself, and watched water trickle from a broken **fountain** (喷泉) outside. A **frog** (蛙) jumped onto his boot, and Leo smiled. He began to think about how even a tiny **germ** (细菌) could cause such chaos, and how people must **glide** (滑翔) through crises with calmness. As the storm passed, he realised he was the **heir** (继承人) to the factory owner’s legacy — the old man had no children. The danger was no longer **imminent** (逼近的). Yet Leo felt a strange force **inhibiting** (抑制) his excitement — he had to stay **inward** (内向的) and humble.

The next morning, he walked out, feeling **inwardly** (内心里) renewed. The town rebuilt, and Leo became a legend — not for the **crystal** he saved, but for his **conscientiousness** (认真负责) in the darkest hour.

**中文翻译：**

从前，在一个小村庄里，住着一位认真的年轻工程师利奥。他以良心著称，凡事都要检查两遍。有一天，一场冠状病毒疫情迫使全镇疏散整个山谷。然而利奥留下来操作一台巨大的起重机，因为需要用它把一座水晶雕塑——小镇的象征——搬到安全的地方。

当危机在暴风雨中达到高潮时，利奥在一家旧工厂落满灰尘的碗橱里找到了避难所。看到暴风雨吹破了工厂的屋顶，他心中充满沮丧。但他注意到一根羽毛从破碎的鸟巢中飘落——那是希望的象征。他抓起一袋化肥来堵住漏水，想起封建时代村民们常用这种袋子筑堤。

那一夜，他点燃一个小壁炉取暖，看着外面破喷泉中滴落的水。一只青蛙跳上他的靴子，利奥笑了。他开始思考，哪怕一个微小的细菌也能造成如此混乱，而人们必须像滑翔一样平静地穿越危机。暴风雨过后，他意识到自己是工厂主遗产的继承人——老人没有孩子。危险已不再逼近。然而，他感到一种奇怪的力量抑制着他的兴奋——他必须保持内向、谦逊。

第二天早晨，他走出去，内心感到焕然一新。小镇重建了，利奥成了传奇人物——不是因为他救下的水晶，而是因为他在最黑暗时刻的认真负责。
