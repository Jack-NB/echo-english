**连锁故事 (英文)**

As the **forthcoming** (即将到来的) conference on urban logistics approached, Jack felt a **handy** (方便的) tool was needed to manage the vast **inventory** (详细目录、清点) of supplies. He checked into a cheap **motel** (汽车旅馆) near the port, where a retired **naval** (海军的) officer named Pete happened to be the night manager. Jack pulled out his **notebook** (笔记本) and sketched a plan under a **palm** (棕榈) tree in the courtyard. Pete suggested using a **relay** (继电器、接力) system to coordinate delivery trucks, and they shared a quick **sandwich** (三明治) while discussing it. The salty **scent** (气味) of the sea filled the air, reminding Jack of his **shrewd** (精明的) old mentor, who had taught him to **specialise** (专门研究、特定范围) in one key area rather than spread thin. Jack’s **stance** (立场) was firm: he would **veto** (否决) any plan that risked delays. He tightened his **waist** (腰) belt, feeling like a **widow** (寡妇) whose only comfort was work. Just then, a call came from the **ambassador** (大使) of a trade delegation, praising Jack’s ability to **avert** (避免) crisis. Jack decided to **bathe** (沐浴) in the motel’s small pool, then wrote a final note to his **beloved** (心爱的) daughter, promising to return before the weekend.

**中文翻译**

随着即将到来的（forthcoming）城市物流会议临近，杰克觉得需要一个方便的（handy）工具来管理庞大的详细目录（inventory）物资。他在港口附近住进了一家便宜的汽车旅馆（motel），那里的夜班经理恰好是一位退役的海军（naval）军官皮特。杰克掏出笔记本（notebook），在庭院里的棕榈（palm）树下画了一张草图。皮特建议用继电器（relay）系统来协调送货卡车，两人边吃三明治（sandwich）边讨论方案。海风带着咸咸的气味（scent）扑面而来，让杰克想起他精明（shrewd）的老导师——那位导师曾教导他，要专门研究（specialise）一个关键领域，而不是四面出击。杰克的立场（stance）很坚定：任何有延误风险的计划，他都会否决（veto）。他收紧腰（waist）带，感觉自己像个寡妇（widow），唯一的安慰就是工作。就在这时，一位贸易代表团的大使（ambassador）打来电话，称赞杰克有能力避免（avert）危机。杰克决定在汽车旅馆的小泳池里沐浴（bathe）一下，然后给心爱的（beloved）女儿写了一张便条，承诺周末前一定回家。
