**连锁故事**

Tom felt **lonely** (孤独地) as he sat in his small dorm room, staring at a **gallon** (加仑) of cheap coffee. His **tutor** (导师) had advised him to take the **railway** (铁路) home for the holiday, but he wanted to **integrate** (整合) his notes first. He decided to **exclude** (不包括) all the messy sketches from his final draft, keeping only the core ideas. With a **casual** (随意的) sigh, he checked his phone for directions, trying to **orient** (定位) himself in the chaos of exam season. Every **component** (组成部分) of his study plan seemed to fall apart — he had a **dozen** (一打) unfinished essays. Yet he dreamed of future **prosperity** (繁荣), knowing that studying **badly** (糟糕地) now would **diminish** (削弱) his chances. He tried to **imitate** (模仿) his roommate's focused routine, feeling an **intense** (强烈的) urge to succeed. But his phone and snacks would **occupy** (占据) his desk and mind. He decided to **distribute** (分发) his tasks across each **phase** (阶段) of the day, planning an **intensive** (密集的) study session. Finally, he sat down to **revise** (修改) his schedule, and for the first time, felt a spark of hope.
