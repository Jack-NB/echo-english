**连锁故事 (Chain Story)**

In a small coastal town, a chef named Leo was known for his unique **flavor** (风味) combinations. One morning, he received a **kilogram** (公斤) of fresh shark meat, which inspired him to **preach** (说教、讲道) to his young apprentice about the art of cooking. **Presently** (不久), the apprentice wrote a **prose** (散文) praising Leo’s skill, comparing him to a **shark** (鲨鱼) of the kitchen. Leo laughed and said, “I’m not **thirteen** (十三) anymore, but I still dream **vertical** (垂直的) goals.”

To **administer** (管理) his restaurant better, Leo decided to **aspire** (立志) to become a certified **attorney** (律师) in food law. His **classification** (分类) of recipes was meticulous, and he made a bold **declaration** (宣告、声明) that every dish must have an **editorial** (社论) in the local paper. His rules were **explicit** (明确的): no shortcuts, only **fruitful** (富有成效的) methods. **Meantime** (同时), his apprentice had a **nightmare** (恶梦) about spoiled sauce, which became a warning **ornament** (装饰) hanging in the kitchen — a reminder that quality is a **perpetual** (永久的) pursuit.

**中文翻译**

在一个沿海小镇上，一位名叫利奥的厨师以独特的**风味**组合而闻名。一天早上，他收到一**公斤**新鲜的鲨鱼肉，这激发他向年轻的学徒**说教**烹饪的艺术。**不久**，学徒写了一篇**散文**赞美利奥的技艺，把他比作厨房里的**鲨鱼**。利奥笑着说：“我早不是**十三**岁了，但我依然追求**垂直**的目标。”

为了更好地**管理**餐厅，利奥决定**立志**成为一名食品法领域的合格**律师**。他的食谱**分类**一丝不苟，他还发表了一项大胆的**宣告**：每道菜都必须在当地报纸上有一篇**社论**。他的规则**明确**：没有捷径，只有**富有成效**的方法。**同时**，他的学徒做了一场关于酱汁变质的**恶梦**，这个梦成了挂在厨房里的一个警示**装饰**——提醒人们，品质是**永久**的追求。
