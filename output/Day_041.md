**The Chain Story**

On a chilly morning, a single **droplet** (小水滴) began to **drop** (掉下) from a broken gutter. It landed on a small **device** (装置、方法) that looked like a sensor. The sensor was part of a **courtyard** (庭院) security system near the local **court** (法庭、球场). A **druggist** (药剂师) from the nearby **drugstore** (药店) noticed it and felt a **warmth** (温暖) of curiosity — the **warm** (温暖) light of dawn made the metal gleam.

He called a **ranger** (护林员) who knew how to **arrange** (安排) the **range** (范围) of the sensor’s detection field. But the problem required a **solution** (解决方案). It was **essential** (重要的) to **solve** (解决) it before the **essence** (本质) of the malfunction caused a false alarm. The ranger **particularly** (尤其) disliked false alarms, as they were a **particular** (特定的) nuisance.

He decided to use an **old-fashioned** (老式的) but **fashionable** (时髦的) trick: he attached a small **indicator** (指示器) to the sensor. The **indication** (迹象) showed that the wiring had an **erratic** (不稳定的) connection. This was an **accident** (事故) waiting to happen — the **incidence** (发生率) of such failures was **slowly** (慢慢地) increasing. The ranger worked **slow** (慢的) and carefully, counting the **six** (六) screws one by one. He used a **tack** (大头钉) to **attach** (附上、附加) a new wire, gaining **respect** (尊重) from the druggist for his **respectful** (恭敬的) attitude.

However, he made an **erroneous** (错误的) assumption about the power source. The **error** (错误) caused a small spark. Fortunately, no one was hurt, and the druggist felt **happy** (快乐的) that the **happiness** (幸福) of a safe morning was restored. The sensor’s **credible** (可信的) performance earned **credit** (信用) for the team, though the repair was **expensive** (贵的) in terms of time. The **expenditure** (支出) was worth it, as the system was now **incredible** (难以置信的)ly reliable.

**中文翻译**

在一个寒冷的早晨，一滴小水滴从破损的排水沟上**掉下**（drop）。它落在一个小**装置**（device）上，看起来像传感器。这个传感器是庭院安保系统的一部分，紧邻当地的**法庭**（court）。附近药店的药剂师注意到了这个情况，感到一丝好奇的**温暖**（warm）——黎明的暖光照得金属闪闪发光。

他叫来一位护林员，他知道如何**安排**（arrange）传感器探测场的**范围**（range）。但问题需要一个**解决方案**（solution）。在故障的**本质**（essence）引发误报之前，**解决**（solve）它至关**重要**（essential）。护林员**尤其**（particularly）讨厌误报，因为那是**特定**（particular）的麻烦。

他决定用一个**老式**（old-fashioned）但**时髦**（fashionable）的技巧：他在传感器上附加了一个小**指示器**（indicator）。**迹象**（indication）显示线路连接**不稳定**（erratic）。这是一场等待发生的**事故**（accident）——此类故障的**发生率**（incidence）正在**慢慢地**（slowly）上升。护林员**慢**（slow）而仔细地工作，一个一个地数着那**六**（six）颗螺丝。他用一根大头钉**附上**（attach）了新电线，赢得了药剂师的**尊重**（respect），因为他态度**恭敬**（respectful）。

然而，他对电源做了一个**错误的**（erroneous）假设。这个**错误**（error）引起了一点火花。幸运的是，没有人受伤，药剂师感到很**快乐**（happy），因为安全的早晨所带来的**幸福**（happiness）得以恢复。传感器**可信的**（credible）表现给团队赢得了**信用**（credit），尽管这次维修在时间上是**贵的**（expensive）。这笔**支出**（expenditure）是值得的，因为系统现在**难以置信地**（incredible）可靠。
