**连锁故事**

In the age of the **dinosaur** (恐龙), a **dread** (恐惧) of extinction loomed over every creature. A young **drum** (鼓) player in the village, who was also a servant to the local **emperor** (皇帝), received orders to **execute** (执行) a secret plan. A terrible **famine** (饥荒) had forced the emperor to build a giant **fence** (栅栏) around the last fertile land. The village **flame** (火焰) keeper stood at the **fore** (船头) of a wooden raft, holding a **hammer** (锤) to repair the hull.

The emperor’s plan was **homogeneous** (同类的) — it treated all villages the same, inviting **intrigue** (阴谋) among rivals. The raft’s planks began to **lap** (重叠部分) awkwardly, and the captain measured water in **litre** (公升) units, but the vessel was too **lofty** (高的) in design. A **magnet** (磁铁) compass failed, so the captain wrote a **memorandum** (备忘录) to the crew about navigation. The sailors began to **mingle** (交往) with the villagers, sharing food in **miniature** (小型的) portions. The emperor’s **niece** (侄女) secretly helped them, smuggling supplies past the fence.

As the raft sailed, the **drummer** (鼓手) beat a rhythm. The **imperial** (帝国的) guards pursued, but the **flaming** (燃烧的) torches on the shore guided the escape. The **foremost** (最重要的) goal was survival. They **overlapped** (重叠) the escape route with a decoy path, and the **magnetic** (有磁性的) rocks near the coast confused the enemy ships. The **memorable** (难忘的) night ended with the crew reaching a hidden island — a place where the **homogenized** (使均匀的) culture of the empire could not reach. The **minimal** (最小的) supplies were enough, and the **nephew** (侄子) of the niece rowed the last mile. The **dreadful** (可怕的) famine was behind them, and the **lofty** (崇高的) dream of freedom was finally real.

**中文翻译**

在恐龙时代，一种对灭绝的恐惧笼罩着每一种生物。村里一位年轻的鼓手，同时也是当地皇帝的仆人，接到命令去执行一项秘密计划。一场可怕的饥荒迫使皇帝在最后一片肥沃土地周围建起了巨大的栅栏。村里的火焰守护者站在木筏的船头，握着一把锤子修理船体。

皇帝的计划是千篇一律的——它把所有村庄一视同仁，这引起了对手之间的阴谋。木筏的木板开始尴尬地重叠，船长以公升为单位测量水量，但船只的设计太过高耸。一块磁铁指南针失灵了，于是船长给船员写了一份关于航行的备忘录。水手们开始与村民交往，分享小份的食物。皇帝的侄女偷偷帮助他们，穿过栅栏走私物资。

当木筏航行时，鼓手敲打着节奏。帝国的卫兵追了上来，但岸上燃烧的火炬指引了逃生的方向。最重要的目标是生存。他们把逃生路线与一条诱饵路线重叠，海岸附近有磁性的岩石使敌舰迷失方向。这个难忘的夜晚以船员抵达一座隐秘岛屿而告终——一个帝国那被均质化的文化无法触及的地方。最少的补给已经足够，侄女的侄子划完了最后一英里。可怕的饥荒被抛在身后，而那崇高的自由梦想终于成真了。
