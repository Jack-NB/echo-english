**The Tower of the Outstanding Biographer**

In a small coastal town, an **outstanding** (杰出的) architect named Leo was asked to design a new **tower** (塔) for the old harbor. He felt a deep sense of **worthy** (有价值的) purpose, as the project would **upgrade** (升级) the entire area. But his work was constantly **disturbed** (打扰) by the noise of construction, and he felt **painful** (痛苦的) frustration.

One evening, while reading the **biography** (传记) of a famous engineer, Leo found a strange note: “To **assemble** (装配、召集) the perfect structure, you must first understand the **compound** (化合物) of its materials.” Inspired, he decided to **supplement** (补充) his design with ancient techniques. He studied the **colony** (殖民地) of seabirds that nested on the cliff, noticing how their nests **confine** (限制) heat while remaining **rough** (粗糙的) yet strong.

Then came the day of his daughter's **wedding** (婚礼). In the middle of the ceremony, a sudden **panic** (惊慌的) swept the crowd as a **crack** (裂缝) appeared in the old tower's base. Leo rushed over, **literally** (逐字地、简直) running on instinct. He ordered workers to bring **wheels** (轮子) and pulleys, and with quick teamwork, they stabilized the structure **till** (直到……为止) nightfall.

That night, Leo **decorated** (装饰) the tower with lanterns, and the town cheered. His design was not just functional—it was a masterpiece. The tower stood as a symbol of resilience, and Leo's name became one worth remembering.

**中文翻译**

在沿海的一个小镇上，一位杰出的建筑师名叫利奥，受托为老港口设计一座新塔。他深感这项工程意义重大，因为这将升级整个地区的面貌。然而，施工噪音不断打扰他的工作，令他痛苦不堪，沮丧不已。

一天傍晚，利奥在读一位著名工程师的传记时，发现了一张奇怪的便条：“要装配出完美的结构，你必须首先理解材料的化合物。”受此启发，他决定用古老技艺来补充自己的设计。他观察了栖息在悬崖上的海鸟群落，注意到它们的巢穴如何限制热量流失，同时保持粗糙却坚固的特点。

不久后，他女儿婚礼的日子到了。仪式进行到一半时，人群中突然爆发出一阵惊慌，因为旧塔的基座出现了一道裂缝。利奥冲了过去，简直是凭着本能奔跑。他命令工人搬来轮子和滑轮，经过快速的团队协作，他们稳住了结构，一直忙到夜幕降临。

那天晚上，利奥用灯笼装饰了塔身，全镇居民欢呼雀跃。他的设计不仅实用，更是一件杰作。这座塔屹立不倒，成为坚韧的象征，而利奥的名字也因此值得被铭记。
