**连锁故事**

In a quiet village, the autumn **harvest** (收获) was finally coming to an end. Farmer Eli found a **mouse** (鼠) nibbling at his grain sacks, and a sly **fox** (狐狸) watching from the fence. Eli poured some **liquid** (液体) – a sweet apple cider – into a bowl to distract them. He remembered his **ancestor** (祖先), old Grandpa Theo, who had once told him: “A true **triumph** (胜利) is not about crushing every pest, but about finding balance.” The village **folk** (民间音乐、人们) often sang that saying at their **folk** (人们) gatherings, and Eli felt **guilty** (内疚) whenever he forgot it. So he gave the mouse and the fox **permission** (允许、许可) to share the orchard’s fallen fruit. He watched the cider **pour** (倾倒) slowly into the bowl, a golden stream that was almost the **equivalent** (相同的) of honey in sweetness. But Eli knew his **ultimate** (终极) goal was not to feed wildlife – it was to prevent **excess** (过剩、过量) waste. He built a small irrigation **stream** (溪流、流淌) from the nearby spring to water his crops, avoiding the **vice** (恶习、邪恶、缺点) of laziness. He stored **coal** (煤) for winter, but that afternoon, a sudden storm caused a **setback** (挫折) – his barn roof leaked. With the **deadline** (最后期限) for the market fair approaching, he had no time to fix everything. His **spelling** (拼写) of the market sign was also wrong, which made him laugh. Yet nothing would **hinder** (阻碍) his spirit. He patched the roof, corrected the sign, and shared his cider with the whole village, feeling truly triumphant.

**中文翻译**

在一个宁静的村庄里，秋天的收获（harvest）终于接近尾声。农民伊莱发现一只老鼠（mouse）正在啃他的粮袋，一只狡猾的狐狸（fox）则在栅栏上观望。伊莱倒了一些液体（liquid）——甜甜的苹果酒——到碗里想引开它们。他想起了自己的祖先（ancestor），老爷爷西奥，曾对他说过：“真正的胜利（triumph）不是消灭每一只害虫，而是找到平衡。”村里的人们（folk）常在聚会上唱起这句话，而伊莱每忘记它都会感到内疚（guilty）。于是他给了老鼠和狐狸允许（permission）分享果园里掉落的果子。他看着苹果酒缓缓倾倒（pour）进碗中，那金色的细流甜得几乎与蜂蜜相同（equivalent）。但伊莱知道自己的终极（ultimate）目标不是喂野生动物——而是防止过剩（excess）浪费。他建了一条小灌溉溪流（stream），从附近泉水引水浇田，避免懒惰这一恶习（vice）。他储存了煤（coal）准备过冬，但那天下午，一场突如其来的暴风雨造成了挫折（setback）——谷仓屋顶漏了。眼看集市最后期限（deadline）临近，他已没时间修好所有东西。他连市场招牌的拼写（spelling）都写错了，这让他自己都笑了。然而，没有什么能阻碍（hinder）他的精神。他补好了屋顶，更正了招牌，并把苹果酒分给全村人喝，心中充满胜利的喜悦。
