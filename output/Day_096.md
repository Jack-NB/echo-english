**The Chain Story**

The **journalist** (记者) hurried into the courtroom, notebook in hand. The **judgment** (裁判) of the case would decide whether the city’s economic **boom** (繁荣) was legal or a fraud. He sat down **fairly** (公正地) quietly, trying not to disturb the tense atmosphere. The judge, a calm and **stable** (稳定的) man, announced the start of the **contest** (比赛) between the prosecution and the defense. The accusation was that the company had made **illegal** (不合法的) deals in a special trade **zone** (区域). **Besides** (除了之外) the money issue, there was also a question of **philosophy** (哲学) — whether profit could justify breaking rules. The lawyer tried to **modify** (修改) the charges, arguing that the business had helped **rural** (乡村的) farmers, almost like a **god** (神) to the poor. But the prosecutor was **reluctant** (不情愿的) to accept any excuse. He pointed to a **sharp** (清晰的) contradiction: the company’s records were not **adequate** (充足的) to prove honesty. Even the **milk** (牛奶) they donated to villages came from suspicious sources. The truth remained a **mystery** (神秘), and the court needed high **literacy** (读写能力) to read the complex documents. Suddenly, a fire alarm rang — an **emergency** (紧急情况) forced everyone to evacuate. The journalist smiled; tomorrow’s headline would be even more dramatic.
