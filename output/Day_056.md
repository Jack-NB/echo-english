In a foggy morning, a detective had to **pick** (挑) the right clue from a messy table. He knew the **overall** (全面的、总体上) picture would only emerge if he could **succeed** (成功、接替) in connecting the old case with a new one. A sudden **strike** (击打) of lightning made him pause; he decided to **examine** (检查) every photograph again, following the **instruction** (指令、教导) written in a torn notebook. His partner (伙伴) warned that the case might hide a deadly **cancer** (癌症) of corruption, but the detective refused to **judge** (判断) too quickly. Instead, he tried to **divide** (划分) the evidence into three piles: the trivial, the suspicious, and the vital. He knew he could **rely** (依靠) on his memory, especially the **former** (以前的、前者) interviews with witnesses. For **instance** (例子), one witness had mentioned a locked room on the top **floor** (地面) of the old hotel. That detail could **release** (释放、解除) a hidden truth. The detective walked to the **front** (前面的) entrance, where a guard tried to **harm** (伤害) him with a baton, but he dodged and managed to **remove** (移除) the obstacle. His **partner** (伙伴) covered him from behind. The clues seemed to **imply** (暗示) that the killer was someone who respected **tradition** (传统) too much — a man who refused to let the past die. With a final push, the detective opened the rusty door, and the case finally broke open like a ripe fruit.

**中文翻译：**

在一个雾蒙蒙的早晨，一位侦探不得不从一张凌乱的桌子上**挑**出正确的线索。他知道，只有当他能够成功地将旧案与新案**接替**起来，**全面的**图景才会浮现。一道闪电的突然**击打**让他停顿了一下；他决定按照一本破旧笔记本上写下的**指令**，重新**检查**每一张照片。他的**伙伴**警告说，这个案子可能隐藏着腐败的致命“**癌症**”，但侦探拒绝过早地做出**判断**。相反，他试图将证据**划分**成三堆：琐碎的、可疑的和关键的。他知道自己可以**依靠**记忆，尤其是**以前**对证人的采访。**举个例子**，一位证人提到过老旅馆顶层**地面**上的一间上锁的房间。这个细节可能**释放**出一个隐藏的真相。侦探走到**前面的**入口，一名警卫试图用警棍**伤害**他，但他躲开了，并设法**移除**了障碍。他的**伙伴**从后方掩护他。这些线索似乎**暗示**，凶手是一个过分尊重**传统**的人——一个拒绝让过去消亡的人。最后猛推一下，侦探打开了那扇生锈的门，案子终于像熟透的水果一样裂开了。
