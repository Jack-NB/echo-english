**连锁故事**

In a small town, the **enemy** (敌人) of all progress was laziness. A young engineer, tired of **abstract** (抽象的) theories, decided to build an **automobile** (汽车) that ran on pure willpower. He was **hungry** (饥饿的) for success, and this **motivated** (激发) him to work day and night. His only **possession** (所有物、财产) was a rusty toolbox, but he refused to wait for **rescue** (拯救) from others. He would **calculate** (计算) every angle, test every **lane** (小路、泳道), and **lift** (提起) heavy parts with raw strength. Soon, his garage became an **assembly** (集合、装配) line of creativity. He managed to **rid** (摆脱) his doubts, and with **upper** (上面的) confidence, he treated his prototype like a beloved **pet** (宠物). He cleaned every **dish** (碟子) of grease, drew a detailed **chart** (图表) of circuits, and waited for the **arrival** (到达) of the test day. His workshop felt like a mechanical **kingdom** (王国). He learned to **compose** (组成、作曲、撰写) a perfect engine rhythm, and **readily** (快捷地、乐意地) shared his plans with the town. The car roared to life, and the **enemy** of laziness was defeated forever.
