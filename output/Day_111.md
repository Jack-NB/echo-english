**连锁故事**

The archaeologist, Dr. Elena, held the **ownership** (所有) of a rare map that showed where ancient civilizations had **originated** (发源). She boarded a small **aircraft** (航空器) to reach a remote island, guided by a strict personal **ethic** (伦理): never disturb sacred sites for profit. The island’s **external** (外部的) appearance was barren, but beneath the soil lay a **universal** (普遍的) truth about human history. As her metal detector began to **react** (反应) wildly, she knew she had found something significant. A local elder, the **representative** (代表) of the tribe, approached her. He warned that the land held **substantial** (大量的) spiritual power, and any excavation required a sacred **qualification** (资格) — a ritual passed down for generations. Elena agreed to **retain** (保留) all artifacts in place until the ceremony. The elder smiled, revealing a missing tooth in his **mouth** (嘴), and said, “We will hold a **poll** (投票) among the tribe to decide your fate.” The decision was communicated **via** (经过) drumbeats across the valley. That night, Elena listened to the **myth** (神话) of the Sky Serpent, whose bones, they said, guaranteed the **stability** (稳定) of the island. Suddenly, a **taxi** (出租车) — an old jeep serving as the island’s only transport — arrived, carrying a doctor who had come to treat a sick child. As the jeep’s wheels began to **slip** (滑) on the muddy path, Elena reached into her **pocket** (口袋) for a rope. She helped pull the vehicle to safety. The elder nodded. “You have proven your heart,” he said. Later that night, she found a **fossil** (化石) embedded in the cave wall — the serpent’s spine. It was not a myth; it was a reality. The tribe voted to let her stay, and Elena realized that true discovery requires not just knowledge, but respect.

**中文翻译**

考古学家埃琳娜博士拥有一张珍贵的地图，上面标明了古代文明的发源地。她登上一架小型航空器，前往一座偏远岛屿，并遵循一条严格的个人伦理：绝不为利益打扰圣地。岛屿的外部看起来荒芜，但土壤之下埋藏着关于人类历史的普遍真理。当她的金属探测器开始剧烈反应时，她知道发现了重要的东西。一位当地长者——部落的代表——走近她，警告说这片土地拥有大量的精神力量，任何挖掘都需要一项神圣的资格——一种世代相传的仪式。埃琳娜同意将所有文物保留在原地，直到仪式完成。长者笑了，露出嘴里缺失的一颗牙，说：“我们会在部落中举行一次投票来决定你的去留。”决定通过山谷中的鼓声传递。那晚，埃琳娜聆听了天空巨蛇的神话，据说它的骨头保证了岛屿的稳定。突然，一辆出租车——岛上唯一的交通工具，一辆旧吉普——驶来，载着一位来治病童的医生。吉普车的车轮在泥泞的路上开始打滑，埃琳娜从口袋里掏出一根绳子，帮助把车拉回安全地带。长者点了点头：“你已证明了你的心。”当晚晚些时候，她在洞穴墙壁上发现了一块化石——巨蛇的脊椎。那不是神话，而是现实。部落投票让她留下，埃琳娜意识到，真正的发现不仅需要知识，更需要尊重。
