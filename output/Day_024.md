She was looking at the **average** (平均的) sales report, and her **eye** (眼睛) caught my **fast** (快) progress. She praised my **behavior** (行为), which was **common** (普通的) to see from others but rare for me. The **light** (点亮) in her face made me feel lighter. We discussed the **global** (全球的) market **condition** (条件) and the **difference** (不同) between our old and new strategies. I realized that **growth** (生长) comes from stepping out of your comfort zone. After the meeting, I went to the **shop** (购物) to buy a gift—a small token of thanks. The **growth** (生长) of our trust was the real reward. **Although** (尽管) it was a small gesture, it **light** (点亮) up her day. That day, I learned that **meeting** (会议) people with sincerity can change everything.
