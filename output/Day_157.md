**连锁故事 (English Story)**

Early in the morning, Tom decided to **patch** (补丁、修补) his old jacket with a piece of cloth while sipping a cup of **tea** (茶). He was in deep **misery** (痛苦) because his landlord, a stern **lord** (主) of the old estate, had just raised the rent. Tom’s **rage** (大怒、狂怒) boiled inside him, but he tried to stay **elegant** (优雅的) in his movements, though a clear **flaw** (缺陷) in his plan was that he had no money. He needed to **formulate** (规划、制订) a new budget, keeping a **moderate** (温和的、适度的) pace of spending. His only **reliance** (信赖) was his part-time job, but the financial **tide** (潮水、潮流) was against him. He made a silent **pledge** (保证、誓言) to never give up. Suddenly, a **snap** (猛咬、拍摄、突然的) of a branch made him jump — a squirrel **undoubtedly** (无疑地) causing trouble. He checked his **visa** (签证) application status, hoping to move abroad, and spread his **wing** (翼) to escape. Every day he had to **commute** (通勤) two hours to the city, but he didn’t **dare** (敢) to complain. He stayed **energetic** (精力旺盛的、能量的) by drinking black coffee, and finally, he raised a white **flag** (旗) of surrender — not to defeat, but to his old habits.

**中文翻译**

清晨，汤姆一边喝着茶（tea），一边决定用一块布给旧夹克打上补丁（patch）。他陷入深深的痛苦（misery）之中，因为那位严厉的领主（lord）——他的房东，刚刚涨了房租。汤姆的怒火（rage）在胸中翻腾，但他努力保持优雅（elegant）的姿态，尽管他的计划有一个明显的缺陷（flaw）：他没钱。他需要制订（formulate）一份新的预算，保持适度（moderate）的消费节奏。他唯一的信赖（reliance）就是那份兼职工作，但财务的潮流（tide）正与他作对。他默默立下誓言（pledge），绝不放弃。突然，一根树枝猛地折断（snap），吓得他一跳——无疑地（undoubtedly）是只松鼠在捣乱。他查看了自己的签证（visa）申请状态，希望能移居海外，展开翅膀（wing）逃离。每天他必须通勤（commute）两小时进城，但他不敢（dare）抱怨。他靠喝黑咖啡保持精力旺盛（energetic），最后，他举起了一面白旗（flag）——不是向失败投降，而是向旧习惯告别。
