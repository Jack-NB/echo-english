**连锁故事**

In a small town, a **beneficial** (有益的) **benefit** (益处、好处) program (项目) was launched for every **worker** (工人) with a **low** (低的) income. The **market** (市场) had been struggling, so the town decided to **teach** (教) people new skills. A **supportive** (支持的) **support** (支持) team gathered **among** (在……之中) the residents, showing great **kindness** (仁慈) and a **kind** (仁慈的、种类) heart to every **friend** (朋友). They created a **skillful** (熟练的) **skill** (技术) training center. **Yet** (然而), the first month was **rather** (相当的) hard. No one had seen a **formal** (正式的) **form** (形式、形成) of such training before. The courses **included** (包括) everything from basic math to digital design. Students learned how to **move** (移动) from old habits to new methods. **Nevertheless** (然而), they **never** (从不) gave up. By the end of the **century** (世纪), the town had transformed. No longer was the situation **badly** (糟糕地) **bad** (坏的). Instead, every worker became a **creator** (创造者) of their own future, and the **movement** (运动) spread across the region. The **friendship** (友谊) formed during that time became the foundation of a new community.

**中文翻译**

在一个小镇上，为所有低收入工人启动了一个有益（benefit）的项目（program）。市场（market）一直不景气，所以镇上决定教（teach）人们新技能。一个支持（support）团队在居民之中（among）集结，对每一位朋友（friend）展现出极大的仁慈（kind）与善意。他们创建了一个熟练的（skill）技术培训中心。然而（yet），第一个月相当（rather）艰难。没有人见过这种正式（form）形式的培训。课程包括（include）从基础数学到数字设计的一切内容。学生们学会了如何从旧习惯移动（move）到新方法。然而（nevertheless），他们从不（never）放弃。到这个世纪（century）结束时，小镇发生了转变。情况不再糟糕（bad）。相反，每位工人都成了自己未来的创造者（create），这场运动（movement）传遍了整个地区。那时结下的友谊（friend）成为了新社区的基石。
