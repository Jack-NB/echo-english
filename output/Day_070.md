**英文故事**

The river began to **flow** (流动) gently at dawn. A young engineer, proud of her new **technique** (技术), planned to build a bridge across it. She had just **woken** (醒来) from a strange dream that seemed to **hurt** (伤害) her chest, but she ignored it. Her design **consisted** (组成) of three arches, and she hoped to **master** (掌握) every detail before noon. A local child gave her a **smile** (笑), which helped **convince** (使人信服) her that the project was worth the risk.

However, a sudden storm threatened to **delay** (推迟) the work. The river became **broad** (宽的) and wild, and the rain was not **sufficient** (足够的) to wash away the mud – it only made things worse. **Mostly** (大部分) of the workers were exhausted, **unlike** (不像) their cheerful boss. The storm threatened to **destroy** (摧毁) the scaffolding, and the forecast predicted heavy **meat** (肉类) shortages for the week, as supply trucks were stuck. The boss decided to **wage** (发动) a rapid repair campaign, using his clear **vision** (想象) of the final bridge. He was a **wealthy** (富裕的) man, but he never boasted. Instead, he planned to **exhibit** (展览) the completed bridge at a local fair.

They managed to **block** (阻止) the flood with sandbags, and by evening the sky cleared. The engineer’s **mastery** (精通) of materials saved the day. The bridge stood solid, and the child’s **smiling** (微笑的) face was the best reward. That night, the **conviction** (信念) that hard work conquers all filled the air.

**中文翻译**

黎明时分，河水开始缓缓**流动**。一位年轻的工程师，为自己掌握的新**技术**而自豪，计划在河上建一座桥。她刚从一场奇怪的梦中**醒来**，那梦似乎让她的胸口阵阵**伤害**，但她没有理会。她的设计**组成**了三个拱形结构，她希望在中午前**掌握**每一个细节。一个当地的孩子对她**笑**了笑，这帮助她**使人信服**——这个项目值得冒险。

然而，一场突如其来的暴风雨威胁着要**推迟**工程。河流变得**宽的**而狂暴，雨水不足以冲走泥浆——反而让情况更糟。**大部分**工人都精疲力竭，**不像**他们那位乐观的老板。暴风雨威胁着要**摧毁**脚手架，天气预报还预测本周**肉类**会严重短缺，因为供应卡车被困住了。老板决定**发动**一次快速修复行动，凭借他对最终桥梁的清晰**想象**。他是个**富裕的**人，但从不炫耀。相反，他计划在当地的博览会上**展览**这座完工的桥。

他们用沙袋**阻止**了洪水，傍晚时分，天空放晴了。工程师对材料的**精通**挽救了局面。桥梁稳固地立住了，孩子**微笑的**脸是最好的回报。那天晚上，一种**信念**——努力可以征服一切——弥漫在空气中。
