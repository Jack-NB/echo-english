**英文连锁故事**

On a foggy morning, a courier received a **package** (包裹) that felt suspiciously heavy. Inside, he found a **genuine** (真的、真诚的) antique music box, which reminded him of a **concert** (音乐会) he once attended. Suddenly, a **helicopter** (直升机) roared overhead, dropping a note from an **amateur** (业余爱好者、外行) detective who claimed this was no **exception** (例外) — every delivery was part of a strange **incident** (事件). The courier decided to **permit** (允许) himself a closer look, and discovered the **core** (核心) of the music box contained a microchip. This was **unexpected** (想不到的), and he could **barely** (几乎不) believe his eyes. He searched **anywhere** (在任何地方) for clues, and his **fellow** (同事、同辈) workers helped him trace the chip to a company that **found** (创立、建立) a secret research lab. The lab’s main **occupation** (占领、职业) was studying sound waves, and their biggest **opponent** (对手) was a rival **army** (军队、陆军) of hackers. Just as the courier prepared the **delivery** (递送、分娩) of evidence to the police, he felt a sudden **stretch** (伸展、延伸) of his arm — the chip was vibrating. It triggered a silent **alert** (警报), and within minutes, the lab’s lights went dark. The courier smiled: the case was finally closed.
