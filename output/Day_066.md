**连锁故事**

In a small seaside town, a diligent **pupil** (学生) named Leo was walking to his **church** (教堂) to meet his **brother** (兄弟) Sam. Sam worked as an insurance agent, and he had just sold a new **insurance** (保险) policy to a fisherman. The fisherman had been worried about his old **ship** (船舶), which was worth a **fine** (很好) sum of money. Leo, however, was more concerned about his own **objective** (目的): to **commit** (把…交托给) his time to studying marine biology.

As they walked, Sam began to **complain** (抱怨) about a customer who tried to **exchange** (交换) a damaged net without a **label** (标签). Leo listened but his mind drifted to the **context** (上下文、环境) of his textbook, where he read about how fish **differ** (与……不同) in their **contribution** (贡献) to the ecosystem. He wanted to **admit** (承认) to Sam that he had failed his last exam, but he hesitated.

Suddenly, they saw a farmer struggling to **yield** (放弃、屈服) his heavy cart on the muddy **soil** (泥土、国土). Leo and Sam helped push the cart, and the farmer offered them a **rent** (租) discount on his field for their future experiments. Sam smiled and said, “Your **criticism** (批评) of my job is fair, but see how even here, every action has its **video** (录像) record in memory.” Leo laughed, realizing that his **pupil** status wasn’t a burden—it was a **ship** (装运) of new ideas, ready to sail.
