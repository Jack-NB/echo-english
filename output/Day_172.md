**连锁故事**

The **dealer** (经销商) parked his truck near the **lawn** (草坪), feeling a sudden **fever** (发烧) from the sun. He had forgotten to **forgive** (原谅) his partner for the messy **disposal** (处理) of old furniture. As he walked toward the **lodge** (小屋), he saw a bird's **nest** (窝) under the eaves, holding a tiny **pill** (药丸) – probably dropped by a careless tourist. He smiled, thinking of the **spectrum** (光谱) of colors in the sky at dusk.

Inside, he found a **turkey** (火鸡) strutting near the **bathroom** (浴室) door. His **chest** (胸) tightened with a **foolish** (愚蠢的) idea: to catch it. But the bird escaped, making him **frustrate** (挫败、使灰心). He lit a **lamp** (灯) and called his **league** (联盟) colleague, who suggested using a **tablet** (平板电脑) to track the bird. The **affluent** (富裕的) friend also mentioned a **cabinet** (内阁) meeting about traffic **collision** (碰撞) risks.

Later, the dealer realized the **pill** was actually a vitamin for the bird. He **disposed** of it properly, and felt a wave of **forgiveness** (宽恕) toward his partner. The **nestled** (依偎) bird returned, and the dealer laughed, his **feverish** (狂热的) energy finally cooled under the **lamplight** (灯光). He **collided** (碰撞) with the doorframe in his haste, but it only made him chuckle. Some days, you just have to **deal** (交易) with chaos – and that's okay.

**中文翻译**

经销商（dealer）把卡车停在草坪（lawn）旁，被太阳晒得突然发起烧（fever）来。他忘了原谅（forgive）合伙人在处理（disposal）旧家具时的混乱。当他走向小屋（lodge）时，看到屋檐下有个鸟窝（nest），里面躺着一颗小药丸（pill）——大概是粗心的游客掉落的。他笑了，想起黄昏天空中的光谱（spectrum）。

进屋后，他发现一只火鸡（turkey）在浴室（bathroom）门口昂首阔步。他的胸（chest）因一个愚蠢的（foolish）念头而绷紧：抓住它。但鸟逃走了，让他感到挫败（frustrate）。他点亮灯（lamp），打电话给联盟（league）中的同事，对方建议用平板电脑（tablet）追踪那只鸟。这位富裕的（affluent）朋友还提到内阁（cabinet）要开关于交通碰撞（collision）风险的会议。

后来，经销商意识到那颗药丸其实是鸟的维生素。他妥善处理（disposed）了它，并对合伙人生出一阵宽恕（forgiveness）之情。那只依偎（nestled）的鸟回来了，经销商笑了，他狂热的（feverish）精力终于在灯光（lamplight）下冷却。他匆忙中撞（collided）上门框，却只是让他轻笑。有些日子，你只需应付（deal）混乱——而这就够了。
