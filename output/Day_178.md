**连锁故事 (英文)**

In a tiny workshop, an old stove (炉子) hissed while a vain (徒劳的) inventor tried to fix it—all his efforts were **vain** because the metal was cracked. A bold (大胆的) move, he thought, would be to redesign the whole machine. With sudden **clarity** (清楚), he realized the dirt (污垢、泥土) clogging the pipes had caused the failure. He decided to disclose (揭露、公开) his findings to the public, a **disclosure** that shocked everyone. His new design would embody (包含) simplicity itself—an **embodiment** of efficiency. To make it work, he had to inject (注入) steam at higher pressure, a risky **injection** process. Nearby, an ancient loom (织布机) clattered, weaving **loomstate** fabric that filled the spacious (宽敞的) hall—so **spacious** that an astronaut (宇航员) could practice walking there, just as **astronauts** do in training. He called a meeting to confer (商讨) with his team; during the conference (会议), they reached a firm conviction (定罪) that the old design was faulty, and they **convicted** it without mercy. His eyesight (视力), though weak, was sharp enough to spot a tiny crack—such **insight** saved the project. He ordered new fabric (织物) for the seals, but the supplier tried to **fabricate** a cheap version, which broke into a fragment (片段) on the first test—a **fragile** piece indeed. The whole system needed to be operational (操作的、运作的) by dawn, so they **operated** nonstop. He sent a telegraph (电报) to headquarters, a message **telegraphed** in code, asking for spare parts. Even the zoo (动物园) across the street sent a monkey to watch, a **zoological** curiosity. The whole plan seemed absurd (荒唐、可笑的), yet in its **absurdity**, it worked perfectly.

**中文翻译**

在一间狭小的作坊里，一只老旧的炉子（stove）发出嘶嘶声，一个徒劳的（vain）发明家试图修理它——他所有的努力都是徒劳的，因为金属已经裂了。他想，一个大胆的（bold）举动就是重新设计整台机器。突然，他清楚地（clarity）意识到，堵塞管道的污垢、泥土（dirt）才是故障的根源。他决定向公众揭露、公开（disclose）他的发现，这一披露（disclosure）震惊了所有人。他的新设计将包含（embody）极简本身——它是效率的化身（embodiment）。为了让它运转，他必须以更高压力注入（inject）蒸汽，这是一个危险的注射（injection）过程。旁边，一台古老的织布机（loom）咔嗒作响，织出坯布（loomstate），填满了宽敞的（spacious）大厅——如此宽敞，以至于宇航员（astronaut）可以在那里练习行走，就像宇航员们在训练中做的那样。他召集团队开会商讨（confer）；在会议（conference）中，他们达成坚定的定罪（conviction）：旧设计有缺陷，他们毫不留情地定罪（convict）了它。他的视力（eyesight）虽弱，却足以发现一条细缝——这种洞察力（insight）拯救了项目。他订购了新的织物（fabric）做密封件，但供应商试图捏造（fabricate）廉价版本，结果在第一次测试时就碎成了片段（fragment）——确实是一块易碎的（fragile）东西。整个系统必须在黎明前进入操作的、运作的（operational）状态，所以他们不停地操作（operate）。他发了一封电报（telegraph）给总部，以密码拍发（telegraphed）消息，索要备用零件。甚至街对面的动物园（zoo）也送来一只猴子观看，真是个动物学（zoological）奇观。整个计划看似荒唐、可笑的（absurd），然而在这种荒谬（absurdity）之中，它却完美地运转了。
