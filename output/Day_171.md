**Depart** (离开) the noisy city, Alice decided to visit her **grandmother** ((外)祖母) in the countryside. She had always admired her grandmother's **endurance** (忍耐) through life's hardships. On the train, she caught a **glimpse** (瞥见) of a strange man who seemed to be examining everyone with intense **scrutiny** (监视). He carried a small **probe** (探测、探针) device, which he occasionally pointed at passengers.

Alice felt uneasy. She knew her own **shortcoming** (缺点) was being too trusting, and she tried to stay **subordinate** (次要的、下级的) in her observations, not drawing attention. But the man seemed to **drag** (拉、拽) his gaze toward her. She worried about the **expenditure** (开支) of her trip, but this was a **fantasy** (幻想) she had held since childhood — to live in a world of **grey** (灰色的) simplicity, far from urban chaos.

Suddenly, the man approached and, to her shock, whispered an **insult** (侮辱) about her luggage. She realized this was a **persuasion** (说服) tactic — he wanted her to lower her guard. She pulled the **plug** (塞、插头) on her headphones and listened carefully. What he said next was a **revelation** (启示): the train had been rerouted due to a security threat. His job was to **terrify** (使恐怖、使害怕) potential troublemakers into leaving. He was using an **algorithm** (算法) to identify suspicious behavior patterns.

Alice smiled and decided to **boycott** (拒绝参加) her own fear. She thanked him and moved to another carriage, where the view of the hills seemed to **coincide** (一致) perfectly with her childhood memories of her grandmother's home.

### 中文翻译

爱丽丝决定离开喧嚣的城市，去乡下看望她的祖母。她一直钦佩祖母在生活艰辛中表现出的忍耐。在火车上，她瞥见一个奇怪的男人，他似乎正用敏锐的监视目光审视着每一个人。他带着一个小型探测装置，不时地指向乘客。

爱丽丝感到不安。她知道自己最大的缺点是过于轻信他人，于是她尽量在观察中保持低调，不引起注意。但那个男人似乎把目光拖向她。她担心这次旅行的开支，但这是她从小就怀有的幻想——生活在一个灰色的简朴世界里，远离城市的混乱。

突然，那个男人走近她，令她震惊的是，他低声侮辱了她的行李。她意识到这是一种说服策略——他想让她放松警惕。她拔掉耳机的插头，仔细倾听。接下来他说的内容是一个启示：火车因安全威胁而改变了路线。他的工作是让潜在的捣乱者感到恐惧而离开。他正使用一种算法来识别可疑的行为模式。

爱丽丝微微一笑，决定拒绝参与自己的恐惧。她感谢了他，然后移到另一节车厢，那里的山景与她童年记忆中祖母家的景象完美地一致。
