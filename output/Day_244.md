The morning was **frosty** (霜冻的) as the explorer stepped onto the ancient **glacier** (冰川). He recalled the **glorious** (辉煌的) stories of his predecessors, but today the view was **grim** (可怕的). At the base camp, the **grocer** (杂货店) had packed extra rations; the man had to **grope** (探索) through his bag for a map. The map was attached to a metal **hinge** (铰链), an **honorable** (光荣的) relic from his grandfather. He unfolded it on a **horizontal** (水平的) rock, then blew a **horn** (喇叭) to signal his team. He smiled, thinking of the **millionaire** (百万富翁) who funded this expedition — a man who never **minus** (减去) a single expense for science. The explorer checked his compass made of **nickel** (镍) alloy. He wrote a quick note, using a **noun** (名词) for every landmark, following the **orthodox** (正统的) method of navigation. Finally, he stood up, **gloriously** (辉煌地) ready to conquer the frozen peak.
