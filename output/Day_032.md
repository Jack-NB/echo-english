**Story**

The detective gathered **evidence** (证据) from the crime scene, and **together** (一共) with his partner, they decided to **speed** (加速) the investigation. They walked through the **middle** (中间的) of the crowded market, **along** (沿着) the narrow alley, where a suspicious **sale** (销售) of stolen goods was rumored. The **production** (生产) of fake documents had become a local problem, and every **grade** (年级) of society seemed affected. They decided to **visit** (参观、访问) the warehouse, whose **content** (内容、目录、满足的) was mostly counterfeit watches. The **source** (来源) of the goods traced back to a small factory, but **security** (安全) there was tight, adding **pressure** (压力) to the case. There was a **gap** (间隙、分歧) between the suspects' stories, and the detectives had to go **across** (穿过) the river to find more clues. They didn't **let** (让) any detail slip, knowing their success would **depend** (依赖) on careful observation, **especially** (尤其) regarding the **modern** (现代的) printing techniques used. In the end, the solution was **simply** (仅仅) a matter of connecting the dots.

**中文翻译**

侦探从犯罪现场收集了**证据**，**一共**和搭档决定**加速**调查。他们穿过拥挤市场的**中间**，**沿着**狭窄的小巷，那里传闻有一笔可疑的赃物**销售**。假文件的**生产**成了当地问题，社会各个**年级**阶层似乎都受影响。他们决定**参观、访问**那个仓库，其**内容、目录、满足的**大多是假表。货物的**来源**追溯到一个工厂，但那里的**安全**很严密，给案件增加了**压力**。嫌疑人陈述之间存在**间隙、分歧**，侦探不得不**穿过**河流寻找更多线索。他们不**让**任何细节溜走，知道成功将**依赖**于仔细观察，**尤其**是关于所用的**现代的**印刷技术。最后，答案**仅仅**是把线索串联起来而已。
