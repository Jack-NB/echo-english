**Chain Story**

Walking down the grand **avenue** (大街) of the old city, I passed a wooden **barrel** (桶) that had been tipped over by the wind. Beside it stood a small **cabin** (机舱、小木屋) with smoke curling from its chimney, though the owner seemed to **conceal** (隐藏、隐瞒) his presence behind drawn curtains. I counted **eleven** (十一) steps to the next corner, where a faded **formula** (公式) scratched on the wall reminded me of a physics lesson. A stray **basket** (篮子) lay under a bench, perhaps left by a vendor who was no longer **eligible** (合适的) to sell here due to new rules.

The sky was **immense** (巨大的), and I felt a sudden urge to **indulge** (迁就、放任) my curiosity by following a trail of cut **log** (圆木、原木) that led into the woods. The path was strangely **magnetic** (有吸引力的), pulling me forward as if guided by a force stronger than will. After **seventy** (七十) meters, I saw a **slim** (苗条的) figure moving quickly between trees — someone who seemed to **surpass** (超越) ordinary human speed. I tried to **bend** (弯曲) down to hide, but my foot snapped a twig. The figure turned, revealing a face marked by years of **charity** (慈善) work — I recognized her from a documentary. She raised a hand as if to **clarify** (澄清) something, but I was too stunned to speak. Her eyes seemed to **defy** (反抗、藐视) any explanation, and in that moment I felt a wave of **gratitude** (感激) for the strange luck that had brought me here.

**中文翻译**

沿着老城宽阔的大街（avenue）行走时，我路过一个被风吹倒的木桶（barrel）。桶旁有一座小木屋（cabin），烟囱冒着烟，但主人似乎拉上窗帘隐藏（conceal）自己的行踪。我数到第十一（eleven）步来到下一个街角，墙上褪色的公式（formula）让我想起一堂物理课。长椅下躺着一只篮子（basket），可能是某个因新规不再合适（eligible）在此摆摊的商贩留下的。

天空巨大（immense），我突然想迁就（indulge）自己的好奇心，跟随一条由砍下的圆木（log）铺成的小路走进树林。那条路有种奇特的吸引力（magnetic），仿佛被某种比意志更强的力量牵引。走了七十（seventy）米，我看到一个苗条（slim）的身影在树间快速移动——似乎超越（surpass）了常人速度。我试图弯腰（bend）躲藏，但脚下踩断了一根树枝。那人转过身，露出一张刻满慈善（charity）工作痕迹的脸庞——我从纪录片里认识她。她举手似乎想澄清（clarify）什么，但我震惊得说不出话。她的眼神仿佛反抗（defy）任何解释，那一刻，我对这奇遇涌起一阵感激（gratitude）。
