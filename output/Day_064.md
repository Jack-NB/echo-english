I wore a **formal** (正式的、形式的) uniform, though I doubted it was **criminal** (犯罪活动、罪犯) to fly so casually. The cockpit felt **familiar** (熟悉的) now, and my co-pilot was **friendly** (友好的). Later, I learned that my **genetic** (基因的) profile made me prone to joint pain, so I had to stay **competitive** (竞争的) in my fitness routine. That flight changed me forever.
