**The Canvas of Farewell**

In a dusty workshop, an old artist stretched a **canvas** (帆布) over a wooden frame, his **chin** (下巴) resting on his hand. He was a former **cashier** (出纳员) who had traded coins for colors, and now he prepared to paint a **chorus** (合唱) of memory—a tribute to the **civilisation** (文明) his ancestors had built from **clay** (黏土) bricks.

His studio was a **compartment** (间隔、划分) of the soul, divided into shelves of pigments and jars of brushes. He had always acted with **compassion** (同情) toward the world, yet his own heart felt like a trapped **deer** (鹿) caught in a thicket of regret. He refused to **detain** (扣留) the past any longer, and chose instead to **deviate** (偏离) from his grief.

Measuring the **diameter** (直径) of a circular palette, he mixed ochre and blue with **discretion** (谨慎). His **ego** (自我) had long dissolved; he no longer painted for fame, but for truth. His brushstrokes were **eloquent** (雄辩的), each line a word in a silent speech. He framed the scene with a small **enclosure** (围墙、附件) of gold leaf, a symbol of the boundaries we build around our memories.

At dusk, he whispered **farewell** (告别) to the unfinished work, then **fling** (投掷) his brush into the air as a final gesture. His hand had become **fluent** (流利的) in the language of light, and the only **friction** (摩擦) left was the soft scrape of his breath against the silence.

**中文翻译**

在一间布满灰尘的工作室里，一位老画家在木框上绷紧了一块**帆布**（canvas），他的**下巴**（chin）托在手上。他曾是一名**出纳员**（cashier），用硬币换来了颜料，此刻他准备描绘一曲记忆的**合唱**（chorus）——向他的祖先用**黏土**（clay）砖块建成的**文明**（civilisation）致敬。

他的工作室是灵魂的一个**间隔**（compartment），被分隔成放颜料的架子和放画笔的罐子。他向来以**同情**（compassion）对待世界，然而自己的内心却像一只被困在悔恨灌木丛中的**鹿**（deer）。他不再愿意**扣留**（detain）过去，而是选择**偏离**（deviate）悲伤。

他测量着圆形调色板的**直径**（diameter），以**谨慎**（discretion）混合赭石与蓝色。他的**自我**（ego）早已消融；他不再为名声而画，而是为真相而画。他的笔触**雄辩**（eloquent），每一笔都是无声演讲中的一个词。他用一圈金箔作为小小的**围墙**（enclosure），框住画面——那是我们为记忆筑起的边界的象征。

黄昏时分，他对未完成的作品低声**告别**（farewell），然后**投掷**（fling）画笔至空中，作为最后的姿态。他的手已经变得**流利**（fluent）于光的语言，而剩下的唯一**摩擦**（friction），只是呼吸轻擦寂静的窸窣声。
