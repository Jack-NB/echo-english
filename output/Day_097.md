**连锁故事**

The old **bridge** (桥) over the river was covered with wild **grass** (草), where a former **slave** (奴隶) had once built a small hut after gaining freedom. He had no **privilege** (特权) in society, but he worked hard to **restore** (恢复) his dignity. One rainy night, he felt deeply **upset** (沮丧) when his **counterpart** (同行) from the city mocked his humble life. Determined to **accelerate** (加速) his progress, he entered a new **era** (时代) of learning. He realized that his earlier beliefs were **false** (错误的) — success was not a **uniform** (统一的) path. Every **pair** (一对) of hands could build something **profitable** (有利可图的). He refused to live like a bird in a **prison** (监狱) of fear. Taking a deep **breath** (呼吸), he broke the **barrier** (障碍) of self-doubt. He began to **breed** (养育) chickens, and soon his **painting** (绘画) of rural life sold well. Each **segment** (段) of his journey taught him patience. Finally, he built a small **dam** (水坝) to irrigate his fields — a symbol of his lasting strength.
