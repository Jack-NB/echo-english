## 二、连锁故事（英文）

**Title: The Awakening in the Ancient Land**

After a long night, Leo finally felt **soberly** awake (清醒). He walked to a small **stall** (货摊) in the village square, where an old woman sold vegetables. Underneath (在下面) the counter lay a rusty key that seemed to belong to a **tomb** (坟墓) nearby. Intrigued, Leo bought the key and headed toward the ancient burial site hidden in the **tropic** (热带) jungle.

The tomb was **underneath** (在下面) a massive stone slab. Inside, he found an **antique** (古董) bronze mirror. When he touched it, a voice echoed, **articulating** (善于表达的) words of an old prophecy. The voice told him to **bake** (烤) a special bread using herbs from the **pasture** (牧场) beyond the hills. Wrapping the mirror in his **blanket** (毯子), Leo felt a sudden **blast** (爆炸) of wind that nearly knocked him over. He whispered a prayer, asking the gods to **bless** (保佑) his journey.

On his way, he passed a field of **carrots** (胡萝卜), whose orange color seemed to **characterise** (以…为特征) the vibrant land. But Leo was **clumsy** (笨拙的) and tripped over a root, dropping the mirror. He had to **concede** (承认) that he needed help. He **enclosed** (附上) a note in a bottle and threw it into the river, hoping someone would find it. He began to **envisage** (想像) a helper arriving from the **oriental** (东方的) kingdom across the sea.

Days passed. Leo felt increasingly **isolated** (隔离) in the wilderness. Then one morning, a traveler appeared, holding the bottle. Together, they followed the prophecy, baked the bread, and opened the tomb’s inner chamber. Inside, they found not gold, but a scroll describing the ancient art of **sober** (清醒) meditation. Leo realized that the real treasure was the clarity he had gained — a mind as calm and clear as the morning sky.

## 三、中文翻译

**标题：古老大地上的觉醒**

经过漫长的一夜，利奥终于清醒地醒来了。他走到村庄广场上的一个小货摊前，那里有位老妇人在卖蔬菜。在柜台下面，有一把生锈的钥匙，似乎属于附近的一座坟墓。利奥被吸引了，买下钥匙，走向隐藏在热带丛林中的古老墓址。

坟墓就在一块巨大的石板下面。里面，他发现了一面古董铜镜。当他触摸它时，一个声音回响起来，清晰地诉说着一个古老的预言。那个声音告诉他，要用山那边牧场上的香草烤一种特别的面包。利奥用毯子裹好镜子，突然一阵爆炸般的狂风几乎把他掀翻。他低声祈祷，求神灵保佑他的旅程。

在路上，他经过一片胡萝卜地，那橙色的颜色似乎正是这片生机勃勃土地的特征。但利奥笨手笨脚，被树根绊了一跤，镜子掉了下来。他不得不承认自己需要帮助。他把一张字条附在瓶子里，扔进河里，希望有人能发现它。他开始想象一位来自海对岸东方王国的帮手会出现。

日子一天天过去。利奥感到自己在荒野中越来越孤立。然后一天早晨，一个旅行者出现了，手里拿着那个瓶子。他们一起遵循预言，烤了面包，打开了坟墓的内室。里面没有金子，只有一卷描述古老清醒冥想艺术的卷轴。利奥意识到，真正的宝藏是他获得的清明——一颗像晨空一样平静而清晰的心。
