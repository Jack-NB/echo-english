**连锁故事**

It was **fifty** (五十) minutes past midnight when Professor Crane received an **urgent** (紧迫的) call. The **category** (类别) of the emergency was clear: a newborn **infant** (婴幼儿) had been found abandoned near the old railway. He grabbed his coat, thinking how **convenient** (方便的) it was that the hospital lay just across the street—no **battle** (战斗) with traffic tonight. But the child was **vulnerable** (脆弱的), hypothermic, and needed immediate care. A **committee** (委员会) of doctors had already gathered, wearing white **band** (乐队、带子) armbands to identify themselves. Someone mentioned the local **football** (足球) team’s fundraiser had raised money for such cases. Professor Crane quickly began to **dress** (穿) the baby in warm blankets, while a nurse called to cancel her **vacation** (假期) to stay and help. It seemed **reasonable** (合理的) to him that everyone would do the same. There was something **odd** (奇数的、奇怪的) about the baby’s birthmark—a small star-shaped scar that held great **significance** (重要性) for the case. After a thorough **examination** (考试), they managed to **detect** (查明、发现) traces of a rare genetic marker. The **host** (主持人) of the local news arrived, wanting to interview the team. Meanwhile, a lawyer appeared, claiming the child’s family **estate** (地产、遗产) was involved. Professor Crane, showing remarkable **initiative** (主动性、进取心), proposed a DNA match with a missing person’s database. Within hours, the mystery unraveled—the infant was the heir to a fortune, and the “abandonment” was a kidnapper’s ruse. The **vulnerable** (脆弱的) little one was safe, and everyone felt the night’s chaos had been worth it.
