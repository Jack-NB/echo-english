Once upon a time, a **detective** (侦探) named Leo decided to **renovate** (翻新) his old house. He found a **swallow** (燕子) nest in the chimney and a **sweater** (毛衣) stuffed with **trash** (垃圾) in the attic. As he worked, he heard a **whisper** (低声说) from the wall — it seemed **abnormal** (不正常的). A **bee** (蜜蜂) buzzed around a **bucket** (下倾盆大雨) of paint, and Leo noticed a **defect** (缺陷) in the plaster. Suddenly, a hidden door slid open, revealing a **loose** (松的) brick with a **pig** (猪) shaped **pot** (壶) behind it. Inside the pot was an old **recipe** (食谱) for roast **poultry** (家禽), written in a **ridiculous** (荒谬的) rhyme. Leo’s **identification** (识别) of the handwriting matched a famous thief, whose plan was to **disrupt** (干扰) the city’s water supply. The **texture** (纹理) of the paper matched a note found at the crime scene. Leo smiled — the case was solved. The thief had tried to **slide** (滑) away, but Leo had already called for backup. The **renovator** (革新者) had become a hero, and the **whisperer** (耳语者) in the wall turned out to be a trapped parrot, now freed and **cheerfully** (愉快地) singing.
