**英文故事**

In a small town, there lived an old watchmaker whose **precious** (宝贵的) gold watch was his **lifetime** (一生) treasure. One evening, **amid** (在其间) the noise of a storm, he heard a knock. A **refugee** (难民) stood at the door, soaked and shivering, wearing a torn **shirt** (衬衫) and a thin **jacket** (夹克). The watchmaker offered him **accommodation** (住处) for the night. The refugee, grateful, spoke with gentle **charm** (魅力) and told his story of fleeing war. His words were **consistent** (一致的) with those of other travelers—each tale **distinct** (不同的) yet sharing the same pain. The watchmaker, wanting to **instruct** (指导) the young man, taught him how to **edit** (编辑) old clock gears. The refugee worked hard, even doing **housework** (家务) to repay the kindness. He took a short **nap** (小睡) each afternoon, then returned to the bench. The watchmaker noticed that the refugee’s hands were **identical** (相同的) in skill to his own late son’s. He gave him a bottle of **beer** (啤酒) and said, “For the **sake** (缘故、目的) of peace, you must heal your **wound** (受伤的) soul.” Years later, the refugee became a **classical** (古典的) music teacher, and the watchmaker’s watch still ticked—a symbol of their shared bond.

**中文翻译**

在一个小镇上，住着一位老钟表匠，他那块**宝贵的**金表是他**一生**的珍宝。一个暴风雨的夜晚，**其间**传来敲门声。一个**难民**站在门口，浑身湿透、瑟瑟发抖，穿着破旧的**衬衫**和单薄的**夹克**。钟表匠为他提供了**住处**过夜。难民感激地以温和的**魅力**讲述了他逃离战争的故事。他的话与其他旅行者的话**一致**——每个故事都**不同**，却共享同样的痛苦。钟表匠想**指导**这个年轻人，便教他如何**编辑**旧钟齿轮。难民努力工作，甚至帮忙做**家务**来报答恩情。他每天下午小睡片刻，然后回到工作台前。钟表匠注意到，难民的手艺与他已故儿子**相同**。他给了他一瓶**啤酒**，说：“为了和平的**缘故**，你必须治愈你**受伤的**灵魂。”多年后，这位难民成为了一名**古典**音乐教师，而钟表匠的金表仍在滴答作响——那是他们共同情谊的象征。
