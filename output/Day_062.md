Once upon a time, a young engineer named **Alex** was obsessed with **productivity** (生产力). He believed that **discipline** (纪律) was the key to success, so he woke up at dawn every day, placed his laptop on a comfortable **seat** (座位), and worked without distraction. One day, he had a **revolution** (革命、旋转) of thought: instead of chasing the **prize** (奖) of money, he should focus on his **primary** (初级的、最初的) passion, which was building sustainable energy systems. Compared to his **previous** (先前的) job, this felt far more **attractive** (吸引人的、动人的).

To **communicate** (传达、交流) his ideas, Alex joined a startup. But soon, a **disaster** (灾难) struck—the company’s **stock** (股票、库存) plummeted. Alex remained **quick** (迅速的) to adapt. He decided to **fly** (飞) to a remote island where a research lab was testing solar **heat** (热) storage. There, he met **Maria**, a brilliant **candidate** (候选人) for a senior position. She questioned his **identity** (身份) as an engineer, but Alex proved himself by helping **repair** (修理、补救) a broken turbine. They worked for **eight** (八) hours straight, until sweat and **tear** (眼泪、撕碎) mixed on their faces—the machine finally hummed. Maria smiled and showed him a **web** (网) of interconnected sensors she had designed. That night, Alex realized that true innovation is never solitary; it’s a **web** of shared dreams.

**中文翻译**

从前，一位年轻的工程师亚历克斯痴迷于生产力（productivity）。他相信纪律（discipline）是成功的关键，所以每天黎明即起，把笔记本电脑放在舒适的座位（seat）上，心无旁骛地工作。有一天，他的思想发生了一场革命（revolution）：与其追逐金钱之奖（prize），不如专注于自己初级的（primary）热情——建造可持续能源系统。与他先前的（previous）工作相比，这显然更吸引人（attractive）。

为了传达（communicate）自己的想法，亚历克斯加入了一家初创公司。但很快，一场灾难（disaster）降临——公司的股票（stock）暴跌。亚历克斯反应迅速（quick），决定飞（fly）往一座偏远岛屿，那里有实验室在测试太阳能热（heat）储存。在那里，他遇到了玛丽亚——一位出色的高级职位候选人（candidate）。她质疑他作为工程师的身份（identity），但亚历克斯通过帮助修理（repair）一台损坏的涡轮机证明了自己。他们连续工作了八（eight）个小时，汗水与眼泪（tear）混在一起——机器终于轰鸣运转。玛丽亚微笑着向他展示了一张由互联传感器组成的网（web）。那晚，亚历克斯明白：真正的创新从不孤独，它是一张由共同梦想编织的网。
