## 二、连锁故事（英文）

Walking through the **southeastern** (东南) district of the old city, I paused to consider my **standpoint** (立场) on urban renewal. A loose **string** (线、弦、一串) from my coat flapped in the wind, and I decided to take a leisurely **stroll** (漫步) along the riverbank. I nearly **stumble** (绊倒、蹒跚) over a cracked pavement stone, which reminded me of my own fragile **temperament** (气质) — sensitive yet stubborn.

Nearby, an old radio repair shop displayed a vintage **transistor** (晶体管) radio. The owner, a poet at heart, recited a **verse** (诗) about the **void** (空白、无效的、空虚) left by forgotten technology. He warned me that clinging to nostalgia could be **adverse** (不利的) to progress, and that we must **amplify** (扩大) the new while honoring the old.

I bought a **bacon** (培根、熏肉) sandwich from a street vendor and watched a **butterfly** (蝴蝶) flutter onto a pile of **cargo** (货物) waiting for shipment. The morning **chill** (寒冷、寒冷的) had faded, and this scene felt utterly **commonplace** (平凡的、老生常谈) — yet strangely beautiful. I noticed a **compact** (紧凑的) delivery truck parked nearby, its driver arguing for a **concession** (让步) from the dock manager. Their raised voices began to **confuse** (混淆) the quiet air, and I saw a uniformed **convict** (罪犯) on a work crew pause to listen, his eyes fixed on the distant horizon.

In that moment, I realized that every **string** of fate — from the **transistor** to the **butterfly** — is woven into a single, unpredictable tapestry. My **standpoint** shifted: perhaps the **void** we fear is not emptiness, but the **adverse** shadow of our own **temperament**. I finished my sandwich, **amplified** my pace, and walked on — no longer stumbling, but striding toward the **southeast** sky, where the sun broke through the **compact** clouds like a **verse** of light.

## 三、中文翻译

我漫步穿过老城的**东南**区，停下来思考自己对城市改造的**立场**。外套上的一根**线**在风中飘动，我决定沿着河岸悠闲地**漫步**。我差点被一块破裂的路面石**绊倒**，这让我想起自己敏感而固执的**气质**。

附近，一家老旧的收音机修理店展示着一台老式**晶体管**收音机。店主骨子里是位诗人，吟诵了一首关于被遗忘科技所留下**空白**的**诗**。他告诫我，固守怀旧可能对进步**不利**，我们必须在尊重旧事物的同时**扩大**新事物。

我从街边小贩那里买了一个**培根**三明治，看着一只**蝴蝶**飞落到一堆待运的**货物**上。清晨的**寒冷**已经消退，这一幕显得极为**平凡**，却莫名美丽。我注意到一辆**紧凑**的送货卡车停在附近，司机正与码头经理争论，要求对方做出**让步**。他们提高的声音开始**混淆**宁静的空气，我看到一名穿制服的**罪犯**在劳改队中停下来倾听，目光投向遥远的地平线。

那一刻，我意识到每一根命运之**线**——从**晶体管**到**蝴蝶**——都编织成一张不可预测的画卷。我的**立场**转变了：也许我们恐惧的**空白**并非空无，而是我们自身**气质**投下的**不利**阴影。我吃完三明治，**加快**了脚步，不再蹒跚，而是大步迈向**东南**方的天空，那里阳光穿透**紧凑**的云层，如同一首光之**诗**。
