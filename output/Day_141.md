**A Night in the Laboratory**

A **gentleman** (绅士) named Professor Aldous, known for his **gentle** (温和的) manners, was studying the **invisible** (看不见的) forces of nature in his private laboratory. He had just discovered that a strange **chemical** (化学的) reaction could make objects **invisible** (看不见的) for a few seconds. But tonight, he was forced to **retreat** (撤退) from his main experiment because of a sudden power failure.

His assistant, a young **chemist** (化学家) named Lena, rushed in with a flashlight. She knew how to **manipulate** (操纵) the backup generator, but the machine was too old. "We must **migrate** (迁移) to the basement lab," she said. As they descended, they saw **somebody** (某人) — a shadowy figure — moving near the **clinic** (诊所) next door. It was **dull** (枯燥的) work, chasing shadows, but Professor Aldous felt a **tight** (紧的) knot in his stomach.

Suddenly, a **wonderful** (精彩的) idea struck him. He could **accommodate** (适应、容纳) the new power source by re-wiring the old circuits. But the storm outside was **destructive** (破坏性的), and lightning (闪电) struck a transformer. They had to **chase** (追逐) the sparks before they reached a **steep** (陡峭的) ladder leading to the roof. "Every **deed** (行为、事迹) counts now!" Lena shouted.

Using a **refined** (精炼的) technique, Aldous quickly **tightened** (收紧) the loose cables. The lights flickered back on. For **recreation** (娱乐), they usually played chess, but tonight they celebrated their survival. Aldous placed a **stamp** (邮票) on his notebook, marking the date of their narrow escape. "Science is not a game," he smiled, "but sometimes it feels like **wonderland** (仙境)."

**中文翻译：**

一位名叫奥尔德斯教授的绅士，以温和的举止著称，正在他的私人实验室里研究自然界看不见的力量。他刚发现一种奇妙的化学反应能让物体几秒钟内隐形。但今晚，由于突然停电，他不得不从主要实验中撤退。

他的助手，一位年轻的化学家莉娜，拿着手电筒冲了进来。她知道如何操纵备用发电机，但那台机器太旧了。“我们得迁移到地下室实验室，”她说。当他们下楼时，看到某人——一个模糊的身影——在隔壁诊所附近移动。追影子是枯燥的工作，但奥尔德斯教授感到胃里打了个紧紧的结。

突然，一个精彩的念头闪过脑海。他可以通过重新布线来适应新电源。但外面的暴风雨具有破坏性，闪电击中了变压器。他们不得不追逐火花，赶在火花到达通往屋顶的陡峭梯子之前。“现在每一个行为都很重要！”莉娜喊道。

奥尔德斯用一项精炼的技术迅速收紧松动的电缆。灯重新亮了起来。平时他们以娱乐活动下棋，但今晚他们庆祝自己的死里逃生。奥尔德斯在笔记本上贴了一张邮票，标记这惊险逃离的日子。“科学不是游戏，”他笑着说，“但有时它就像仙境。”
