**英文连锁故事**

Paul stepped **onto** (到上面) the old wooden porch, feeling **proud** (骄傲) of his small **neighborhood** (小区) project. He wanted to **relieve** (缓解) the tension among residents who complained about the abandoned **tobacco** (烟草) shop. With volunteers, he planned to **construct** (建造) a community garden. That morning, he happened to **encounter** (偶遇) an old friend, a retired **truck** (卡车) driver, who offered to help. The task didn’t **overwhelm** (征服) them; a local company agreed to **sponsor** (赞助) the tools. They began to **equip** (装备) the site with benches and planters. The **initial** (起初的) idea was simple, and soon the garden became the **favorite** (最爱的) spot for kids. In an **instant** (立刻), the empty lot transformed into a green oasis. They kept the cost to a **minimum** (最低的). Every **neighbor** (邻居) contributed, and the **neighborhood** (社区) grew closer. The **principal** (最重要的) goal was unity, and their success was clear **proof** (证据). Later, Paul filed a **file** (文件) of photos and notes, promising to **investigate** (调查) other vacant spaces too.

**中文翻译**

保罗踏上了旧木门廊，为自己在小区里的小项目感到骄傲。他想缓解居民们对那家废弃烟草店的不满情绪。和志愿者们一起，他计划建造一个社区花园。那天早上，他偶遇了一位老友——一位退休卡车司机，对方主动提出帮忙。这项任务并没有压垮他们；一家本地公司同意赞助工具。他们开始用长椅和花盆装备场地。起初的想法很简单，很快花园就成了孩子们最爱的去处。一眨眼的功夫，空地被改造成了绿色绿洲。他们把成本控制在最低限度。每位邻居都出了力，社区也因此更加紧密。最重要的目标是团结，他们的成功就是明确的证据。后来，保罗整理了一份文件和照片，并承诺还要调查其他空置的空间。
