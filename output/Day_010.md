## 二、连锁故事（英文）

**A Quiet Evening at Home**

There were **few** (很少) people in the small town, and tonight was no exception. I returned **home** (家) after a long day, and **while** (当…时) I was making tea, I noticed something **different** (不同的) about the living room — a small **point** (点) of light flickering on the shelf. It was the **last** (最后的) page of an old **text** (文本) I had never read. Curiously, its words seemed to **correspond** (相符合) perfectly with my own thoughts about **education** (教育). I **placed** (放置) the book on the table, wondering if I should **pay** (支付) more attention to the **language** (语言) of forgotten writers. The room was **still** (仍然), and I decided to **keep** (保持) the book, unsure of **where** (哪里) it had come from. I later tried to **book** (预定) a visit to the local library, but the **government** (政府) had closed it for renovation. My old **college** (大学) professor once said that every book is a **second** (第二的) chance to **listen** (听) to history. So I sat down, turned on the lamp, and began to read — the light grew brighter, and the words came alive.

## 三、中文翻译

**家中的静谧夜晚**

这个小镇上很少有人（few），今晚也不例外。经过漫长的一天，我回到了家（home），当（while）我泡茶时，我注意到客厅有些不同（different）——书架上有一个小小的光点（point）。那是本旧文本（text）的最后一页（last），我从未读过。奇怪的是，书中的文字似乎与我对教育（education）的想法完全相符（correspond）。我把书放在（place）桌上，想着我是否应该多关注（pay）那些被遗忘作家的语言（language）。房间依然（still）安静，我决定保留（keep）这本书，却不知道它来自哪里（where）。后来我试图预订（book）去当地图书馆的行程，但政府（government）已将其关闭进行翻修。我大学（college）时的老教授曾说，每一本书都是倾听（listen）历史的第二次（second）机会。于是我坐下，打开台灯，开始阅读——灯光越来越亮，文字仿佛活了过来。
