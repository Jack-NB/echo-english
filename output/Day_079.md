**连锁故事 (Chain Story)**

My **assistant** (助手) walked into the office with a cup of **coffee** (咖啡), looking exhausted. She had just returned from an **overseas** (外国的、在海外) business trip, and her flight had a rough landing that nearly caused a **crash** (碰撞). She told me about the beautiful **river** (河流) near the hotel, where she spent her **weekend** (周末) before coming back. The local **administration** (管理、政府) had been criticized for **excessive** (过多的) bureaucracy, which made her trip frustrating. She had to follow a narrow **path** (小道) through the forest to find a taxi, but the trip was surprisingly **productive** (盛产的、富有成效的) because she signed a contract worth an **enormous** (巨大的) sum.

She felt a strong **urge** (催促、敦促) to share her ideas immediately, so she asked me to **concentrate** (集中) on her presentation. Her first **impression** (印象) of the foreign partners was excellent, and their company is now **worldwide** (全世界的) famous. However, their management style is quite **liberal** (不严格的、自由的), which sometimes causes confusion. She suggested that we need an **immediate** (立即的) response to their proposal, or they might **retire** (退休) from the deal. After the meeting, she collapsed onto the **bed** (床) in my office, saying she needed a week of sleep.

**中文翻译**

我的**助手**端着杯**咖啡**走进办公室，看上去疲惫不堪。她刚结束一趟**国外**出差，航班降落时差点发生**碰撞**事故。她跟我讲了酒店旁那条美丽的**河流**，她在出差前的**周末**曾在那里散步。当地**政府**因**过多**的官僚程序而备受批评，这让她的行程很受挫。她不得不穿过森林里一条狭窄的**小道**去打车，但这次出差却**富有成效**，因为她签下了一笔数额**巨大**的合同。

她感到一阵强烈的**冲动**，想立刻分享她的想法，于是让我**集中**精力看她的演示。她对国外合作方的第一**印象**非常好，那家公司如今**全球**知名。不过，他们的管理风格相当**宽松自由**，有时会造成混乱。她建议我们必须对对方的提案做出**立即**回应，否则他们可能会从交易中**退出**。会议结束后，她瘫倒在我办公室的**床**上，说自己需要睡上一周。
