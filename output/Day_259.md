**连锁故事 (英文)**

The old **nylon** (尼龙) backpack was heavy on Tom's shoulders as he set out on the weekend **outing** (郊游). He had to climb over a fallen tree that formed a natural **obstruction** (障碍) on the path. The morning fog made everything **opaque** (不透明的), hiding the distant hills where an **ox** (牛) was grazing. Tom felt a bit **pathetic** (可怜的) when he slipped on a wet **pebble** (鹅卵石), but he laughed it off. He paused to **peel** (果皮) an orange, its **perfume** (香水) mingling with the fresh air. Suddenly, he remembered his grandmother's words about **pneumonia** (肺炎) — never stay out too long in the damp cold. He tried to **recollect** (回忆) her advice, but his brother's **retort** (反驳) from last night echoed in his mind: “You worry too much!” Tom smiled and pulled out a small **saw** (锯) to clear a branch, acting like a **scout** (侦察) on a mission. Nearby, he saw a shepherd's hut; the **shepherd** (牧羊) was mending a fence, using a needle and thread to **sew** (缝) a torn leather strap. A distant **siren** (警报器、汽笛) wailed from the village, reminding Tom of emergency drills. He thought of the **royalty** (版税) his uncle earned from writing music — a strange word, like the **patriotic** (爱国的) songs he wrote. Tom washed his face with water, pretending it was **shampoo** (洗头), and then headed home, his heart filled with the **obstruction**-free joy of a simple day.
