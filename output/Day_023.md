**英文连锁故事**

A **strong** (强壮的) farmer named Tom decided to **fill** (填) his empty barn with hay before winter. He was a **patient** (有耐心的) man, never breaking his daily **rule** (规则) of working from dawn **until** (直到) dusk. He would **continue** (继续) his labor even when tired, and he always managed to **receive** (收到) help from neighbors. By November, **already** (已经) half the barn was full, and Tom kept a careful **record** (记录) of every bale stored.

One day, a new **employee** (雇工) named Jack came to help. Jack was eager to **sell** (卖) his old tractor, but Tom wrote a **note** (笔记) reminding himself to check the engine first. For a whole **month** (月), Jack worked hard, and Tom praised him, saying, “You are a true part of our **community** (社区).” Jack, however, began to **demand** (要求) higher wages, which made Tom uneasy. Tom’s **personal** (个人的) philosophy was simple: work first, money later. **Instead** (反而) of arguing, Tom gave Jack a **challenge** (挑战): repair the old tractor by Friday. Jack accepted and worked tirelessly, discovering a hidden **store** (存储) of spare parts in the shed. Tom then did a final **check** (检查) of the barn and smiled—everything was perfect.

That winter, the barn stayed warm and full, and Jack learned that patience and hard work always pay off.

**中文翻译**

一位强壮的农夫汤姆决定在冬天到来之前，把空谷仓填满干草。他是个有耐心的人，从不打破自己从黎明工作到黄昏的规则。即使疲惫，他也会继续劳作，而且总能得到邻居们的帮助。到十一月，谷仓已经装满了一半，汤姆仔细记录着每一捆干草的存放。

一天，一位新雇工杰克来帮忙。杰克急于卖掉他的旧拖拉机，但汤姆写了一张笔记，提醒自己先检查发动机。整整一个月，杰克工作很努力，汤姆称赞他说：“你真是我们社区的一分子。”然而，杰克开始要求更高的工资，这让汤姆感到不安。汤姆的个人哲学很简单：先工作，后谈钱。汤姆没有争吵，反而给了杰克一个挑战：在周五前修好旧拖拉机。杰克接受了，并不知疲倦地干活，在棚屋里发现了一处隐藏的零件储存。最后，汤姆对谷仓做了全面检查，微笑着——一切完美。

那个冬天，谷仓温暖且满盈，杰克也明白了耐心和努力终有回报。
