**连锁故事**

The **cardinal** (基本的) rule of the village was to respect the **clergy** (神职人员), so when old Father Brown lost his **clutch** (抓住) on the rope and fell into the river, everyone rushed to help. The rescue team worked in a **cohesive** (团结的) manner, forming a human chain. The river's **configuration** (构造) was tricky—rocks and fallen trees hidden beneath the surface. Their laughter was **contagious** (传染性的), spreading from the children on the bank to the adults. A lone **crow** (乌鸦) perched on a branch, cawing loudly, as if directing the operation. The morning air was **damp** (潮湿), and the moss on the stones made footing treacherous. Some villagers began to **denounce** (谴责) the bridge keeper for negligence, while others **deplore** (谴责) the lack of repairs. This incident threatened to **depress** (降低) the village's morale. Meanwhile, a young boy found an old **disc** (圆盘) half-buried in the mud—a rusty metal plate that looked like a **discus** (铁饼). He tried to **distil** (蒸馏) the water from it, pretending it was a treasure. His imagination was like a **dragon** (龙), fierce and boundless, carrying him away from the grey **dusk** (黄昏) settling over the fields. The headmaster wanted to **emphasise** (着重) the importance of safety, so he planned to address the whole school. He stood at the **exterior** (外部) of the chapel, waiting for the crowd. Someone suggested they **fabricate** (制造) a new bridge from old planks. The village council offered a **fellowship** (奖学金) to any young engineer who could design it. As the moon rose, the river's surface turned to silver **foam** (泡沫), and the villagers finally smiled, their spirits lifted by the promise of a new beginning.

**中文翻译**

这个村庄的基本（cardinal）规则是尊重神职人员（clergy），所以当老布朗神父没能抓住（clutch）绳子掉进河里时，所有人都冲去帮忙。救援队以团结的（cohesive）方式协作，组成了一条人链。这条河的构造（configuration）很棘手——水面下藏着岩石和倒下的树木。他们的笑声具有传染性（contagious），从岸边的孩子传到了大人那里。一只孤独的乌鸦（crow）栖在枝头，大声啼叫，仿佛在指挥这场行动。清晨的空气潮湿（damp），石头上的苔藓让落脚点变得危险。一些村民开始谴责（denounce）守桥人的疏忽，另一些人则谴责（deplore）缺乏修缮。这件事差点降低了（depress）整个村庄的士气。与此同时，一个小男孩在泥里发现了一个半埋的圆盘（disc）——一块生锈的金属板，看起来像铁饼（discus）。他试图从中蒸馏（distil）水，假装那是件宝物。他的想象力像龙（dragon）一样凶猛无边，把他从笼罩田野的灰色黄昏（dusk）中带走了。校长想着重（emphasise）强调安全的重要性，于是计划向全校讲话。他站在教堂的外部（exterior），等待人群。有人建议用旧木板制造（fabricate）一座新桥。村委会为任何能设计它的年轻工程师提供了一份奖学金（fellowship）。月亮升起时，河面变成了银色泡沫（foam），村民们终于笑了，精神因新开始的希望而振奋。
