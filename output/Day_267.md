**英文连锁故事**

In the old laboratory, a strange **vibrating** (颤动) machine sat beside a **volatile** (不稳定的) chemical flask. The scientist checked the **volt** (伏特) reading, but the display flickered like a **waterfall** (瀑布) of electrons. She applied some **wax** (蜡) to a gear to stop the **whirl** (旋转) from shaking the table. On the wall hung a portrait of a **witch** (女巫) knitting **wool** (羊毛), a joke from her students. Suddenly, a **wrench** (猛扭) of pain hit her back; she sighed, rubbing the **wrinkle** (皱纹) on her forehead.

**According to** (根据) the manual, she should have warmed the machine first. Years of work had **accustomed** (习惯) her to such mistakes, but an **adverb** (副词) in the note — "slowly" — made her laugh. The **air conditioning** (空调) hummed, **albeit** (虽然) weakly, and the **analog** (模拟的) dials wobbled. She wanted to **apologise** (道歉) to her assistant for the earlier outburst, but it was already **April** (四月), and the **archeology** (考古学) dig next door had uncovered a strange **artefact** (人工制品) — a bronze coil that looked exactly like her machine’s core. She decided to investigate, forgetting the pain.

**中文翻译**

在旧实验室里，一台奇怪的颤动机器旁边放着一瓶不稳定的化学药剂。科学家检查了伏特读数，但显示屏像瀑布一样闪烁不定。她给一个齿轮涂了些蜡，以阻止旋转震得桌子晃动。墙上挂着一幅女巫织羊毛的画像，那是学生们开的玩笑。突然，一阵猛扭般的疼痛袭上她的背部；她叹了口气，揉了揉额头上的皱纹。

根据手册，她本该先预热机器。多年的工作已经使她习惯了这类错误，但注释中的一个副词——“缓慢地”——让她哑然失笑。空调嗡嗡作响，虽然微弱，模拟表盘也随之晃动。她想为之前对助理发火而道歉，但已经是四月了，隔壁的考古发掘出土了一件奇怪的人工制品——一个铜线圈，和她机器的核心一模一样。她决定去调查，暂时忘了疼痛。
