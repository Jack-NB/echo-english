**英文故事**

One morning, a **swarming** (蜂拥的) group of scientists gathered for a **symposium** (讨论会) in a basement lab. They checked the **thermometer** (温度计) and decided to **withhold** (保留) their data until the experiment was perfect. Suddenly, an **arrow** (箭) shot through the window, carrying a note. It landed on a pile of **baggage** (行李), causing a **blunt** (钝的) thud and leaving a **bruise** (擦伤) on the wooden floor. A loud **buzz** (嗡嗡声) came from the **cab** (驾驶室) of a truck outside; the driver had spilled **calcium** (钙) powder. The scientists used a **compass** (指南针) to navigate the dusty hall, then rushed **downstairs** (楼下) to find the source. They discovered a vat of **dye** (染料) leaking into a **fuse** (保险丝) box. As they tried to fix it, a masked figure took a **hostage** (人质) — the lab assistant. The team leader began to **jog** (慢跑) after the intruder, holding a **knife** (刀) from the kitchen. He slipped on a **loaf** (块) of soap and crashed into a shelf. At that moment, the **lunar** (月亮的) light through the window revealed the intruder was just a robot. Everyone laughed, and the **symposiast** (研讨会参加者) resumed their calm, while the **fused** (融合的) wires sparked harmlessly. The **hostager** (人质扣押者) robot was reprogrammed, and the day ended with a **buzzing** (嗡嗡响的) celebration.

**中文翻译**

一天早上，一群**蜂拥的**科学家聚集在地下实验室参加一场**讨论会**。他们检查了**温度计**，决定**保留**数据直到实验完美。突然，一支**箭**射穿窗户，带着一张纸条，落在一堆**行李**上，发出**钝的**闷响，并在木地板上留下**擦伤**。外面卡车**驾驶室**传来响亮的**嗡嗡声**；司机洒了**钙**粉。科学家们用**指南针**在尘土飞扬的大厅中导航，然后冲**楼下**寻找源头。他们发现一缸**染料**漏进**保险丝**盒。正当他们试图修理时，一个蒙面身影劫持了**人质**——实验室助理。团队负责人开始**慢跑**追入侵者，手里拿着厨房的**刀**。他踩到一块肥皂**块**滑倒，撞上架子。那一刻，透过窗户的**月亮的**光揭示入侵者只是个机器人。大家笑了，**研讨会参加者**恢复平静，而**融合的**电线安全地闪着火花。**人质扣押者**机器人被重新编程，一天在**嗡嗡响的**庆祝中结束。
