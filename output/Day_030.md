**The Chain Story**

The **importance** (重要性) of a single decision can never be underestimated. When Dr. Ellen refused to **decline** (拒绝) a risky research grant, she knew her **claim** (声称) about a new cancer treatment would be tested. She built a **model** (模型) of the human immune system, but early results were **wrong** (错误的) — the data kept failing. As an **expert** (专家) in immunology, she decided to **carry** (携带) her experiments forward with a bold **act** (行动): she would **send** (发送) her findings to a global lab. To her surprise, they agreed to **accept** (接受) her proposal. The **potential** (潜在的) breakthrough was enormous, but the **degree** (程度) of risk was high. She needed more **resource** (资源), so she requested a short **pause** (暂停) in funding negotiations. Her **opinion** (意见) was clear: without new equipment, the project would fail. Just then, a private donor offered a generous **income** (收入) stream — a significant **amount** (amount) — to continue the work. Months later, the treatment showed promise against a rare **disease** (疾病). Ellen could finally **stand** (站立) before the scientific community, not as a dreamer, but as a pioneer. The **political** (政治的) pressure was intense, but her **important** (重要的) discovery spoke louder than any critic. She knew that **acceptance** (接受) from peers meant more than fame — it meant lives saved.

**中文翻译**

一次决策的重要性永远不能被低估。当艾伦博士拒绝接受一项高风险的研究拨款时，她知道自己关于一种新型癌症疗法的声称将会受到检验。她建立了一个人体免疫系统的模型，但早期结果全是错误的——数据不断失败。作为免疫学专家，她决定以大胆的行动推进实验：将她的发现发送给一家全球实验室。令她惊讶的是，对方同意接受她的提案。潜在的突破巨大，但风险程度也很高。她需要更多资源，于是请求在资金谈判中短暂暂停。她的意见很明确：没有新设备，项目就会失败。就在此时，一位私人捐赠者提供了可观的收入来源——一笔可观的金额——以继续这项工作。几个月后，该疗法对一种罕见疾病显示出希望。艾伦终于能够站在科学界面前，不再是一个梦想家，而是一位先驱。政治压力巨大，但她重要的发现比任何批评者都更有说服力。她知道，来自同行的接受比名声更重要——它意味着生命被拯救。
