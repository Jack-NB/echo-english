**连锁故事**

At dawn, the old port was quiet. The **turnover** (营业额) of the harbor shop had dropped, so the owner, a loyal **ally** (伙伴、同盟者) of the ship captain, decided to join a **cruise** (巡航) to inspect the coast. The captain’s **deputy** (副手) wrote in his **diary** (日记): “The wind is **faint** (微弱的), but the cargo weighs a **gram** (克) more than usual.” He felt **idle** (无目的) until a **lorry** (货车) arrived, carrying bolts and **nuts** (螺母、坚果). They loaded them into the ship’s **oven** (烤箱)-like engine room, then prepared to stay **overnight** (过夜) on board.

From the deck, they saw a **skyscraper** (摩天大楼) on the horizon—the city’s **summit** (顶点) of trade. Through the **lens** (镜头) of his binoculars, the deputy watched a **porter** (搬运工) **practise** (练习) lifting crates. Suddenly, the sea began to **swell** (膨胀、涌), and the captain drew his **sword** (剑) to cut a loose rope. The dock, however, remained **vacant** (空的), waiting for the next ship.

**中文翻译**

黎明时分，老港口一片寂静。港口小店的**营业额**（turnover）下降了，于是店主——船长的忠实**伙伴**（ally）——决定加入一次**巡航**（cruise）去检查海岸。船长的**副手**（deputy）在**日记**（diary）里写道：“风很**微弱**（faint），但货物比平时多了一**克**（gram）。”他觉得**无目的**（idle），直到一辆**货车**（lorry）开来，载着螺栓和**螺母、坚果**（nuts）。他们把货物装进船上像**烤箱**（oven）一样的引擎室，然后准备在船上**过夜**（overnight）。

从甲板上，他们看到地平线上有一座**摩天大楼**（skyscraper）——那是这座城市贸易的**顶点**（summit）。透过双筒**镜头**（lens），副手看见一个**搬运工**（porter）在**练习**（practise）举箱子。突然，海浪开始**膨胀、涌**（swell），船长拔出**剑**（sword）割断一根松动的绳子。而码头依然**空的**（vacant），等待着下一艘船的到来。
