**The Chain Story**

An **anonymous** (匿名的) figure slipped into the cheese (奶酪) factory, holding a coupon (息票) that promised a free sample. He was a descendant (后裔) of a long line of dairy farmers, but today he felt a downward (向下) spiral of embarrassment (embarrass) as he tripped over a drain (下水道). His bare (赤裸的) arm scraped against a rusty hook (钩), and he let out a horrible (horrible) cry. A worker, mad (生气的) at the noise, ordered him to discharge (释放) his load immediately. The man confessed (承认) he only wanted to see the scenery (scenery) from the rooftop, but now his utmost (极限) priority was to escape. He grabbed whichever (无论哪一个) door seemed open, but every exit was blocked by garbage (garbage) piles. His handicap (障碍) — a fear of heights — made the fire escape impossible. The probability (probability) of getting caught rose to ninety percent. Just then, a marathon (马拉松) runner burst through the back gate, and the man slipped out behind him. He ran until his lungs burned, barely (barely) breathing, but he was free. Later, he realized the whole chase had been a silly mistake — the coupon was expired, and the cheese was already spoiled.
