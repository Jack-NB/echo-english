**The Chain of Fate**

In a dim **cellar** (地窖) beneath an old castle, a group of conspirators gathered. Their leader, a man with a **coarse** (粗糙的) beard, held an **ax** (斧子) and whispered about their plan to **assassinate** (暗杀) the king. They decided to poison his drink with a **drip** (滴) of deadly venom, hoping the act would be quick and silent. But among them was a **darling** (亲爱的) young spy, whose heart was **cordial** (热忱的、诚恳的) to the crown. She secretly planned to **deduct** (扣除) the poison from the cup.

The next morning, the king played **badminton** (羽毛球) in the garden. A **blackboard** (黑板) nearby displayed the schedule for the day. The spy, disguised as a servant, brought a tray of **buffet** (自助餐) food and a cup of wine. As she approached, she noticed a **blade** (叶片) of grass stuck to the king’s shoe. She smiled, remembering the **brevity** (简洁) of life. Just then, a **comet** (彗星) streaked across the sky—a rare omen. The king looked up, and in that moment, the spy swapped the cups.

The conspirators, however, had a **batch** (一批) of backup plans. They released a **degenerate** (退化) beast from the **cemetery** (墓地) to cause chaos. The beast’s roar made the guards scatter. Meanwhile, the spy led the king through a secret passage. The two became **cosy** (相互勾结的、亲切友好的) allies, and the king declared her his **darling** (亲爱的) counselor. The plot was foiled, and the conspirators were caught. The king ordered a **deduct** (扣除) of their lands, and the spy was honored. That night, as **dew** (露水) fell, the king thought of the **discrete** (不连续的) events that had saved him—each moment separate, yet woven together by fate.

**中文翻译**

在一座古老城堡下昏暗的地窖里，一群密谋者聚集在一起。他们的首领，一个长着粗糙胡须的男人，握着一把斧子，低声商议着暗杀国王的计划。他们决定在国王的酒里滴入一滴致命毒液，希望行动迅速无声。但其中有一位亲爱的年轻间谍，她对王室心存热忱。她秘密计划从杯中扣除毒药。

第二天早晨，国王在花园里打羽毛球。附近的一块黑板上写着当天的日程。间谍假扮成仆人，端来一托盘自助餐和一杯酒。走近时，她注意到国王鞋上粘着一片草叶。她微微一笑，想起生命的简洁。就在这时，一颗彗星划过天空——这是个罕见的预兆。国王抬头看天，就在那一瞬间，间谍换掉了酒杯。

然而，密谋者们还有一批备用计划。他们从墓地放出一只退化的野兽制造混乱。野兽的咆哮使卫兵四散奔逃。与此同时，间谍带着国王穿过一条秘密通道。两人成了相互勾结、亲切友好的盟友，国王封她为亲爱的顾问。阴谋被挫败，密谋者全部被捕。国王下令扣除他们的领地，并嘉奖了间谍。那夜，露水降下，国王回想着拯救他的那些不连续的事件——每个瞬间彼此独立，却又被命运编织在一起。
