The old professor picked up a dusty **textbook** (课本) from the shelf. Its **title** (标题) was faded, but he knew every page by heart. He had always been a **creative** (有创造力的) teacher, and his students placed absolute **trust** (信任) in his wisdom. Yet today he felt **ill** (有病的、坏的) at heart—the university had decided to close his beloved department.

He opened a small **box** (盒子) containing old photographs. Seeing his younger self, he began to **cry** (哭泣) silently. Life had **hardly** (几乎不) been easy, but he had always felt **responsible** (应负责的) for his students. He tried to **track** (追踪) the faces in the photos, each one a memory. His colleagues had tried to **warn** (警告) him about the budget cuts, but he had ignored them. The **total** (总共的) number of students he had taught was over three thousand. From their success, he could **derive** (取得) great pride.

He glanced at a delicate piece of **china** (瓷器) on his desk—a gift from a grateful student. Beside it lay an **extra** (额外的) copy of his favorite textbook. He powered up his **electronic** (电子的) tablet, but the screen flickered. He chose to **ignore** (忽视) the technical issue. His spirit would **survive** (幸存) this setback. A **bird** (鸟) landed on the windowsill, singing. The mood felt less **heavy** (重的). He smiled, realizing that teaching was not confined to a classroom—it lived on in every mind he had touched.

老教授从书架上拿起一本落满灰尘的课本。它的标题已经褪色，但他对每一页都烂熟于心。他一直是一位有创造力的老师，学生们绝对信任他的智慧。然而今天他感到心里很难受——大学决定关闭他心爱的系。

他打开一个装有旧照片的小盒子。看到年轻时的自己，他无声地哭了起来。生活几乎不容易，但他始终觉得自己有责任照顾学生。他试图追踪照片中的每一张面孔，每一张都是一段回忆。同事们曾警告他预算削减的事，但他没有理会。他教过的学生总数超过三千人。从他们的成功中，他可以取得巨大的自豪感。

他瞥了一眼桌上的一件精致瓷器——是一位感恩的学生送的礼物。旁边放着一本额外的他最喜欢的课本。他打开电子平板，但屏幕闪烁不定。他选择忽视这个技术问题。他的精神会从这次挫折中幸存下来。一只鸟落在窗台上，唱着歌。气氛不再那么沉重。他笑了，意识到教学并不局限于教室——它活在他接触过的每一个心灵中。
