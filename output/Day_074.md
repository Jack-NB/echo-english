**英文连锁故事**

One night, a thief tried to **steal** (偷) a rare diamond from a high-tech museum. He moved with **stealth** (秘密行动), avoiding every camera. But his plan failed because he didn't realize that the museum’s security system was an **evolving** (进化的) artificial intelligence, a product of **evolution** (进化) itself. The AI alerted an international **corporation** (公司、法人) that owned the building. The thief’s **homework** (作业) was clearly incomplete — he had missed the **insight** (洞察力) that the diamond was just a hologram.

From the security room, the guard watched the whole scene with perfect **sight** (视野、景象). He was a former **athlete** (运动员) with **athletic** (健壮的) reflexes. The **outcome** (结果) was inevitable: the thief was trapped. The guard pressed a button, and the floor’s **surface** (表面) split **apart** (相隔、分开), revealing a pit. It was a **pleasant** (令人愉快的) surprise for the guard, who always took **pleasure** (愉快、乐事) in catching criminals. The thief’s **entry** (进入) into the vault was now blocked by laser beams.

Soon, **justice** (公平、审判) arrived. The police chief, a woman known for her **just** (公正的) decisions, turned on the **electricity** (电、电学) to power the prison door. She left a **notice** (注意、通知) on the thief’s cell: “Your **final** (最终的) chance to confess.” The thief **rarely** (罕见地、几乎不) spoke, but he knew his **privacy** (隐私) was gone. He sent an **email** (电子邮件) to his lawyer, but the message was intercepted. The whole **cycle** (周期) of crime and punishment was repeating — like a **satellite** (卫星) orbiting the earth, always returning to the same point. The thief sighed, realizing that this was the **final** (最终的) chapter of his career.

**中文翻译**

一天夜里，一个小偷试图从一家高科技博物馆**偷**（steal）一颗稀有钻石。他**秘密行动**（stealth），躲开了所有摄像头。但他的计划失败了，因为他没有意识到博物馆的安保系统是一个**进化的**（evolving）人工智能，是**进化**（evolution）本身的产物。人工智能通知了拥有这栋楼的国际**公司、法人**（corporation）。小偷的**作业**（homework）显然没做完——他错过了那个**洞察力**（insight）：钻石只是个全息影像。

在监控室里，保安以完美的**视野、景象**（sight）看到了整个过程。他曾是一名**运动员**（athlete），有着**健壮的**（athletic）反应能力。**结果**（outcome）是注定的：小偷被困住了。保安按下按钮，地板的**表面**（surface）裂开**相隔、分开**（apart），露出一个深坑。这对保安来说是个**令人愉快的**（pleasant）惊喜，因为他总是从抓罪犯中获得**愉快、乐事**（pleasure）。小偷的**进入**（entry）金库之路被激光束封锁了。

很快，**公平、审判**（justice）到来。警察局长是一位以**公正的**（just）决定而闻名的女性，她打开了**电、电学**（electricity）来给监狱门供电。她在小偷的牢房上留了一张**注意、通知**（notice）：“你**最终的**（final）坦白机会。”小偷**罕见地、几乎不**（rarely）说话，但他知道自己的**隐私**（privacy）已经没了。他给律师发了一封**电子邮件**（email），但信息被拦截了。犯罪与惩罚的整个**周期**（cycle）正在重演——就像一颗**卫星**（satellite）环绕地球，总是回到同一点。小偷叹了口气，意识到这是他职业生涯的**最终的**（final）篇章。
