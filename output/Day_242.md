**连锁故事 (English Version)**

In the quiet **meadow** (草地) beyond the village, an old **plumber** (管道工) named Tom was repairing a water pipe. He had just received a small **packet** (数据包) containing a new valve, but his mind was elsewhere—he was still troubled by his recent study of **psychiatry** (精神病学), trying to understand his stubborn neighbor. Tom set down his **racket** (球拍) and decided to **recite** (背诵) a few medical terms to improve his **retention** (保存、记忆力). He used **shorthand** (速记) to jot down notes, then took a gentle **sip** (抿一口) of tea.

His neighbor, a **stubborn** (固执的) old man, refused to let Tom fix the pipe, claiming he would do it himself. Tom sighed and loaded his tools onto a **trolley** (手推车), planning to **amend** (修正) the situation by waiting patiently. As he walked, he twisted his **ankle** (踝) on a rock, stumbling onto a **barren** (贫瘠的) patch of land shaped like a small **basin** (盆地). Above him, a **bat** (蝙蝠) flew out of a cave, startled by the noise.

Tom chuckled, thinking how strange the world was—each creature a **beast** (兽) in its own way. He noticed a **brass** (黄铜的) doorknob lying in the dirt, a clear **breach** (违反) of the village’s cleanliness rules. He picked it up, planning to use it as a handle for his **broom** (扫帚). As he swept the dust, he realized that even a broken day could be mended with patience and a bit of humor. That evening, he wrote a letter to his neighbor, **amending** the quarrel, and felt his mind finally at peace.

**中文翻译**

在村庄外那片安静的**草地**上，老**管道工**汤姆正在修理一根水管。他刚收到一个小**数据包**，里面装着一个新阀门，但他的心思却不在这里——他最近一直在研究**精神病学**，想弄明白他那固执的邻居到底在想什么。汤姆放下**球拍**，决定**背诵**几个医学术语来增强**记忆力**。他用**速记**记下笔记，然后**抿**了一口茶。

他的邻居，一个**固执**的老人，拒绝让汤姆修水管，声称要自己动手。汤姆叹了口气，把工具装到**手推车**上，打算耐心等待，以**修正**这个局面。走着走着，他扭伤了**踝**关节，跌进了一块形状像小**盆地**的**贫瘠**土地。头顶上，一只**蝙蝠**被声响惊动，飞出了洞穴。

汤姆笑了笑，觉得这世界真奇妙——每个生灵都以自己的方式成为一只**兽**。他注意到泥土里有一个**黄铜**门把手，这明显**违反**了村里的清洁规定。他捡了起来，打算用它当**扫帚**的把手。清扫灰尘时，他意识到即使糟糕的一天也能用耐心和一点幽默来修补。那天晚上，他给邻居写了一封信，**修正**了这场争吵，心里终于平静了下来。
