**英文连锁故事** (约 420 词)

Maria’s primary **concern** (关心) was not her own **task** (任务) but the quality of her research. She had always been a **scientific** (科学的) thinker, applying **effort** (努力) to every detail. Her lab was housed in an old **plant** (工厂) building, now converted into a modern workspace. Her mentor often tried to **encourage** (鼓励) her, saying that a **career** (职业) in biology required patience. But recently, her results began to **fail** (衰退), threatening her progress. She knew that external **influence** (影响) from funding cuts could not be **avoid** (避免), yet she refused to give up. She listened to classical **music** (音乐) to calm her nerves, and every day she tried to **add** (增加) one new experiment to her routine. The university’s **policy** (政策) required her to document every **item** (物品) she used, which felt like a constant **force** (强迫) on her freedom. As a **member** (成员) of the research committee, she had to **explain** (解释) her methods clearly. Her **translation** (翻译) of complex data into simple language became her strength. On the **side** (一边), she taught junior students, and she began to **enjoy** (享受) this teaching role.

One afternoon, her mentor asked her to **multitask** (多任务处理) between writing a grant proposal and supervising the lab. The **taskmaster** (工头) in her mind pushed her hard, but she felt **effortless** (不费力的) when she focused. She noticed that her **concerning** (令人担忧的) results were actually due to a faulty **transplant** (移植) of cells. Her **encouraging** (令人鼓舞的) discovery revived her confidence. She wrote an **influential** (有影响力的) paper that caught the attention of a **policymaker** (政策制定者) who visited her lab. The **additional** (额外的) funding allowed her to **reinforce** (加强) her equipment. She learned that **failure** (失败) was not the end but a **sideline** (边缘) lesson. Her **explanation** (解释) of the error impressed everyone. She finally felt **unavoidable** (不可避免的) pressure, but she faced it with **forcefulness** (有力). Her **musical** (音乐的) hobby helped her stay balanced. She realized that every **membered** (有成员的) team needed **cooperation** (合作) to succeed. She decided to **itemize** (逐条列出) her future goals. Her **careerism** (事业心) softened into genuine **enjoyment** (享受) of discovery. She no longer feared **mistranslation** (误译) of her ideas. She stood on the **inside** (内部) of her own confidence, knowing that **unconcerned** (不关心的) critics would never stop her. She continued to **translate** (翻译) her passion into action. And at the end of the year, her **unfailing** (不衰的) dedication earned her the title of **scientist** (科学家) of the year.

**中文翻译**

玛丽亚最关心（concern）的不是自己的任务（task），而是研究的质量。她一直是一个科学（scientific）的思想者，对每个细节都付出努力（effort）。她的实验室设在一座旧工厂（plant）大楼里，现已改造成现代工作空间。她的导师经常鼓励（encourage）她，说生物学职业（career）需要耐心。但最近，她的结果开始衰退（fail），威胁到她的进展。她知道外部的影响（influence）——比如资金削减——是无法避免（avoid）的，但她拒绝放弃。她听古典音乐（music）来平静神经，每天尝试增加（add）一个新实验。大学的政策（policy）要求她记录使用的每一件物品（item），这感觉像是一种强迫（force）她自由的压力。作为委员会的成员（member），她必须解释（explain）自己的方法。她将复杂数据翻译（translation）成简单语言的能力成了她的强项。在一边（side），她教低年级学生，并开始享受（enjoy）教学角色。

一天下午，导师让她在多任务（multitask）之间切换——写基金申请书和监督实验室。她脑海中的监工（taskmaster）逼得很紧，但当她专注时，她感到毫不费力（effortless）。她注意到，令人担忧的（concerning）结果其实是由细胞移植（transplant）出错引起的。这个令人鼓舞的（encouraging）发现让她重拾信心。她写了一篇有影响力的（influential）论文，引起了一位来访的政策制定者（policymaker）的注意。额外的（additional）资金让她能加强（reinforce）设备。她明白了，失败（failure）不是终点，而是边缘（sideline）的一课。她的解释（explanation）让所有人印象深刻。她终于感受到不可避免的（unavoidable）压力，但她以有力（forcefulness）的态度面对。她的音乐（musical）爱好帮助她保持平衡。她意识到，每个有成员的（membered）团队都需要合作才能成功。她决定逐条列出（itemize）未来的目标。她的事业心（careerism）软化为对发现真正的享受（enjoyment）。她不再害怕自己的观点被误译（mistranslation）。她站在自己信心的内部（inside），知道那些不关心的（unconcerned）批评者永远不会阻止她。她继续将自己的热情翻译（translate）为行动。年底，她不懈的（unfailing）奉献为她赢得了年度科学家（scientist）的称号。
