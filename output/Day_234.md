**English Story**

In a small coastal town, Captain Reed always felt people **misunderstood** (误解) his stubbornness. He lived near an old **nucleus** (核) power plant, whose cooling tower looked like a giant **pinch** (捏、一撮) of salt. One stormy night, a **plea** (恳求) came over the radio: a cargo ship was sinking. Reed grabbed his **puppet** (木偶) — a lucky charm his daughter made — and rushed to the **republic** (共和国)'s emergency dock. He knew the **reservoir** (水库) upstream had overflowed, turning the bay into a muddy **swamp** (沼泽). His boat's engine had a loose **screw** (螺丝、螺钉), so he tightened it with a **stiff** (硬的) wrench. At the **stern** (船尾), he shouted orders to his crew. They used a **tug** (拖船、拖) to pull the sinking vessel. Reed's **analytic** (分析的) mind calculated the safest route, avoiding the **tray** (盘)-like sandbars. He told the sailors, "Don't **tease** (取笑) my old boat, but she's **apt** (恰当的) for this rescue." He even **ventilated** (通风) the flooded engine room to prevent fumes. His **vocation** (职业) as a rescuer gave him **zeal** (热情). The injured captain's wound turned out **benign** (良性的), and everyone thanked Reed for his courage. That night, Reed smiled — perhaps the town would finally stop calling him a **puppet** (木偶) of the old rules.

**中文翻译**

在一个沿海小镇，里德船长总觉得人们**误解**了他的固执。他住在老**核**电站附近，冷却塔远看像一**捏**盐。一个暴风雨夜，无线电传来**恳求**：一艘货船正在下沉。里德抓起女儿做的**木偶**幸运符，冲向**共和国**的紧急码头。他知道上游的**水库**已漫溢，海湾成了泥**沼泽**。他船上的引擎松了一颗**螺丝**，他用一把**硬**扳手拧紧。在**船尾**，他大声指挥船员，用一艘**拖船**拖住下沉的船。里德**分析**的头脑算出最安全航线，避开**盘**状沙洲。他对水手们说：“别**取笑**我的老船，但她**恰当**地适合这次救援。”他还为灌水的机舱**通风**防毒气。他的救援**职业**给了他**热情**。受伤船长的伤口最终是**良性**的，大家都感谢里德的勇敢。那晚，里德笑了——也许镇上终于不再叫他旧规条的**木偶**了。
