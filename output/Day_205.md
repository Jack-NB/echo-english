**连锁故事**

The **dwarfish** (侏儒般的) old man decided to **embark** (着手) on a journey that would change his life. He opened his **wardrobe** (衣柜), pulled out a clean **blouse** (女衬衫) — it was his late wife's, but he wore it for luck — and gave a polite **bow** (鞠躬) to his reflection in the mirror. He **embedded** (嵌入) a small key into his pocket, the key to the castle's **courtyard** (院子), **namely** (即) the place where his treasure was hidden.

He made no **fuss** (大惊小怪) about leaving; his neighbors would only have made a **terrible** (可怕的) **terror** (恐惧) of it. A soft light began to **permeate** (渗透) through the curtains, and he noticed the **resemblance** (相似) between the dawn sky and the scarf his wife used to wear. He planned to visit **sometime** (改天) the old market, but **tonight** (今晚) he had to hurry. He grabbed a **staple** (主要的) tool — a stapler — from the desk, and took a **suck** (吸) of water from his flask.

The **wreck** (残骸) of an old carriage lay in the courtyard, a **compatible** (兼容) reminder of a past he had nearly forgotten. He touched his **cheek** (脸颊) and smiled. The journey would be long, but the **stapler** (订书机) in his bag would hold his maps together. He was not **incompatible** (不兼容的) with adventure, after all.

**中文翻译**

那位矮小的老人决定着手一段改变人生的旅程。他打开衣柜，取出一件干净的女衬衫——那是他已故妻子的，但他穿着以求好运——然后对着镜子里的自己礼貌地鞠了一躬。他把一把小钥匙嵌入衣袋，那是城堡庭院大门的钥匙，即他藏宝之地。

他离开时没有大惊小怪；邻居们只会对此感到可怕的恐惧。柔和的光线开始渗透过窗帘，他注意到黎明的天空与妻子常戴的围巾之间的相似。他计划改天去逛旧市场，但今晚他必须赶路。他从桌上抓起一个主要工具——一台订书机——并从水壶里吸了一口水。

庭院里躺着一辆旧马车的残骸，这是一个兼容的提醒，提醒他几乎遗忘的过去。他摸了摸脸颊，笑了。旅程会很漫长，但他包里的订书机会把他的地图钉在一起。毕竟，他与冒险并非不兼容。
