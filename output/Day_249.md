**A Connected Story**

The ringmaster, a **benevolent** (仁慈的) man with a **puffy** (膨胀的) red coat, stood on the **balcony** (阳台) of the train car, shouting the schedule **anyhow** (无论如何) to the crowd below. Meanwhile, the acrobats were practicing on the **arch** (拱) of the big tent, their **ribcages** (胸腔) gleaming under the **tan** (晒黑) light of the setting sun. Their **reciprocal** (互惠的) trust was evident—each flip depended on the other’s catch. In **retrospect** (回顾), the troupe had **aggregated** (聚集) from all corners of the country, carrying **bulletin** (公报) boards and **centimetre** (厘米)-long needles for sewing costumes. But he had to **beware** (小心) the lion tamer’s whip, which could make him **blush** (脸红) with embarrassment. A young girl, **born** (出生的) into the circus family, practiced her **barbecue** (烧烤) skills for the evening feast, while her mother used a **puff** (粉扑) to powder her face. The smell of grilled meat filled the air, and the **rib** (肋骨) bones were tossed to the dogs. “**Anyhow** (无论如何), we are one big family,” the ringmaster smiled, knowing that their **aggregate** (集料) strength was more than just bricks and mortar—it was the **reciprocal** (相互的) love that kept the show alive. As the night fell, they lit the torches, and the **circus** (马戏团) became a glowing island of wonder.
