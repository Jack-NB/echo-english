**连锁故事**

In a dusty (布满灰尘的) old workshop, a **broker** (经纪人) named Leo was trying to **nail** (钉、钉子) a loose **lever** (杠杆) onto a broken machine. His **limb** (肢体) ached from hours of work, but he refused to **terminate** (终止) his task. Suddenly, a **crown** (王冠) fell from a shelf, landing on his head—it was a **comedy** (喜剧) of errors! He laughed, but then a **preliminary** (初步的) inspection revealed that the crown was made of gold. Leo **proclaimed** (表明、声明) that he would **pave** (铺路) his way to success by selling it. He decided to **await** (等候) the right buyer, and **incidentally** (偶然地、附带地), a wealthy client walked in. The client wanted to **filter** (过滤) out impurities from a **fluid** (流体) sample, and Leo offered to help. He used the crown as a **shield** (盾) to block a spray of liquid, and then he **swing** (摇摆、秋千) it around, accidentally knocking over a display of glassware. The noise attracted a **parade** (游行) of curious onlookers outside. Leo’s quick thinking and **likelihood** (可能性) of a big deal increased when the client, impressed by his resourcefulness, signed a contract. That night, Leo’s **brokerage** (经纪业务) flourished, and he proudly **condemned** (谴责) his old lazy habits, vowing to never let **dust** (灰尘) settle on his ambition again.

**中文翻译**

在一间布满灰尘（dust）的旧工坊里，一位名叫利奥的经纪人（broker）正试图把一根松动的杠杆（lever）钉（nail）到一台坏机器上。他的肢体（limb）因长时间劳作而酸痛，但他拒绝终止（terminate）任务。突然，一顶王冠（crown）从架子上掉下来，正好落在他头上——这简直是喜剧（comedy）般的错误！他笑了，但初步（preliminary）检查发现这顶王冠是金子做的。利奥表明（proclaim）自己要通过卖掉它来铺路（pave）走向成功。他决定等候（await）合适的买家，而偶然地（incidentally），一位富有的客户走了进来。客户想过滤（filter）掉一种流体（fluid）样本中的杂质，利奥主动帮忙。他用王冠作为盾（shield）挡住飞溅的液体，然后摇摆（swing）着它，不小心撞翻了一架子玻璃器皿。响声引来外面一队好奇的游行（parade）人群。利奥的机敏和做成大生意的可能性（likelihood）大增——那位客户对他的足智多谋印象深刻，签下了合同。那天晚上，利奥的经纪业务（brokerage）蒸蒸日上，他自豪地谴责（condemn）自己过去懒惰的习惯，发誓再也不让灰尘（dust）落在自己的雄心之上。
