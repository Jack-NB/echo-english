**The Lady of the Rolling Forum**

A **lady** (女士) named Emma stood by the **rolling** (滚动、卷) conveyor belt in her small shop, watching a **forum** (论坛) notification blink on her phone. She had spent weeks in **preparation** (准备) for the city’s annual craft fair, and by sheer **luck** (运气、好运), she had won a **scholarship** (奖学金、学问) to attend a design course. The course was held at an old **rail** (栏杆、铁轨) station converted into a creative hub, where the **dean** (院长、主任) was a strict but kind woman.

From the station’s high balcony, one could **overlook** (俯瞰、忽略) the entire hall—but Emma never **overlooked** (忽略) a single detail, for she had learned to **defend** (保卫、防守) her ideas against harsh critics. When the class got too noisy, the dean would **dismiss** (解散) everyone early, only to **reinforce** (加强) the rule of silence the next day. Emma found this habit a bit of a **bother** (打扰), but she managed to **overcome** (克服) her irritation by focusing on her sketches.

The local **academy** (学院) had just opened a **supermarket** (超级市场) of art supplies, and Emma, being **ambitious** (有雄心的、艰巨的), decided to stock up. She climbed onto the **roof** (屋顶) of the store to take photos for her portfolio—a strange act that made a **thief** (小偷) below drop his stolen goods in surprise. The police praised her, and she began to **accumulate** (积累) both fame and friends.

**中文翻译**

一位名叫艾玛的女士站在自家小店滚动的传送带旁，看着手机上论坛的通知闪烁。她为城市年度手工艺博览会准备了数周，凭着纯粹的好运，她赢得了一笔奖学金，得以参加一门设计课程。课程设在一个由旧铁轨火车站改造的创意中心，院长是一位严厉但善良的女性。

从车站的高阳台上，可以俯瞰整个大厅——但艾玛从未忽略任何一个细节，因为她学会了保卫自己的想法，对抗苛刻的批评者。当课堂太吵时，院长会提前解散所有人，第二天却又加强安静规则。艾玛觉得这个习惯有点打扰人，但她通过专注于素描，成功克服了烦躁。

当地学院刚开了一家艺术用品超级市场，艾玛很有雄心，决定去囤货。她爬上商店的屋顶为作品集拍照——这一奇怪举动让楼下一个小偷吓得丢掉了赃物。警察称赞了她，她也因此开始积累名声和朋友。
