**连锁故事（英文）**

In a dusty garage, a **mechanic** (技工) named Tom was **enthusiastic** (热情的) about an old car engine. He believed it could **flourish** (繁荣) again if he fixed every **inch** (英寸) of its worn parts. His **lung** (肺) burned from the fumes, but he carefully built a new **frame** (框架) for the chassis. He sketched the car’s **profile** (轮廓、简介) on a notepad, imagining a **youngster** (少年) driving it someday. He laid a **brick** (砖) under the jack to stabilize it, then began to **dictate** (规定、口述) a repair checklist to his assistant. Each **organ** (器官) of the machine—the pistons, valves, and carburetor—needed attention. He tuned the **piano** (钢琴)-like valve springs, giving them a gentle **twist** (扭曲、捻) to align perfectly. **Wherever** (无论在哪里) he worked, his eyes would **gaze** (凝视) at the engine with fierce focus. His **junior** (年资较浅的) apprentice watched, learning every trick. After hours, they took a break and rode the **subway** (地铁) downtown to admire the **pyramid** (金字塔)-shaped glass building. Tom said, “The only **remedy** (补救、治疗) for a broken machine is patience.” The apprentice nodded, feeling more **mature** (成熟的) already.
